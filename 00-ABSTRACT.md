# Abstract

**Sprint 1 status note.** The current live formulation treats the formal
terminus as a bounded structural argument, not a proven theorem unless and until
it is formalized. The convergence layer is `THE-STRAIGHT-LINE.md`; the remaining
threshold is the human gate.

**Thesis.** Nothing possesses its own existence. To exist is to be conferred —
spoken into being (*kun*), animated by an inscribed truth (*emet*), held in a
received act of being (*esse ab alio*), and re-created at each instant
(occasionalism). The strong reading of ontological nihilism is therefore not
"nothing exists" (which is self-refuting) but **no aseity**: nothing exists
intrinsically, independently, or on its own footing. On this reading the great
creation-theologies are not evidence *against* nihilism but evidence *for* it —
the highest creative act produces dependence, not self-standing being.

If existence is conferred rather than possessed, then the act of conferral —
**the *for***, the purpose, the will that directs — is authored and cannot be
read off the thing conferred. Capability is direction-neutral; the *for* lives
in the tending, never in the seed. Abstraction births every primitive precisely
because it commits to none. Selection is never criterion-free, and the criterion
is the *for*. Standing does not track origin; whether a thing was fabricated or
cultivated tells us its etiology, not its status.

Carried into security engineering, this becomes exact. We design an
operator-protective authentication channel from **intrinsic** primitives —
object-capability, zero-knowledge, content-addressing, continuous probabilistic
identity, a corticomuscular-coherence liveness witness, an error-potential veto,
a fuzzy-extractor biometric envelope — each chosen to replace an *extrinsic
check* with a property whose existence *entails* what the check verified, and to
move security from **detection** (catch the attack) to **construction** (make
the attack unrepresentable). A full adversarial teardown then shows the limit:
the cryptographic and biometric layer is the strong part; the residual threat is
the human and the authored root — coercion, perception-blind deception, slow
drift, holder-compromise, will-shaping, and the two irreducible roots
(enrollment and the trusted sensing path). The honest claim the channel may make
is *authentication*; the authorization-of-meaning claim is bounded mitigation,
paid continuously in availability.

**The terminus is a bounded argument.** Any "node-mover" — anything that would let an
authenticated command's *meaning* be captured — would have to be a constructor
of type `free authorship (historical, normative) → present-physical property
(instantaneous, physical)`. That requires two transits: **is → ought** (Hume's
gap) and **history → instant** (the open future). Determinism does not supply
either; it is a thesis about *dynamics*, orthogonal to both axes. Each transit
admits only a *degenerate* constructor — institutional facts (conventional,
contestable) for the first, full-fidelity instantiation (a new author, not a
property) for the second — and **every inhabitant either transit admits is the
node itself**. The gap is therefore not a defended wall that might fall; it is
the *shape of the thing*. Free authorship is unholdable in the live argument
unless someone supplies the missing formal constructor, which is exactly why no
one — not the device, not the adversary, not the designer — gets to hold it. The
defensible perimeter around it is held not by any built property but by process,
re-spoken for the life of the channel.
