# Presentation — *Conferred Existence and the Limits of Authentication*

A defense-style deck (≈28 slides). One claim per slide; speaker notes in
parentheses. Maps the whole arc from metaphysics to a bounded terminus argument.

**Sprint 1 status note.** Use this with `THE-STRAIGHT-LINE.md`: the convergence
claim is abductive pattern-strength, not proof, and the human gate remains open.

---

**1 — Title.** Conferred Existence: From Ontological Nihilism to a Theorem about Authentication.

**2 — The one claim.** Nothing possesses its own existence; existence is conferred; the act of conferral can never be reduced to a property of the thing conferred.

**3 — Two nihilisms.** "Nothing exists" (self-refuting) vs. "no aseity" (defensible). We take only the second. *(Westerhoff; Madhyamaka; van Inwagen.)*

**4 — Existence as speech-act.** *kun* ("Be"); *emet* animates, erase the *aleph* and *met* (death) remains. The *aleph* is the smallest possible edge.

**5 — Creation is evidence FOR nihilism.** The highest creative act produces *dependence*, not aseity (*esse ab alio*; occasionalism). Theology and nihilism converge.

**6 — The correction.** *Waḥdat al-wujūd* makes all forms equal manifestations — it does **not** crown any contingent locus. No "operator in the God-slot."

**7 — The open future.** Tomorrow is not yet spoken. *(Aristotle's sea-battle; Dummett.)* In tension with classical omniscience — heterodox, and we say so.

**8 — The *for* is spoken.** Capability is direction-neutral; the *for* is in the tending, not the seed. Abstraction births every primitive *because* it commits to none.

**9 — Selection's criterion.** "Until it sticks" presupposes a criterion; the criterion is the *for*. Even abiogenesis is selected — by physics, not by nothing.

**10 — Made or grown?** Trained systems are *cultivated*, not fabricated. Standing tracks present capacity, not origin → stewardship, not sovereignty.

**11 — The bridge.** The *for* = authorization. And authorization is not authentication.

**12 — The category line.** Authn: "is P real?" Authz: "is this real P the operator's uncoerced will toward *this* referent, *now*?" Will ≠ a property of a signal. *(Brentano = Hume's is/ought.)*

**13 — Independent convergence.** Philosophy (is/ought) and security (authn/authz) meet without sharing a model. *That* convergence carries evidentiary weight.

**14 — Design law I.** Intrinsic over extrinsic: replace a check with a property whose existence *entails* it. (Capability; ZK; content-addressing.)

**15 — Design law II.** Construction over detection: make the attack unrepresentable. Caveat: impossibility is always *modulo a construction assumption*.

**16 — The channel.** Object-capability · ZK · content-addressing · continuous probabilistic identity · CMC witness · ErrP veto · fuzzy extractor. All edges, no passthrough.

**17 — The witness.** Corticomuscular coherence: live coupling carries liveness + freshness intrinsically; identity via fuzzy extractor (intrinsic core, revocable wrapper).

**18 — The veto.** Error-related potentials: the operator's own cortex flags error — and the reflective self vetoes the motor self.

**19 — Teardown: the roots.** A rational adversary ignores the crypto and attacks the two things that can't be made intrinsic: enrollment and the trusted sensing path.

**20 — The two clocks.** Biometric capture (per-lifetime, irreversible) and root-assumption decay (per-deployment). Both monotone toward certainty → scheduled migration.

**21 — The human seam.** Four roots: intent-semantics, duress, legitimate-but-wrong, biological permanence. Only the last is irreducible-by-construction.

**22 — Will-shaping.** A closed loop can shape what the operator *wants*; no authentication catches a shaped will. Defense: loop-gain below reflection-bandwidth; protect the capacity to revise.

**23 — The clone.** Forge the light, don't hold the flame. Cloneable surface = your predictability; unforgeable residue = your capacity to surprise your own model.

**24 — The honest spec.** Strong claim: authentication, to a thresholded root with degradation that survives the root's fall. Authorization-of-meaning: bounded mitigation, paid in availability.

**25 — The terminus argument.** A node-mover : free authorship (historical, normative) → present-physical property (instant, physical). Two transits, each with only a degenerate constructor; every inhabitant is the node unless a genuine formal constructor is supplied.

**26 — Determinism is orthogonal.** It is a thesis about *dynamics* — it supplies neither readable provenance nor physical-normativity. Not a weak candidate: a non-candidate.

**27 — The gap is the shape.** The is/ought + history/instant gaps are not a *defended* wall that could fall; they are *constitutive*. To "breach" them is to change the subject. Unholdability is the present structural claim; formal theorem status requires an actual formalization.

**28 — Process, not property.** The core is constitutive; the perimeter is held only by continuous re-authoring (occasionalism of defense). "Enough" is authored, not discovered. The remaining crossing is human ownership.
