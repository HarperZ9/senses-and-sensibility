## C — The Cosmological / Epistemic / Civilizational Frame (the bookends)

This cluster does not argue the no-aseity thesis; it *stages* it at the two scales the dissertation cannot itself inhabit — the cosmic and the civilizational — and supplies the epistemological floor beneath both. Treat the cluster as a **frame**, not a proof. Its job is the opening nihilism and the closing return: the universe holds no aseitic meaning, therefore meaning is whatever a conscious locus confers by looking back. ### Carl Sagan — the meaning-closure in a single image

**The works.** *Cosmos* (1980, book and television series); *Pale Blue Dot: A Vision of the Human Future in Space* (1994); *The Demon-Haunted World: Science as a Candle in the Dark* (1995).

**The specific claims.**
- *Cosmos*: "We are a way for the cosmos to know itself." The sentence appears in *Cosmos* (Episode 1 / Chapter 1, "The Shores of the Cosmic Ocean"). **[high]** that the line is Sagan's and from *Cosmos*; **[moderate]** on its *exact* wording — it is frequently rendered "we are a way *for the universe* to know itself," and the book/series wordings differ slightly. Cite it as a claim, not a pinned quotation, unless the exact transcript line is pulled. This is the **handle-with-care** flag for the whole section: the most quotable sentence is the one most often misquoted.
- *Pale Blue Dot*: the "pale blue dot" passage — the Earth as "a mote of dust suspended in a sunbeam," and the moral that no aseitic cosmic significance is conferred on us from outside; "there is no hint that help will come from elsewhere to save us from ourselves." **[high].**
- *The Demon-Haunted World*: the epistemology of the "baloney detection kit" — skepticism, falsifiability, and the demand for evidence as the discipline that keeps a meaning-conferring mind honest. **[high].**

**What it attaches to.** The **Meaning Closure (Part IV, PASSED)** and the **Movement VI orthogonality result** in their cosmological register. Sagan's image is the closure compressed to one frame: an indifferent universe with no meaning-of-its-own (the nihilist premise), plus a conscious *locus* that confers significance *by the act of looking back* — "a way for the cosmos to know itself" is conferred sourcehood spoken at planetary scale. The "undergoing" (the A/B remainder) is exactly what is added when the cosmos is *inhabited* rather than merely described: the pale-blue-dot passage performs the withdrawable-without-subtraction-of-fact structure — the photograph subtracts no physical fact, yet the significance is bestowed, not found.

**Chapter served.** The opening bookend (nihilism premise) and the closing bookend (the return to the inhabited locus). Sagan is the *image* that opens and closes the spiral.

**Epistemic-weight verdict: ILLUSTRATION-ONLY** (with the quote flagged HANDLE-WITH-CARE). Sagan is a popularizer, not a metaphysician; he asserts the conferral picture, he does not argue it. He earns his place as the load-bearing *image* but must not be cited as if he *grounds* the thesis. Using him as an argument rather than an illustration is the failure mode to avoid.

### Yuval Noah Harari — conferral at civilizational scale

**The works.** *Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind* (English ed. 2014); supporting: *Homo Deus* (2016).

**The specific claims.**
- *Sapiens*: the "imagined orders" / "shared fictions" thesis — money, nations, human rights, gods, corporations, and law are not features of physical reality but **intersubjective** entities that exist *only* because large numbers of people collectively believe in them and keep re-affirming them; they are binding exactly insofar as they are collectively re-spoken. **[high].** Harari's stock examples — money as "the most universal and most efficient system of mutual trust ever devised," the limited-liability company (Peugeot) as a legal fiction, "there are no gods in the universe, no nations, no money, no human rights, no laws, and no justice outside the common imagination of human beings." **[high]** on substance; **[moderate]** on exact wording of any single sentence.
- Free-will skepticism / the self-as-story: in *Sapiens* and more pointedly in *Homo Deus*, Harari argues the unitary, free-willing self is a narrative the brain assembles — the "experiencing self" vs. the "narrating self" distinction, and the claim that liberal free will does not survive the life sciences. **[high]** that he makes this argument; **[moderate]** on which book carries which formulation (the experiencing/narrating-self pairing leans on Kahneman and appears most explicitly in *Homo Deus*).

**What it attaches to.** Two corpus results at once, which is why Harari is the workhorse of this cluster:
1. **Movement II conferral, at civilizational scale.** "Imagined orders" *is* the no-aseity thesis applied to institutions: nothing self-standing; status and authority are conferred, relational, and *re-spoken*. This is Searle's institutional facts (status functions, collective acceptance, "X counts as Y in context C") in narrative-historical dress. Harari supplies the **empirical-historical demonstration** that Searle gives analytically — that conferred-and-re-spoken is how money, law, and rights actually came to bind.
2. **Movement IV non-unitary subject.** The self-as-story / free-will skepticism attaches to the plural-brain result (the reflective narrator is *one* sub-personal process speaking for the whole), supplying the civilizational-scale echo of the Libet/Schurger sub-personal reading.

**Chapter served.** Part II (conferral) at the institutional/civilizational tier, and a secondary thread into Movement IV. The bridge from "money is a fiction we keep speaking" to "meaning is significance-for we keep speaking" is the spine of the return.

**Epistemic-weight verdict: LOAD-BEARING SOURCE** — but with a discipline. Harari is load-bearing *for the conferral-at-scale claim*, where the imagined-orders thesis does real work and converges independently with Searle. He is **not** an authority on the free-will metaphysics; there he is reporting a consensus, not establishing one, so cite the self-as-story strand as *illustration of reach*, not as ground. Pair him with Searle in every load-bearing use so the weight rests on the analytic source and Harari carries the historical demonstration. Alone, he reads as pop-history; tethered to Searle, he is the civilizational instantiation the thesis needs.

### Stephen Hawking (with Leonard Mlodinow) — the epistemic floor

**The work.** *The Grand Design* (2010), co-authored with Leonard Mlodinow — **cite both authors; the "model-dependent realism" doctrine is theirs jointly, not Hawking's alone.** **[high].**

**The specific claim.** **Model-dependent realism**: there is no theory- or picture-independent test of reality; we never access reality "as it is in itself," only models, and a model is to be judged by its empirical adequacy and internal coherence rather than by correspondence to an aseitic reality behind the model. Their own illustration: the goldfish in a curved bowl could formulate valid physical laws from inside its distorted frame, and we have no standpoint from which to declare its picture "less real" than ours — only differently modeled. **[high]** on the doctrine and the goldfish example.

**What it attaches to.** The **no-aseity *epistemology*** that underwrites the entire framework. If existence/status/meaning are conferred and never self-standing (the ontological claim), model-dependent realism is the matching epistemic claim: we never reach an aseitic reality to *measure* the conferral against, because access itself is model-mediated and frame-relative. This closes a flank the dissertation must defend — the objection "but surely there's a real reality the conferral falsifies against." Hawking–Mlodinow answer: there is no model-independent vantage from which to lodge that objection. It also reinforces **Movement VI orthogonality**: a complete causal/physical model is *still a model*, inhabited and selected, not a God's-eye readout — which is exactly why the complete causal story "neither locates nor dissolves" the inhabited standpoint.

**Chapter served.** The epistemic bookend — placed early, beneath the cosmological and civilizational frames, so the reader cannot retreat to naive realism when the conferral thesis lands.

**Epistemic-weight verdict: LOAD-BEARING SOURCE, used narrowly.** Model-dependent realism does genuine structural work as the no-aseity epistemology, and it is a *physicists'* formulation, which earns it weight a pure-philosophy citation could not. The handle-with-care caveat: it is a contested and somewhat loose doctrine (philosophers of science note it slides between instrumentalism and structural realism), so cite it for the **single load-bearing point** — no model-independent access to an aseitic reality — and do *not* lean on its finer commitments. Used for that one claim, it is the cleanest epistemic floor available; over-read, it invites a philosophy-of-science quarrel the dissertation does not want.

### How the cluster frames the spiral

The three are tiered deliberately, and the tiering is the argument:

- **Hawking–Mlodinow (epistemic floor, LOAD-BEARING, narrow):** *we never reach an aseitic reality to check against* — so the question "what is the real, un-conferred meaning?" has no standpoint from which to be asked.
- **Sagan (cosmological bookend, ILLUSTRATION):** *the universe confers no meaning on itself* — the nihilist premise, made into one indelible image, with the conscious locus as the only site where "the cosmos knows itself."
- **Harari (civilizational tier, LOAD-BEARING with Searle):** *at the scale of money, law, and rights, conferred-and-re-spoken is demonstrably how meaning binds* — the empirical proof that the conferral mechanism is not a philosopher's fancy but the actual load-bearing structure of human worlds.

Read together they trace the spiral the dissertation argues: **no aseitic meaning anywhere** (Sagan's indifferent cosmos, Hawking's inaccessible reality-in-itself) → **therefore all meaning is conferred at the inhabited locus** (Harari's re-spoken orders, generalized to the significance-for of the Meaning Closure) → **and the conferral is alive while inhabited, void when withdrawn** (the *emet / met* of the Aleph, now legible at cosmic and civilizational scale, not only the personal). The bookends keep the thesis from reading as a private phenomenology: the same withdrawable-without-subtraction-of-fact structure that governs one life governs a currency and a cosmos.
