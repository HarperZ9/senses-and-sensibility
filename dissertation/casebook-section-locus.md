## B — The Locus / Meaning / Trauma Cluster → Part IV (the Meaning Closure)

This cluster supplies the empirical and clinical casebook for two of Part IV's load-bearing moves: the **A/B undergoing** (that there is a felt "what-it-is-like" remainder no functional redescription closes) and the **occasionalism of meaning** (significance as continuously re-bestowed *for* an inhabited locus, withdrawable without subtraction of fact and re-inhabitable without addition). It also reaches back to Movement IV's **non-unitary subject**. The cluster's danger is precisely the dissertation's stated risk: four well-known names can read as a parade. The defense is tiering. Only one figure here is genuinely load-bearing for the closure; the others are illustration or must be handled with care, and I mark where the joint is *my* argumentative attachment rather than the author's own claim.

---

### Viktor Frankl — *Man's Search for Meaning* (1946; logotherapy)

**Status: ILLUSTRATION-ONLY, with one honest tension that must be staged, not smoothed.** (Already in the chapter.)

- **The work and the actual claim.** Frankl's clinical thesis is that meaning is the primary human motivation ("will to meaning"), and — critically for us — that meaning is *discovered* or *found* in a situation, not invented or assigned. His formulation is that one cannot *give* life a meaning; one detects the meaning a concrete situation demands and responds to it. He locates meaning in three sources: creative work, experience/love, and the attitude one takes toward unavoidable suffering. [high]
- **What it attaches to.** Frankl is the clinical witness for *significance-for-a-locus surviving fact*: in the camps the facts (starvation, loss, imminent death) were fixed, yet meaning could be present or absent depending on the prisoner's inhabited stance — the man who "had a why" endured. That is a near-perfect anecdotal instance of meaning as withdrawable-without-subtraction-of-fact. **Serves Part IV.**
- **The honest tension — do not paper over it.** Frankl's verbs are *found*, *discovered*, *detected*. The corpus says *conferred* and *re-bestowed*. These are not synonyms. Frankl is a realist about meaning: the meaning is *out there* in the situation, awaiting detection, with a strongly theistic horizon ("ultimate meaning," "super-meaning") underwriting it. The occasionalism-of-meaning thesis is *anti-realist in that specific sense* — there is no pre-seated meaning waiting to be read off; there is a re-speaking *to* a locus. So Frankl is a **witness against fact-realism about significance** while being a **realist about meaning's source**. Use him for the first and explicitly mark the divergence on the second; do not recruit "meaning cannot be given" as if it endorsed conferral. It is the opposite verb. This is the single most important place not to inflate. [high]
- **Epistemic-weight verdict: ILLUSTRATION-ONLY.** The memoir is testimony, not mechanism. Cite it for the phenomenology of significance-under-fixed-fact; quarantine its realist metaphysics.

---

### Bessel van der Kolk — *The Body Keeps the Score* (2014); trauma as undergone and somatically stored

**Status: LOAD-BEARING SOURCE — the one genuinely load-bearing figure in this cluster.**

This is where the casebook does real work for the closure, on two fronts.

- **Front 1 — the A/B undergoing made empirical.** Van der Kolk's central, repeatedly stated claim is that traumatic experience is registered and retained *below* and *apart from* the narrative-propositional system: stored as somatic state, sensation, and implicit memory rather than as a tellable story. He marshals the clinical phenomena of **alexithymia** (the inability to put inner states into words) and the dissociation of the self-observing capacity to argue that the body retains what the verbalizing self cannot represent. The corpus's A/B distinction — that the *undergoing* is a remainder no functional/propositional redescription closes — gets an empirical analogue here: trauma is precisely an undergoing that resists discursive closure, that "the body keeps" when the redescribing self cannot. This is also the empirical face of **Merleau-Ponty's incarnate meaning** (the lived, pre-reflective body as a meaning-bearing standpoint), which is why the cluster pairs them. [high for the book's thesis; **moderate** for the strength of the A/B mapping — see caveat]
  - *Caveat (the load-bearing joint is partly mine).* The identification "trauma-as-somatically-stored ≅ the A/B undergoing-remainder" is **my philosophical attachment**, not van der Kolk's claim. He is making a claim about memory systems and neurobiology, not about an irreducible phenomenal remainder in the philosophy-of-mind sense. The dissertation should present this as *the corpus reading van der Kolk*, an interpretive use of clinical data, not as something he asserts. Stated that way it is defensible; stated as "van der Kolk shows the A/B remainder is real" it overclaims and invites the parade critique. [high — this is a methodological point, not an empirical one]

- **Front 2 — the literal test data for the occasionalism of meaning.** This is the strongest, most specific use, and it is genuinely van der Kolk's territory. In trauma and in depression, **significance drains while the facts hold steady**: the person can still report, accurately, that they have a family, a job, a history — the propositional inventory is intact — but the *mattering* is gone; the world is grey, depersonalized, "behind glass." In recovery, the facts have not changed, yet significance returns. That is exactly the corpus's *withdrawable-without-subtraction-of-fact* and *re-inhabitable-without-addition*: nothing in the factual ledger is added or removed across the withdrawal and return of meaning. **This is the empirical operationalization of the occasionalism of meaning, and it is the reason van der Kolk is load-bearing rather than illustrative.** [high for the clinical phenomenon of intact-facts-drained-significance; the phenomenon is also well-attested in the depression and depersonalization literatures independently, which strengthens it beyond a single author]
  - *Strengthening move:* because this depersonalization/anhedonia phenomenon is independently documented outside van der Kolk (clinical depression, depersonalization-derealization disorder), the closure does not rest on a single popular book. Cite van der Kolk as the vivid clinical articulation but anchor the *claim* in the broader phenomenology so the argument survives any specific critique of *The Body Keeps the Score*. [high]

- **Handle-with-care note on the source itself.** *The Body Keeps the Score* is a trade book and has drawn methodological criticism (some neurobiological claims are stronger in popular framing than in the underlying literature; certain studies it leans on have been contested). This does **not** touch the two phenomena we use — somatic/implicit retention and intact-facts-drained-significance are not the contested parts — but the dissertation should cite the *phenomena* and the supporting clinical literature, not the book's more sweeping neuro-claims, so a hostile examiner cannot discredit the closure by discrediting a popularization. [moderate]

- **Epistemic-weight verdict: LOAD-BEARING SOURCE** for Part IV's occasionalism front, **with the explicit qualification** that (a) the A/B mapping is the corpus's interpretive reading, not van der Kolk's assertion, and (b) the load is carried by the clinical *phenomena*, which are independently attested, more than by the trade book as such.

### Richard Schwartz — Internal Family Systems; *No Bad Parts* (2021)

**Status: LOAD-BEARING for the non-unitary-subject map (Movement IV); ILLUSTRATION-to-LOAD-BEARING for the unity-vs-connectedness question.**

- **The work and the verified claim.** IFS models the mind as a plurality of **parts** in structured roles — *exiles* (carriers of wounded affect), and two classes of *protectors*, *managers* (proactive) and *firefighters* (reactive) — organized around a **Self** that is *not itself a part*. Schwartz's explicit, distinctive structural claim is that the Self is not one subpersonality among others and not the "ego/manager" (the ego is itself a coalition of protective parts); the Self is a witnessing, leading center characterized by the "**8 Cs**" of Self-energy (calm, curiosity, compassion, etc.). IFS further posits "**Self-like parts**" — managers that *imitate* the Self's calm or compassion but lack its genuine connectivity — and the clinical task of distinguishing the true Self from its imitators. [high — verified against the IFS model description, including the "Self is not a part," the manager/firefighter/exile taxonomy, the 8 Cs, and the Self-like-part subtype]
- **What it attaches to — and the precise philosophical payload.** This is the clinical map of **Movement IV's non-unitary subject**: the brain/mind is plural, and there is a leading locus that is not reducible to any of the parts. The exact value for the corpus is in the **unity-vs-connectedness distinction**: IFS denies *substantival unity* (there is no single homuncular self) while affirming a *leading, witnessing locus* that integrates by *connection* rather than by *being one more thing*. That is a remarkably close clinical analogue to **conferred sourcehood** — agency that is neither libertarian self-origination (the Self does not *manufacture* the parts or stand outside the system) nor determinism's dissolution (the locus genuinely *leads*). The Self is the **inhabited standpoint** that orchestrates without being a substance. **Serves Movement IV and feeds Part III's conferred-sourcehood.** [high for the structural claim; **moderate** for the strength of the conferred-sourcehood analogy, which is again *my* attachment]
- **Handle-with-care wrinkle (the one the verification surfaced).** Schwartz's **later work drifts metaphysical** — "guides," "unattached burdens" as external ancestral/entity energies entering during dissociation, the Self as a "sacred essence" connected to a "Divine" consciousness, and unburdening procedures critics have called "exorcism." Mainstream clinical framing reads the Self instead as a *state of neurological integration* (prefrontal observation without amotional overwhelm). **The corpus must cite the clinical-structural IFS (parts + non-part Self + 8 Cs) and explicitly disavow the entity/Divine metaphysics**, or it imports exactly the kind of unfalsifiable apparatus the no-aseity framework is trying to *replace*. The conferred-sourcehood reading actively conflicts with "Self as sacred self-standing essence" — a Self that is a sacred *aseity* is the wrong model for a framework whose thesis is conferral *without* aseity. Use IFS's structure; reject its later substance-of-Self. [high]
- **Epistemic-weight verdict: LOAD-BEARING SOURCE for the non-unitary-subject map**, **ILLUSTRATION-toward-load-bearing** for unity-vs-connectedness, **strictly fenced** from Schwartz's spiritual metaphysics.

### C.G. Jung — individuation; the Self as archetype of wholeness; thrownness into unauthored archetypal structure

**Status: HANDLE-WITH-CARE.**

- **The work and the claim.** Jung's **individuation** is the lifelong process of integrating conscious and unconscious contents toward psychic wholeness; the **Self** (distinct from the ego) is the archetype of wholeness and the regulating center/telos of the psyche; the individual is "thrown" into pre-given **archetypal structures** (the collective unconscious) it did not author and must come to terms with. [high for these being Jung's actual positions]
- **What it attaches to.** Two genuine contact points. (1) **Thrownness into unauthored structure** rhymes with the corpus's *thrown inhabited locus* — one inherits the archetypal/symbolic field as one inherits a language, and confers meaning *from within* a structure one did not originate. (2) **The Self-as-telos** offers a teleological gloss on the inhabited locus's directedness. Both **could** serve Part IV's locus and Part III's sourcehood — *as framing and vocabulary, not as evidence.* [moderate that these are real resonances; **low** that they do probative work]
- **Why handle-with-care — and be blunt about it.** In analytic-philosophy and empirically-oriented departments, Jung's archetypes and collective unconscious carry a **rigor-reception liability**: they are widely regarded as unfalsifiable, and leaning on them as *support* will *lower* the dissertation's credibility precisely with the examiners the framework most needs to convince. There is also a **structural conflict** identical to the Schwartz wrinkle but sharper: Jung's Self is a *substantival archetype of wholeness*, a self-standing telos — which is close to an **aseity of the Self**, the very thing "Conferral Without Aseity" denies. Recruiting Jung's Self as the inhabited locus risks smuggling back the aseity the title forecloses. [high on the reception liability; high on the structural conflict]
- **Permitted use.** Cite Jung **for structure and vocabulary** — individuation as a name for the integrative trajectory, thrownness-into-archetype as an evocative parallel to thrown locus — and **frame it explicitly as illustrative resonance the corpus reinterprets, not as authority.** Never cite Jung as evidence that the locus or the Self is real, and never let Jung's wholeness-telos define the locus, or the no-aseity thesis collapses. **Serves Part IV/III at the level of framing only.** [high — this constraint is the safe envelope]
- **Epistemic-weight verdict: HANDLE-WITH-CARE.** Tier explicitly as illustration; foreground the reinterpretation; quarantine the substantival/teleological Self.
