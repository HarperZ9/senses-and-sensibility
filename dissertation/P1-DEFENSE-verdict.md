# Verdict on P1 — "Conferral is occupation"

## 0. The question, stated as the dossier forces it

P1 claims that *occurring* entails *occupied-at-an-index* in a sense **stronger** than "is a dependently-arisen event." The C/Z disjunction that forces the dissertation's seam is exhaustive **only if** P1 holds at a level stronger than mere `pratityasamutpada`. The single admissible standard:

> Demonstrate the INCOHERENCE of "indexed-at-nothing occurrence" WITHOUT ALREADY ASSUMING THE INDEX.

Two objectors meet in the middle — both **grant the occurrence** and **deny the index-as-residue**: (i) Prasangika Madhyamaka (ownerless occurrence; the goer/going dissolution; `sunyata-sunyata`), and (ii) the deflationary type-A physicalist (the thin/thick dilemma; Stroud's gap relocated inside the predicate "conferral"). A defense wins outright only by defeating **both** without presupposing the index.

A critical disambiguation governs everything below:

- **THIN index** = the event is a concrete particular at a determinate spatiotemporal position. Every dependent event has this. It does **no modal work** P1 needs; it is `pratityasamutpada` with a coordinate attached.
- **THICK index** = a standpoint / perspective **from which** sorting is performed *as this sorting and not its negation*. This is what P1 actually requires to force the C/Z seam.

The entire verdict turns on the gap between these two. The dossier's own structure makes this gap the load-bearing joint.

Three defenses are simply dead — every lens reports `presupposes`:

- **act-potency** dies because "actualization = collapse to a THIS-side" encodes asymmetric directedness by fiat; `pratityasamutpada` is symmetric co-arising with no privileged vertex. The Aristotelian `entelecheia` is not a physical kind the type-A physicalist grants, so it imports the index rather than earning it. Step 7 (the denier's enacting act) reaches only the conventional ceiling, and does so by a *different* mechanism — it is P1 applied to a token, not derived from premises the opponent grants.
- **distinction** dies because Spencer-Brown's single primitive — "draw a distinction" — **already** contains an interior/exterior relative to a crossing performed from somewhere. Mapping "index" to "the mark's interior" reads the index back out of a formalism that embedded it at the foundation. The circularity auditor scores it `none`: the index enters at Step 1 as a *definition*, and the Madhyamaka contests precisely that definition. An analytic argument is non-circular only if the opponent grants the meaning of the antecedent; here the denial **is** a denial of that meaning.
- **givenness** dies hardest (two lenses at `level=none`). "Act" is made to carry from-here structure by definitional stipulation (`prajnapti`), then the structure is harvested as though phenomenologically discovered. Calling the dependent stream "an act" rather than "a complex causal process" is the contested overlay. Under type-A physicalism the Nullpunkt is either proprioceptive self-modeling (THIN) or a smuggled non-physical premise. The performative reply fails because a Prasangika running a `prasanga` asserts no thesis from anywhere — the goer/going analysis dissolves the very "performative self" the retorsion needs.

That leaves three live candidates, and they share an identical fate: **survival at the conventional level, on the thin index only.**

## 2. The decisive finding: the circularity auditor clears three defenses — but only to the thin index

The lens whose entire job **is** the admissible standard — the circularity auditor — returns `index=independent` for exactly three defenses: **retorsion**, **token-reflexive**, **deflation-internal**. This is the most important datum in the dossier, and it must not be overread.

What the auditor certifies is narrow and real: in these three, "occupied-at-an-index" is **derived, not smuggled**. The derivation runs from premises the opponent **already grants**:

- For **token-reflexive** and **deflation-internal**: occurrence = tokening (Peirce — tokens are what exist) and tokening = spatiotemporal individuation (SI / trope individuation, *the deflationist's own* account, Campbell 1990 / Schaffer 2001). Neither link mentions conferral, standpoints, or discrimination. The index falls out of the **general metaphysics of particulars**, applied to conferral as one event among all.
- For **retorsion**: the inference runs from the opponent's own granted characterization of the denial — that it has propositional/evaluative form, that a `prasanga` selects consequences as damaging — to "is enacted as this-and-not-that." The Madhyamaka, to describe her **own argumentative practice**, must grant the directed form; the index is then a consequence of *her* description, not an imported premise.

So the answer to question 1 — *does any defense bridge occurs → occupied-at-an-index without presupposing the index?* — is **yes**. But the bridge lands on the **thin** index.

## 3. Why the thin index is not P1's index — the two objectors close ranks

Here the Madhyamaka and deflationist do the work the auditor cannot, and they converge on the same verdict from opposite directions.

**The deflationist (thin/thick dilemma).** Against token-reflexive and deflation-internal, the deflationist concedes the thin index *cleanly* — and then shows it is idle. Either:
- (a) "standpoint" just means spatiotemporal location → every physical event satisfies it (a thermostat transition is token-distinct and indexed), the index does no modal work, and **the seam collapses to `pratityasamutpada` with a Latin label**; or
- (b) "standpoint" means something richer — a perspective, a discriminating subject-pole → then the step from "is located at L" to "sorts FROM L" reads a normative/intentional structure off a physical description that does not contain it. **That is Stroud's gap reopened inside the predicate "conferral."**

The deflation-internal attack sharpens this to the cleanest form in the dossier: identifying the located event *as a conferral-token* (rather than as heat-dissipation at the same coordinate) requires the event to instantiate the type "conferral" as a real property — a fact over and above the substrate that type-A physicalism denies. The conditional "IF conferral-token THEN indexed" is valid; **the antecedent is assumed, not earned.** Two of the three live defenses score `level=none` under this lens for exactly this reason.

**The Madhyamaka (`sunyata-sunyata`).** Against the same defenses, the Madhyamaka refuses to treat the type/token distinction and SI as framework-independent. SI ("a = b iff same spatiotemporal position") is itself a *thesis* — and if accepted as what *really* individuates, it makes spatiotemporal coordinates intrinsic identity-fixers, which is **svabhava reasoning**. She grants spatiotemporal *description* as conventional function; she denies it picks out a real individuating essence. Crucially, even "occurrence" and "token" are empty, and their emptiness is empty — so the incoherence of "0-residue conferral" **cannot be underwritten by a type/token joint, because that joint is not a fixed metaphysical joint.** The retorsion's "perspectival determinacy survives substance-dissolution" is the exact spot the index re-enters: it is either own-being under a relational label (contradicting emptiness) or mere conventional designation (`pratityasamutpada` renamed). The Madhyamaka **denies the middle the retorsion needs** — "relational but real, perspectival without substance, conventional but stronger than designation" — because `sunyata-sunyata` empties the perspectival-character concept too.

The defenders' **own self-audits concede this.** Token-reflexive: the thick-index extension "is not fully secured." Deflation-internal: "borderline... insufficient against a Prasangika who distinguishes samvrti from paramartha." Retorsion: "at the ultimate level, `sunyata-sunyata` does dissolve even this perspectival property." None of the three claims to have crossed the thin/thick line.

## 4. Naming the single strongest survivor and exactly where it holds and stops

**Strongest surviving defense: token-reflexive** (with deflation-internal its co-equal twin; I separate them only at the margin).

**Why token-reflexive over its rivals:**
- It is one of only two defenses the circularity auditor clears with `index=independent` while resting on premises drawn from the *general* metaphysics of particulars (not from conferral, not from a contested realist apparatus the way distinction's Spencer-Brown is contested).
- Its closing move against the denier is a genuine, non-question-begging regress: deny that the denial is a token, and the denial-of-the-denial is performed as a token. This **forces** the thin index on the enacting party without assuming a standpoint-substance. That is precisely the FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY mechanism the task anticipates ("the denier's own enacting act instantiates an index at the conventional level").

**Why deflation-internal is its twin, not its superior:** deflation-internal is marginally cleaner on circularity (it uses *only* the deflationist's commitments, so it cannot be accused of importing a foreign framework). But that very economy is also its exposure: the Madhyamaka reframes SI itself as a covert svabhava thesis, and the deflationist scores it `level=none` because identifying the located event *as conferral* is the unearned antecedent. Token-reflexive carries the same thin/thick wound but pairs it with the explicit performative regress, which is the part that actually does the *forcing*. I therefore name token-reflexive as the survivor and treat deflation-internal as confirming the same ceiling by an independent route.

**Exactly what token-reflexive holds:** occurs → is-a-token → individuated → occupies-a-**spatiotemporal-position**. The denier's denial is a located token; she cannot say otherwise without performing another located token. **THIN index, samvrti, non-circular, opponent-granted.** "Conferral with a 0 residue" is incoherent **as a conventional truth**: a type with no token does not occur.

**Exactly where it stops:** the move from "located at L" to "performs the sorting FROM L *as this discrimination*" — the **thick** index, P1's actual content — is never derived. The defense's self-audit flags this as the open joint, and the deflationist and Madhyamaka both confirm it is not a minor caveat but **the load-bearing joint P1 needs.** At paramartha, `sunyata-sunyata` empties occurrence/token/position; the thick residue is zero.

## 5. Verdict

**P1-FORCED-CONVENTIONALLY.**

- Not **P1-PROVEN**: no defense earns the thick standpoint-index without either presupposing it or invoking a structure (`entelecheia`, the Spencer-Brown crossing, the Husserlian "act", "perspectival determinacy") that `sunyata-sunyata` dissolves. PROVEN-at-the-ultimate-level self-refutes here exactly as the task warned, and the dossier does not earn the exception.
- Not **P1-FAILS**: the seam is **not** merely `pratityasamutpada` renamed *with nothing added*. A genuine, non-circular, opponent-grantable result survives: the denier's enacting act instantiates the thin index, and "0-residue conferral" is conventionally incoherent. The circularity auditor's three `index=independent` findings are real. The contribution does **not** collapse to the bare membrane identity (authn ≠ authz :: is/ought).
- Not **P1-DEFENSIBLE-AS-DECLARED-PREMISE**: a declared premise is one *no* defense earns independently. Here a defense **does** earn the (thin) index independently. P1 is therefore more than a stipulation a committee swallows — but **only** at the conventional level. Its *thick* content remains a declared, undischarged premise; the candidate must defend that part dialectically, conceding the ultimate 0.

**The honest ceiling is exactly the one the corpus already conceded.** The dossier confirms FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY-ONLY; it does not exceed it, and it does not fall below it.

## 6. Consequence for the contribution

**holds-conventional.** The bounded result stands: at the samvrti / conventional level, occurring forces a (thin) index, the denier cannot enact a counterexample, and "conferral with 0 residue" is incoherent there. The contribution is a real bounded conventional result — neither a proof of necessity (it is not) nor a renamed triviality (it is not *merely* that, because the performative forcing is genuine). It does **not** expand to an ultimate result, and it does **not** shrink to the membrane identity. The seam does the work P1 was meant to do **at one level only**, and the keystone/Contribution text must say so in those exact terms.

## 7. Errata forced (summary)

1. Strike any "PROVEN / ultimate / unconditional" framing of P1. Earned result: occurs → thin index, samvrti only.
2. Downgrade the C/Z disjunction's exhaustiveness to the conventional level; it is not a paramartha constraint.
3. Disambiguate "indexed at a standpoint" wherever it appears: thick reading unearned (declared premise), thin reading earned.
4. Bound the contribution to FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY-ONLY; name the ultimate 0 as a *conceded* Madhyamaka limit, not a defeated objection.
5. Confirm — do not exceed — the corpus's prior concession.
