# What the Contribution Owes to Rejecting Type-B Physicalism

*Adjudicated verdict: **shrinks-to-membrane**. The reframe is type-B-neutral and succeeds —
but only for the bottom two-thirds of the edifice. The one claim `00-CONTRIBUTION.md` names
as original — the **forced relocation** of the explanatory gap from states to seams — is
type-B-dependent, undischarged, and additionally returned as REDESCRIPTION by the thesis's
own Law. The premise is **declarable, not defensible-as-proven.** This document states
precisely what rests on rejecting type-B and what does not, and does not re-inflate.*

---

## 0. The debt, stated

The contribution prints two undischarged conditions (`00-CONTRIBUTION.md` §"conditions"):
P1 (adjudicated FORCED-CONVENTIONALLY) and **the rejection of type-B physicalism** —
"every result the Remainder Law certifies inherits the explanatory gap's unsettled status
against the physicalist who closes it." This front litigates the second debt. The question
is not whether type-B is true (the hard problem is genuinely unsettled and this document
manufactures no proof either way) but **what in the contribution survives if it is**, and
**what the thesis may honestly claim** given that the rejection can be declared but not
proven.

The three live outcomes were tested, not assumed. The result: **(a) is unavailable, (b)
succeeds partially, (c) is already operative and needs only sharpening.** The combination is
**(b)+(c) = shrink-to-membrane**.

## I. The PCS deflation, stated exactly

Type-B physicalism via the Phenomenal Concept Strategy (Loar 1990; Papineau 2002; Block &
Stalnaker 1999; Tye 2003; Hill & McLaughlin 1999; Stoljar 2005) grants a real
epistemic/explanatory gap and denies it is ontological: the gap is a feature of our
phenomenal **concepts**, not of reality. If type-B holds, the forced seam's "irreducibility"
is merely epistemic, and clause (iii) detects a fact about how we conceive conferral, not a
feature of the world — the "result" deflates to a claim about conception, not about what is.

The deflation lands at exactly one joint: the **thick-index** reading of P1. A type-B
physicalist says the appearance of irreducibility in "sorting-from-a-standpoint" is a feature
of our intentional/phenomenal concept of the act — and Loar's recognitional account is **not
restricted to qualia** — not of the act itself; relocating the gap merely relocates the
concept-application site, and the gap reappears for the conceptual reasons PCS specifies.

---

## II. Outcome (a) — Rejection-defensible — is UNAVAILABLE

The strongest published anti-PCS argument is Chalmers' master dilemma ("Phenomenal Concepts
and the Explanatory Gap," 2006/2007). Let C be any psychological thesis a PCS proponent uses
to explain the epistemic gap. **Verified verbatim from primary source** (consc.net/papers/pceg.html,

> "(1) If P&~C is conceivable, then C is not physically explicable.
> (2) If P&~C is not conceivable, then C cannot explain our epistemic situation.
> (3) Either C is not physically explicable, or C cannot explain our epistemic situation."

Formidable. But it does **not** refute type-B, and Chalmers says so in print (verified
verbatim, same source, lines 897–901; **high confidence**):

> "(Note that in this paper I am not arguing that type-B materialism is false. I have done
> that elsewhere. Here I am simply arguing that the phenomenal concepts strategy provides no
> support for type-B materialism, and provides no grounds for rejecting arguments from the
> epistemic gap to an ontological gap.)"

And the escape route is explicit (verified verbatim, lines 888–895; **high confidence**): a
type-B materialist who **drops PCS and accepts an explanatorily primitive (brute) identity**
between physical/functional and phenomenal properties is untouched by the dilemma — "the
phenomenal concept strategy will do nothing to undermine these reasons." Loar's own endorsed
move. Goff (2011, *Mind* 120/478) and Nida-Rümelin add anti-PCS arguments but support
panpsychism / broad non-physicalism, not a refutation of type-B; both are contested.

**Verdict on (a): the rejection of type-B cannot be derived from the anti-PCS literature.**
The hard problem is unsettled; type-B is a live position held by serious philosophers. No
committee may be told this is settled. **Confidence the rejection is a knock-down: low.** It
can only be **declared and defended dialectically — exactly as P1's thick index is.**

### B. Type-B-DEPENDENT (the distinctive original claim)

The originality claim as stated in `00-CONTRIBUTION.md`: the **RELOCATION** of the
explanatory-gap/irreducibility structure (Nagel 1974 / Levine 1983) from a **state**
(consciousness) to a **conferral-seam**, and the showing that it is **FORCED** there.

This claim's third load-bearer — that what is forced at the seam **IS the same
irreducibility the hard problem identifies** — requires the **thick-index** reading of P1:
conferral performed "from a standpoint as this sorting," perspectivally irreducible, not
merely "located at L." `P1-DEFENSE-verdict.md` concedes the thick index is undischarged: the
step from "located at L" to "sorts *from* L" "reads intentional structure off a physical
description; that is **Stroud's gap reopened inside the predicate 'conferral'**" (§4, lines
54–56).

The PCS lands **exactly here**. The full-scope Keystone Conclusion (perspectivally
irreducible standpoint forced wherever conferral occurs), and any clause-(iii\*) certification
whose basis is the relocated explanatory gap applied to **intentional/phenomenal** phenomena
(as opposed to a governing-theory provability result), **inherit the explanatory gap's
unsettled status** against the physicalist who closes it. **Confidence: high** that this is
the joint where type-B dependency genuinely bites.

## IV. The compounding cost — the thesis's own Law does not certify the relocation

The verdict's decisive corpus fact, which the draft underweights: even **setting type-B
aside**, the Remainder Law returns **REDESCRIPTION**, not RESULT, on the forced-seam claim.
`REMAINDER-LAW-TEST.md` §3: case **C7** — "every conferral-event is identical, under a second
description, to a non-zero occupied seam-event," the *exact* forcing target — is blind-graded
REDESCRIPTION by both graders, every time, via **clause (ii)**: under P1 ("conferral is
occupation"), non-zero occupancy is entailed by the bare existence of S, so the claim has no
surplus content over P1. "**The forced-seam claim is P1 restated, not a result the Law
earns.**"

So two independent instruments converge on the same narrowing:
1. **Type-B dependency:** the relocation is conditional on rejecting type-B (undischarged).
2. **The thesis's own demarcation filter:** correctly applied, does **not** certify the
   relocation as a RESULT — it returns REDESCRIPTION (clause ii).

The type-B-neutral results the Law *can* certify (Bell, joint-force) are **not** the
relocation the thesis wants. Net: **what is type-B-neutral is not the relocation; what is the
relocation is not type-B-neutral and is not even Law-certifiable.** The reframe does not
rescue the core. It produces a shrink-to-membrane.

## V. Outcome (b) — Reframe-type-B-neutral — succeeds CLEANLY but PARTIALLY

The reframe's mechanism is severing the identification **"seam-gap = hard-problem gap."**
Once severed, what is type-B-neutral is **(i) a DEONTIC gap** (Pigden's is/ought, partially
anticipated by ABLP / von Wright / Searle) and **(ii) a STRUCTURAL gap** (thin-index
occurrence-particularity). The PCS was built for *qualia* concepts and does not reach either
a structural-deontic gap or a formal-provability gap.

But severing the identification is **also what costs the thesis its headline.** Neither the
deontic gap nor the structural gap **is a relocation of the phenomenal explanatory gap**
(what-it-is-like, Nagel/Levine). They are **different gaps**. So the reframe does not relocate
the gap type-B-neutrally — it **abandons** the relocation and keeps two adjacent, weaker,
already-conceded-as-partially-anticipated results.

**The honest cost, named:** if the thick reading is suspended, the Keystone Conclusion
weakens to "wherever conferral occurs, a located non-zero particular event occurs, and
dissolution cannot reach 0 while conferral is happening." Substantive against
Madhyamaka/Stroudian deflationism **at the conventional level** — but it does **not** force
the irreducibility of the perspectival standpoint. "Structural seity" survives at the thin
level; what does not survive is that the seam is perspectivally irreducible in the
hard-problem sense. **Confidence: high** that partial decoupling works and is honest;
**moderate** that it preserves what the thesis wants from the relocation move — it does not.

## VI. Outcome (c) — Declared-premise — already operative; sharpen the bound

The corpus already declares the condition (`00-CONTRIBUTION.md` condition 2;
forcing-movement §5/§6). The declaration is accurate but **too broad** — a blanket "every
result the Law certifies inherits the gap's unsettled status" obscures the membrane's full
independence. The refined, bounded declaration the thesis should print:

- The **membrane result** (is/ought gap, deontic bridge premise, unmeasurability, failure
  taxonomy) does **not** inherit the dependency — it rests on Pigden's theorem and ABLP's
  formal non-entailment. Even if type-B is true, no authentication facts entail an
  authorization verdict.
- The **thin-index structural seam** (P1's conventional forcing, P2, P4, Keystone Conclusion
  at thin reading) does **not** inherit it — spatiotemporal particularity, not phenomenal
  character.
- The **provable clause-(iii\*) certifications** (Bell, joint-force, PDP/PIP) do **not**
  inherit it — governing-theory provability, not phenomenal-concept gaps.
- The **thick-index perspectival irreducibility** (P1's full force) and any clause-(iii\*)
  certification of the relocated explanatory gap applied to intentional/phenomenal phenomena
  **do** inherit it — and are the thesis's distinctive novel claim.

## VIII. Net

The type-B-neutral remainder is **genuine and non-trivial**: a clean is/ought instance in an
engineered system, a forced thin seam against Madhyamaka/Stroud deflationism at the
conventional level, and a working filter for provable relational non-entailments. But it is,
by the corpus's own admission, the **partially-anticipated and modest** part (membrane:
"moderate novelty"; thin seam: weaker than the thesis wants).

**The novel part is exactly the type-B-dependent part.** The distinctive claim — the forced
**relocation** of the explanatory gap from states to seams — is (i) conditional on rejecting
type-B, (ii) undischarged because that rejection is declarable but not provable (Chalmers'
own anti-PCS argument disclaims refuting type-B), and (iii) additionally returned as
REDESCRIPTION by the thesis's own Remainder Law (C7, clause ii).

That is the shrink-to-membrane: **not a catastrophic collapse, but a real narrowing of the
thesis's maximum ambition to its honest, conditional, undischarged core.** The rejection of
type-B is **defensible-as-declarable, not defensible-as-proven** — and the honest path is to
print the condition at exactly the joint where it bites (the thick-index relocation), and to
free the membrane, the thin seam, and the provable certifications from a dependency they
never carried.
