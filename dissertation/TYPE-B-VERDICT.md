# What the Contribution Owes to Rejecting Type-B Physicalism — Adjudication

## Verdict: SHRINKS-TO-MEMBRANE (with a clean, honest partial reframe available)

The reframe to type-B-neutrality **succeeds for the bottom two-thirds of the edifice and fails for the one claim the thesis names as original.** Decoupling the core from qualia-ontology does not rescue the core; it abandons the relocation and keeps the membrane plus the thin-index seam. ## I. The two facts the whole adjudication turns on (verified, not from memory)

**Fact 1 — Chalmers' scope disclaimer (exact quote, line 892–895 of the primary source):**
> \"Note that in this paper I am not arguing that type-B materialism is false. I have done that elsewhere. Here I am simply arguing that the phenomenal concepts strategy provides no support for type-B materialism, and provides no grounds for rejecting arguments from the epistemic gap to an ontological gap.\"

And, on the brute-identity escape (line ~885):
> \"if a type-B materialist accepts an explanatorily primitive identity between certain physical/functional properties and phenomenal properties, they might also accept an explanatorily primitive identity between certain physical/functional properties and the properties of phenomenal concepts.\"

**Consequence:** Outcome (a) — rejection-defensible-as-knock-down — is **dead on arrival.** The strongest published anti-PCS argument explicitly refuses to refute type-B; it refutes only PCS-as-support-for-type-B. A type-B physicalist who drops PCS and accepts brute identity (Loar's own endorsed position) survives the dilemma untouched. Confidence: **high** (primary source, verbatim).

**Fact 2 — Chalmers' founding definition of PCS (line ~40):**
> \"phenomenal concepts — our concepts of conscious states — have a certain special nature.\"

PCS is officially scoped, by its own most authoritative statement, to *concepts of conscious states*. Whether the strategy extends to *intentional act-concepts* such as \"sorting-from-a-standpoint\" is precisely the disputed question. Chalmers does not settle it; Loar's recognitional account is not obviously restricted to qualia. This is the hinge on which the reframe's success/failure for the thick index turns — and it is **contested, not closed.**

## III. Why this is SHRINKS-TO-MEMBRANE, not REFRAME-NEUTRAL

The reframe-auditor and both type-B attacks converge on a point the draft underweights, and the corpus's own experiment confirms it decisively:

**The relocation works only by identifying the seam-gap with the hard-problem gap. The reframe works only by severing that identification. They are mutually exclusive.** Once you grant (to win type-B-neutrality) that PCS reaches qualia-concepts but not deontic/structural gaps, you have not made the *relocation* type-B-neutral — you have replaced it with two *different* gaps: a **deontic** gap (Pigden, partially anticipated per `00-CONTRIBUTION.md` by ABLP/von Wright/Searle) and a **structural** gap (thin-index occurrence-particularity). Neither is a relocation of the *phenomenal* explanatory gap (Nagel 1974, Levine 1983).

**The compounding corpus fact (`REMAINDER-LAW-TEST.md` §3):** the Law returns **REDESCRIPTION** — not RESULT — on the forced-seam claim itself (C7, the exact forcing target), via clause (ii): under P1, non-zero occupancy is entailed by the bare existence of S, so the seam claim is **P1 restated, not a Law-earned result.** So even setting type-B entirely aside, the thesis's own demarcation instrument does not certify the relocation as a result. The type-B-neutral results the Law *can* certify (Bell, joint-force) are not the relocation the thesis wants; the relocation the thesis wants is returned as redescription.

Net: *what is type-B-neutral is not the relocation; what is the relocation is neither type-B-neutral nor Law-certifiable.* The reframe produces a contribution that is (i) the partially-anticipated membrane framing, (ii) the modest thin-index seam, (iii) a working filter for provable relational non-entailments. The distinctive novel claim is exactly the type-B-dependent, undischarged part. That is the shrink-to-membrane — named honestly, not catastrophic, but real.

## IV. The three outcomes, scored

- **(a) Rejection-defensible:** **NO.** The hard problem is unsettled. Chalmers explicitly disclaims refuting type-B; brute identity survives the dilemma. Rejecting type-B is **declarable, not provable.** Confidence the rejection is a knock-down: **low.**
- **(b) Reframe-type-B-neutral:** **PARTIAL.** Clean for membrane + thin seam + provable certifications. Fails for the relocation core. The domain-exclusion argument that saves the membrane (PCS cannot reach a vocabulary-type deontic gap) is *asserted, not argued* for the thick index — and there it is the disputed question, because Loar's recognitional account plausibly reaches intentional act-concepts.
- **(c) Declared-premise:** **ALREADY OPERATIVE, under-bounded.** `00-CONTRIBUTION.md` condition (2) declares the dependency but too broadly (\"every result the Remainder Law certifies\"), which is FALSE for Pigden-grounded and provability-grounded results. The fix is precision, mapping the condition onto the thick-index joint.

**Recommended: (b)+(c), with the shrink printed.** Split the declaration; designate the membrane, thin seam, and provable certifications type-B-independent; confine the condition to the thick-index Keystone Conclusion and the relocated-gap certifications; name the conditionality *inside* the originality statement; address the brute-identity escape on the record; and either argue (not assert) the thick-index domain exclusion or concede the core is type-B-dependent.

## V. The honest one-paragraph status for the dissertation to print

> The membrane framing (is/ought = authn/authz, on Pigden's autonomy of the deontic) and the thin-index structural seam (a located non-zero token event occurs wherever conferral occurs; the only 0 is cessation) are **unconditional** results — the former partially anticipated and of moderate novelty, the latter novel but modest. The Remainder Law, post-revision, is a working demarcation filter for **provable** relational non-entailments (Bell, joint-force), and these certifications are type-B-independent. The thesis's distinctive claim — that the explanatory gap is *forced at the conferral-seam* as the same irreducibility the hard problem of consciousness names — is **conditional on rejecting type-B physicalism**, rests on the **undischarged thick-index** reading of P1, and is **not certified as a RESULT by the Remainder Law** (which returns REDESCRIPTION on it via clause (ii)). The rejection of type-B is **declared and defended dialectically, not proven**; the hard problem is genuinely unsettled, and the strongest anti-PCS argument (Chalmers 2006) explicitly establishes only that the phenomenal concept strategy gives type-B no support — not that type-B is false. A committee concedes this conditional claim because it is printed at the exact joint where it bites; it would fail an unconditional one that hid it.
