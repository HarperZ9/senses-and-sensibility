## Circularity Audit: P1 Defense

**Lens applied:** Does the bridge argument earn "occupied-at-an-index" from premises the opponent already grants, or does the index enter as a covert definition?

**Verdict: survives at the conventional level; does not presuppose the index.**

### The pinch point

The single step to audit is the inference in Step 8 / the critical-step summary: from "a denial of P1 occurs as a propositional act, prasanga, or evaluative discrimination" to "an index is occupied." The circularity worry is that "perspectival determinacy" — the load-bearing bridge concept — might be a disguised synonym for "indexed at a standpoint," making the move definitional rather than earned.

### Why it is not circular

The defense does not begin by asserting "there is an index" and then conclude "therefore there is an index." It begins from characterizations of D that the Madhyamika opponent grants from within their own framework:

1. D is not random physical noise — the Prasangika debater presents D as a philosophical act with propositional content.
2. A prasanga selects certain consequences as damaging — the operator of the prasanga distinguishes damaging from non-damaging, acceptable from unacceptable.
3. The goer/going analysis (MMK ch. 2), applied by the Madhyamika themselves, leaves the directedness of motion intact as a relational property even after the substantial goer is dissolved.

From these three concessions — all extracted from the opponent's own position — the defense infers that any act with determinate propositional or evaluative form is enacted as this-partition-and-not-its-complement. That structural claim about what propositional form IS is not equivalent to assuming a standpoint-substance. The index enters as a relational perspectival property of the act's form, derived from the opponent's own descriptions, not inserted as a premise.

### The genuine ceiling

The defense correctly identifies where the retorsion loses grip: the enacting silence. A Madhyamika who abandons discourse entirely — entering non-propositional, non-evaluative cessation — produces no act with propositional or evaluative form. Against that move, the three concessions above are not in play, and the retorsion has nothing to grab. This is not a case where the defense presupposes the index and is therefore circular; it is a case where the retorsion's scope is genuinely limited to interlocutors who remain in discourse.

### Narrow survival condition

The defense survives the circularity test on the condition that the opponent, in performing their denial, grants propositional or evaluative character to that denial. The moment they grant this — which any practicing debater must — the index is earned, not assumed. The defense is independent of the index as a premise; it is dependent on the opponent's own characterization of their act as its input.

**Level actually reached: conventional (samvrti).** The inference is not circular, but it does not extend to the paramartha level, where sunyata-sunyata dissolves even perspectival determinacy and where enacted silence is a coherent exit the retorsion cannot follow.
