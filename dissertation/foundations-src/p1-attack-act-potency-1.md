## Verdict: P1 does not survive the Prasangika lens

The defense fails to establish the bridge independently of the contested asymmetry premise. The level claimed (conventional) is not reached because the conventional forcing move is not independent of P1.

### Where the bridge breaks

The critical step runs: actualization = determination = carving off alternatives = occupying the THIS-side of a THIS/NOT-THAT contrast = occupying a contrast-vertex (index).

Every equals sign after "determination" depends on determination having a directed structure — a THIS-side that the act arrives at. The act/potency framework (Aristotle Theta, Aquinas De Potentia) encodes this directedness as primitive: potency is the not-yet-THIS-nor-THAT state; actualization is the collapse TO a specific determination. The asymmetry is built in from the start.

The Prasangika denies exactly this structure. Pratityasamutpada is not potency-to-act. There is no privileged direction of realization in dependent arising; there is only the web of mutual conditions. The defense in its own self-audit correctly identifies this as "framework-dependent" — the step is earned against an Aristotelian but not against the Madhyamika who rejects directed collapse as a primitive. That diagnosis is correct and is fatal at the level of the main argument.

### MMK ch.2 applied to "occupation"

The goer/going analysis targets locatability. Where is the occupation of the contrast-vertex? Not in the pre-act state (potency — no THIS yet, so no vertex to occupy). Not in the completed act (the act has already occurred; the occupying is finished; we cannot retrieve the moment of indexing from the result). Not in the act-in-progress as a third thing separable from both. The defense explicitly says the index is "the address of the carving." Nagarjuna's move is to ask for the address of the address: the locatability claim generates regress without a primitive THIS-position — and that primitive is what is under dispute.

### Why the conventional forcing move (Step 7) fails independently

Step 7 is the defense's strongest move. Candrakirti does grant conventional validity to discriminative acts, and the Prasangika's own denial-act is conventionally real. The defense argues: even the denier's enacting act instantiates a contrast-vertex at samvrti, forcing a concession.

This fails for two reasons. First, VV is Nagarjuna's answer: the denial is a prasanga, not a thesis. The Prasangika does not occupy a THIS-side in the act of denying — they draw consequences from the opponent's premises. The prasanga form is specifically designed to avoid locating the speaker at a contrast-vertex. Second, and more precisely: what samvrti-satya licenses is nominal designation — conventional usage treats acts as discriminative. That is different from vindicating the act/potency structural claim that discriminative acts occupy a contrast-vertex. The defense imports the Aristotelian architecture into the conventional level; samvrti does not license that architecture, only the nominal act. The forcing move therefore assumes that the denier's enacting act has the THIS-side structure — which is P1 applied to a particular act — rather than deriving it.

### Sunyata-sunyata closes the samvrti escape

The two-truths framework does not privilege samvrti as the level at which the contrast-vertex is structurally present. Emptiness is itself empty: the conventional level is also without svabhava. The defense's boundary ("FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY is the honest ceiling") would be correct if the conventional level licensed the structural claim. It does not. It licenses the act's nominal reality, not its act/potency architecture.

### What the defense actually achieves

The defense establishes a coherent internal derivation within the Aristotelian-Thomist framework. Against any interlocutor who accepts directed collapse as the structure of actualization, the index is earned. That is a real result with content — it is not merely question-begging in the trivial sense. But it is framework-internal, and the Prasangika's entire position is the rejection of the framework's asymmetry primitive. The defense needed an argument that the symmetric-web reading of arising is itself incoherent — that even ownerless dependent arising requires a THIS-side — and no such argument is supplied. The closest candidate is Step 7, which fails as shown above.

### Level actually reached

None. The defense does not reach even the conventional level against this lens because the conventional forcing move presupposes P1 rather than deriving it. The seam is pratityasamutpada under an Aristotelian description.
