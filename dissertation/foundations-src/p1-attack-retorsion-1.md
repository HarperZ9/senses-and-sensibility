## Prasangika Verdict on P1 / "Conferral is Occupation"

### What the defense earns

The retorsion is genuinely effective at the **conventional (samvrti) level**. A Madhyamika who is arguing — adducing prasangas, selecting consequences as damaging, framing a discourse — performs acts that, described within the conventional conceptual scheme, are indexed discriminations. The retorsive point stands there: from within discourse, the denial of P1 cannot coherently claim that no index is occupied, because the discourse itself conventionally occupies one. The forced seam (either conferral is occurring or it has wholly ceased) holds at samvrti.

### Where it breaks

**The perspectival-determinacy move is the fracture point.**

The defense distinguishes (a) the substantial standpoint-substance (dissolved by MMK ch.2) from (b) the perspectival determinacy of the act (claimed to survive dissolution as a "relational but real" property). The entire weight of the bridge inference rests on (b): the step from "has determinate discriminatory form" to "occupies an index" requires that perspectival determinacy be a feature the act carries — not merely a conventional label applied to it from a conceptual scheme.

But Madhyamaka denies this middle ground exists. The MMK ch.2 analysis does not leave a residue of intrinsic relational perspectival character once the substantial substrate is dissolved. Candrakirti in the Prasannapada is explicit: the apparent perspectival features of dependently arisen events are themselves conventionally designated relative to conceptual schemes; they are not built-in structural features of the events. To assert that the sorting act carries perspectival determinacy as a robust feature of its own structure is to treat that feature as non-relational — own-being under a relational description. That is svabhava reintroduced, precisely what the defense disavows.

The dilemma therefore reasserts itself at the critical step:

- **THIN reading:** "perspectivally determinate" means "conventionally described as directed within a conceptual framework." This is all that dependently arisen events carry. The index is a conventional label. The seam is real at samvrti but does no new metaphysical work — pratityasamutpada renamed.

- **THICK reading:** "perspectivally determinate" means the act has, from its own side, the character of being THIS discrimination and not its complement. This would do the required modal/metaphysical work. But it is a form of intrinsic character — svabhava lite. The emptiness of dependently arisen events (and sunyata-sunyata: the emptiness of emptiness) forecloses this.

### The vitanda treatment

The defense's handling of vitanda (Step 5) has a circularity the defense does not fully acknowledge. The inference that a prasanga-performance is itself a discriminatory act indexed at a standpoint is valid only if perspectival determinacy survives dissolution as a real feature of acts — i.e., only if P1 is already in force. The retorsion runs: D is a conferral act (by P1), therefore D is indexed (by P1). The critical step is P1 itself. Against the Prasangika interlocutor, this is precisely the step that needs independent support, and the defense supplies it only by asserting perspectival determinacy as a structural feature of acts — which is the disputed premise.

### What the defense cannot reach

At the **ultimate (paramartha) level**: sunyata-sunyata dissolves even "perspectival determinacy" and "discriminatory form" as conceptual constructions with no intrinsic reality. The defense correctly acknowledges this ceiling. The Madhyamika who enters non-conceptual awareness — or who simply refuses to grant that the prasanga-performance carries any intrinsic perspectival character — has not been defeated by the retorsion.

More importantly: the defense does not demonstrate the **incoherence** of indexed-at-nothing occurrence without already assuming the index. It demonstrates the conventional incoherence of *claiming, within discourse*, that no index is occupied. These are different claims. The first would win P1 as a metaphysical result. The second wins only the samvrti point that the Madhyamika concedes anyway.

### Final assessment

**Survives: yes, at the conventional level.**
The retorsion secures that any enacted denial of P1 — any argumentative, therapeutic, or evaluative performance — is conventionally indexed. The forced seam holds for any Madhyamika operating within discourse.

**But does not reach ultimate:** The defense's honest ceiling is FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY. It does not prove the incoherence of ownerless occurrence in a sense stronger than conventional designation. The contribution — that the seam is real at samvrti, and that the enacted denial occupies the index it denies — is a genuine bounded result. It is not nothing. But it is not the ultimate forcing the dissertation may need if its downstream modal claims require stronger than conventional grounding.

**Presupposes verdict: presupposes** — the critical bridge (perspectival determinacy as a robust feature of acts, surviving dissolution of the substantial self) is precisely the claim the Prasangika denies, so the inference against the Prasangika interlocutor begs the question at the single load-bearing step.
