# Defense of P1 — DEFLATIONIST-INTERNAL Strategy

## The Claim and Its Target

P1 states: any act of conferral — sorting A from not-A, meaning, designating, applying a convention — is performed AS THIS DISCRIMINATION AND NOT ITS NEGATION, indexed at a standpoint. An occurrence indexed at nothing is no occurrence.

The DEFLATIONIST-INTERNAL strategy argues that even a bare physical event, on the deflationist's own account of what it is for a physical event to occur, already carries the only index P1 needs. The argument does not invoke a thick standpoint, an agent, or intentionality. It draws only on what the deflationist herself must commit to when she grants that an event occurs.

## The Bridge Argument (Step by Step)

### Step 1 — The Type/Token Constraint on Occurrences

Peirce's original formulation (1906), as reported by Wetzel (SEP "Types and Tokens," 2025), draws the line exactly where the argument needs it: a Type "does not exist; it only determines things that do exist." A Token is "a Single event which happens once and whose identity is limited to that one happening... significant only as occurring just when and where it does."

This is a deflationist commitment, not a thick metaphysical addition. The deflationist grants that events occur. But events, when they occur, occur as tokens. The type — the abstract discriminating act — "does not exist" in Peirce's formulation; only its tokens exist. Every token exists only as occurring just when and where it does. The step from "an event occurs" to "a token occurs at a spatiotemporal location" is therefore built into the deflationist's own account of what occurrence is. No standpoint, owner, or intentional structure is added.

### Step 2 — Spatiotemporal Individuation Gives the Minimum Index

The SEP "Tropes" article (Maurin 2025, section 2.3) documents that spatiotemporal individuation (SI) is the main deflationist account of event-token numerical identity: for any two exactly resembling tropes/events a and b, a ≠ b iff a is at non-zero spatiotemporal distance from b (Campbell 1990: 53–54; Schaffer 2001: 249).

This is thin. It does not require an agent, a perspective, or an intentional viewpoint. It requires only that the event have a spatiotemporal location — which is precisely what the deflationist must grant when she says that a physical event occurs. To be a token is to be located. To be located is to occupy an index. The index is not imported from outside; it is constitutive of what the deflationist means by "this event token."

On the Kim property-exemplification view (SEP "Events"): an event is an object's exemplifying a property at a time. The time-constituent is part of the event's structure, not a subsequent label. A conferral-event is therefore: [this process] exemplifying [the property of sorting A from not-A] at [time t]. The index (t, location) is a constitutive component, not an inference.

### Step 3 — Applying This to Conferral

If conferral occurs, it occurs as a token. If it occurs as a token, it is numerically distinct from (a) its negation — no-conferral at that location — and (b) other conferral-tokens at other locations. Its numerical distinctness from its negation at location L is exactly what SI delivers: conferral-token and no-conferral-token at L differ in their constitutive property (sorting vs. not-sorting), making them different events at the same index, or (equivalently) making the conferral-token the unique occupant of the (location L, property: sorting A from not-A) slot.

This is P1's requirement: the conferral occurs AS THIS DISCRIMINATION AND NOT ITS NEGATION. The negation is excluded not by an agentive act of distinguishing but by the structure of token individuation: a token cannot simultaneously instantiate sorting-A-from-not-A and not-sorting-A-from-not-A at the same spatiotemporal location. The law of non-contradiction, at the level of event-token individuation, does this work without adding any thick standpoint.

### Step 4 — "Conferral with Residue 0" Is Incoherent

The thesis's seam requires that "conferral with a 0 residue" — conferral occurring at no index — is p AND NOT-p. The argument delivers this at the token level: a type without a token "does not exist" (Peirce). A conferral-type floating free of any token is not a conferral that is occurring at all; it is the type, which only determines things that exist. So if what we are asking is whether conferral is presently occurring (as opposed to whether the type is abstractly instantiable), the answer is yes iff some token of that type is occurring at some index. There is no third option: an occurring that is tokenless is no occurring. The C/Z disjunction (conferral occurring vs. conferral wholly ceased) is exhaustive at the token level.

## The Critical Step and Honest Audit

**The critical inference:** "occurs" (bare physical event) → "occurs at a determinate spatiotemporal index" (occupied-at-index).

This inference has two links:

1. Occurrence = tokening (a Peircean commitment: to occur is to be a token, individuated by its occurrence just when and where it does).
2. Tokening = spatiotemporal individuation (the SI account: to be a token is to be at a spatiotemporal index that distinguishes it from other tokens).

Both links are drawn from the deflationist's own commitments. Neither presupposes a thick standpoint.

**Does the step presuppose the index or earn it?**

Against the deflationist/Humean: it earns the index. The deflationist herself endorses SI or some equivalent account of event-token individuation. The argument uses only her commitments. It does not beg the question against her.

Against the Prasangika Madhyamika: the result is more complicated, and honesty requires reporting it plainly.

The Prasangika grants pratityasamutpada — dependent arising — which already includes spatiotemporal location as a dependent condition of any arising. The Nagarjuna SEP (Westerhoff 2026) confirms that the MMK chapter 2 argument is about showing that the mover/motion distinction does not carve at intrinsically real joints ("no ready-made world"), not about denying that events have spatiotemporal extent. The Prasangika can say: yes, every event token is dependently located spatiotemporally — that is exactly what pratityasamutpada says. The spatiotemporal index is a conventional (samvrti) fact, fully granted. But no ultimate (paramaric) residue follows: the index is itself empty of svabhava, dependently arisen, not self-standing.

So the Prasangika's reply to the argument is: you have correctly described the conventional level. Conferral, when conventionally occurring, is conventionally indexed at a spatiotemporal location. But this is already in the bag — it is pratityasamutpada under a different label. The argument does not establish an index that the Madhyamika must resist at the ultimate level.

**Honest verdict on the disjunction:** The C/Z seam holds at the conventional level. The exhaustion of the disjunction (conferral occurring vs. conferral wholly ceased) is genuine at samvrti: either a token is occurring at this index or no token is. The Prasangika concedes this. What she denies is that this conventional occupation of an index carries any ultimate significance — any svabhava — and the argument gives her no reason to deny that denial. The "conferral with 0 residue" incoherence is an incoherence at the conventional level (a type without a token does not exist as an occurrence), which the Prasangika grants. She just notes that conventional incoherence and ultimate vacuity are compatible: the token's conventional existence is itself empty.

## Which Horn the Argument Lands On

The argument lands on the **trivial spatiotemporal horn** — stated honestly:

The index established is the spatiotemporal index. Every physical event has one, by the deflationist's own account. The argument is not trivial in the derogatory sense — it is genuine and the reasoning is tight — but it is trivial in the specific sense that it does not add any modal or metaphysical work over what the Prasangika already grants when she affirms pratityasamutpada. The seam (C vs. Z) is real at the conventional level. The index is real at the conventional level. The claim that "conferral with 0 residue is p AND NOT-p" is genuine at the conventional level: it is incoherent to affirm that a token-type is occurring while denying that any token of that type is occurring.

But this is samvrti, not paramartha. The Prasangika wins at the ultimate level: the index is itself empty. The argument forces an honest concession — **the contribution of the DEFLATIONIST-INTERNAL strategy is to establish FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY, not PROVEN.**

---

## Level Reached

**Conventional (samvrti).** The argument forces an index at the conventional level: a conferral-token, if occurring, is necessarily occupying a spatiotemporal index, and "conferral with 0 residue" is conventionally incoherent. The Prasangika who concedes pratityasamutpada has already conceded this much — but she grants it rather than having it forced on her, since it is her own doctrine. The seam holds at samvrti. The argument does not defeat the Prasangika at the ultimate level.

---

## Self-Grade: Moderate

The argument is tight within its terms. It draws no fabricated citations, uses only deflationist commitments that the deflationist herself cannot refuse, and the critical inference is derivable from established accounts of event-token individuation (Peirce, Kim, SI trope theory). It does what the strategy promises: it does not smuggle a thick standpoint; it argues from the thin reading itself.

But its ceiling is real. The Prasangika's reply — "this is conventional; I concede it; it does not establish ultimate residue" — is not a stretch. It follows directly from the two-truths doctrine as documented in the SEP entry. The argument gives the thesis FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY, which is a genuine philosophical result (the seam holds at samvrti, the C/Z exhaustion is not merely stipulated), but it does not transcend the membrane between conventional and ultimate that a full-strength P1 proof would require.

A defense claiming PROVEN (ultimate) from this argument would be dishonest. FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY is the honest ceiling, and this argument reaches it by a clean route.
