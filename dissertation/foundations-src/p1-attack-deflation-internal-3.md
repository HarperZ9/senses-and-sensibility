## Circularity Audit: P1 Defense

**Lens applied:** Does the bridge demonstrate the incoherence of "indexed-at-nothing occurrence" without already assuming the index?

**Verdict: the defense survives the circularity test, with ceiling at conventional.**

### Where the index enters

The index enters at the junction of Links (1) and (2):

- Link (1): occurrence = tokening. Source: Peirce's type/token distinction. The deflationist already accepts this — it is a precondition of her own account of what exists. No index appears here; only the claim that events, if they occur at all, occur as tokens.

- Link (2): tokening = spatiotemporal individuation (SI). Source: Campbell (1990), Schaffer (2001), endorsed by the deflationist as her own account of what makes concrete particulars numerically distinct. The spatiotemporal position is not appended from outside; it is extracted from the SI principle the deflationist already uses.

The index therefore enters as a derived consequence of two commitments the opponent already holds, not as a starting assumption. This is the circularity test's pass condition.

### Why this is not circular

Presupposition would mean: to run the argument at all, you must already have the index in hand. That is not what happens here. The argument runs from (occurrence) to (token) to (spatiotemporally individuated) using only the deflationist's own machinery. She cannot reject the conclusion without rejecting SI individuation, which is her own account of numerical distinctness. The index is earned from inside her ontology.

### The Prasangika scope objection is not a circularity charge

The Prasangika grants conventional (samvrti) events, including their spatiotemporal extent under pratityasamutpada. Her objection is that the index established is only conventional — it carries no ultimate (paramartha) residue, so it does not establish svabhava. This is a scope limitation on what the argument proves, not evidence that the argument presupposes what it is trying to prove. These are different failure modes. The circularity auditor's mandate is the presupposition test; the Prasangika's objection passes that test while limiting the ceiling.

### What the defense actually establishes

At the conventional level: "conferral with residue 0" is incoherent because a type without a token does not exist (Peirce), and a token without a spatiotemporal index is not a token (SI). The C/Z disjunction holds at samvrti. The seam is real at this level.

At the ultimate level: the Prasangika concedes nothing. Spatiotemporal individuation is itself empty of svabhava. The defense does not reach paramartha, and it does not claim to — the defender's own self-audit correctly identifies the ceiling as conventional.
