## Verdict: Does not survive — presupposes the index

### Lens applied

Prasangika Madhyamaka as developed by Nagarjuna (MMK, VV) and Candrakirti (Madhyamakavatara). Three weapons: (a) the goer/going reductio (MMK ch. 2); (b) "I have no thesis" — the VV's refusal to grant that any positive philosophical assertion has svabhava-grounded standing; (c) sunyata-sunyata — the emptiness of emptiness itself as a brake on self-exempting privileged claims.

---

### What the defense actually does

The bridge has five steps, but the critical inference is concentrated in links (1) and (2):

- **Link (1):** occurrence = tokening (Peirce, SEP "Types and Tokens")
- **Link (2):** tokening = spatiotemporal individuation (SI, Campbell 1990, Schaffer 2001)
- **Conclusion:** occurrence = occupied-at-an-index

The defense presents this as deriving the index from commitments the deflationist already holds, without importing a thick standpoint or svabhava. The self-audit calls this "borderline" and concedes the result holds only conventionally.

---

### The Prasangika attack

**On Link (1) — the goer/going reductio.**

MMK 2.1: "That which has gone is not going. That which has not gone is not going. Apart from that which has gone and that which has not gone, going is not understood." The point is not that motion does not occur; the Prasangika grants dependent arising, which includes spatiotemporal change. The point is that you cannot LOCATE the going in any fixed locus — not in the goer, not in the going, not in their conjunction — because each attempt presupposes a stable referent from which the relation is individuated. The goer/going analysis is a template for all "X occurs as THIS X and not its negation" claims.

Link (1) invokes Peirce's semantic formulation: a token is "significant only as occurring just when and where it does." But to the Prasangika, this formulation is itself a conventional posit — a rule internal to the type/token vocabulary. The claim that "to occur is to be a token" is not a framework-neutral observation about events; it is what you get when you APPLY the type/token framework. That framework already encodes indexedness as part of what "token" means. So Link (1) does not derive the index; it unpacks a definition that already contains it. This is the MMK ch. 2 structure in miniature: the index is not found in the bare event prior to the vocabulary; it is installed by the vocabulary.

**On Link (2) — SI as svabhava reasoning.**

The SI thesis (a = b iff same spatiotemporal position; a ≠ b iff at non-zero distance) is a substantive philosophical account of what makes concrete particulars numerically distinct. Accepting SI as a genuine individuation principle — not merely a conventional description that functions within a linguistic framework — amounts to treating spatiotemporal coordinates as real, intrinsic individuation-makers: svabhava for events. The VV's "I have no thesis" move applies here: the Prasangika does not assert SI and does not assert its negation. She uses spatiotemporal descriptions conventionally (they are dependent designations, part of the everyday vocabulary), but she denies that any description picks out a real individuating essence that is the "what makes this event THIS event" in a metaphysically thick sense.

The defense is aware of this and tries to defuse it by calling the move "thin." But "thin" is doing heavy work here. Either SI is merely a conventional redescription of dependent arising (in which case it does not ground the seam against the Prasangika, because she already has dependent arising and it does not generate the C/Z disjunction in the required form), or SI is a real account of what individuates events (in which case it is svabhava reasoning, and the Prasangika's reductio applies). The defense cannot have both.

**On sunyata-sunyata — the self-exemption block.**

The defense attempts to establish that "conferral with 0 residue" is incoherent by pointing to the type/token structure: a type without a token does not exist. But sunyata-sunyata closes this. The type/token distinction is itself empty — it arises dependently, within a conceptual framework, and has no intrinsic metaphysical authority. "Token does not exist without spatiotemporal index" is true inside the type/token vocabulary. It is not a fixed joint in the structure of what can coherently occur. The Prasangika can grant that the vocabulary functions (conventional truth) while denying that its internal structure constrains what can ultimately be the case. So the incoherence claim holds only within the vocabulary that already encodes indexedness — which is exactly what "presupposes the index" means.

**The concession that closes the case.**

The defender's own self-audit concedes: "the argument establishes something real (the C/Z seam holds at the conventional level; 'conferral with 0 residue' is incoherent because a type without a token does not exist), but it does not establish it against the Prasangika at the ultimate level." This is honest, but the consequence is under-stated. If the seam holds only conventionally, the Prasangika's reply is: granted — we have dependent arising at the conventional level, which already includes spatiotemporal location as a condition of arising. The seam is just pratityasamutpada restated in type/token vocabulary. The P1 contribution — that "occurring entails occupied-at-an-index in a sense STRONGER than is a dependently-arisen event" — is not established. What is established is exactly what dependent arising already delivers and no more. The modifier "stronger than" fails.

---

### What the defense does earn (conceded genuinely)

Against a Humean deflationist who accepts SI and the type/token distinction as genuine metaphysical accounts, the bridge is not question-begging. It uses the deflationist's own commitments and derives the index from them. This is real. The seam is forced at the conventional level, and the deflationist cannot escape by invoking SI while denying indexedness. That is a genuine result.

The defense also correctly identifies that the Prasangika concedes conventional indices — she does not deny that the event occurs, that it is spatiotemporally located, or that the C/Z disjunction is true under a conventional description. So the membrane identity (authn != authz :: is/ought) survives as a conventional result.

---

### Level the defense actually reaches

**Conventional (samvrti).** The defense earns the index against the Humean/deflationist interlocutor and secures the seam at the conventional level. It does not earn the index against the Prasangika at the ultimate level, and the claim that indexedness is stronger than pratityasamutpada-derived spatiotemporal location is not established. The defense reaches exactly the ceiling the self-audit named — not because the argument fails to try, but because every move it makes is a conventional unfolding of a vocabulary that already encodes what it purports to derive.
