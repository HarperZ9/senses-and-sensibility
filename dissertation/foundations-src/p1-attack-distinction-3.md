The defense does not survive the circularity audit.

The self-audit in the defense's own words says the index is "extracted from the meaning of the term 'conferral.'" That is the problem, not the solution. The single decisive question is whether the extraction is from neutral ground or from a meaning that already contains the conclusion. Step 1 defines conferral as "assigning A rather than not-A" — a definition that builds an interior (the perspective from which A and not-A are ranged against one another) into the concept before the formal apparatus is applied. The Shannon/Spencer-Brown machinery in Steps 2 through 4 is then valid conditional on that definition, but it does not vindicate the definition against someone who disputes it.

The Madhyamika's move is available here: they can deny the definition at Step 1 without incoherence on their own terms. Their position is that discriminative acts arise dependently and ownerlessly — pratityasamutpada without svabhava, including without the svabhava of interiority. Granting "conferral occurs" does not compel acceptance that the occurrence has the structure "A rather than not-A from a here," because that structure is exactly what the ownerless-occurrence reply denies. The defense has not shown that denying the structure is incoherent; it has shown that denying the structure while accepting the definition is incoherent. That is circular.

The conventional-level ceiling acknowledged in the defense's own self-audit makes this explicit: "within any framework where distinctions are made." The conditional "within any framework" is the concession that the argument does not work for a party who contests whether distinctions, in the indexed sense, are what is occurring. The Madhyamika is precisely that party.

FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY was the projected honest ceiling. The circularity audit finds the defense does not reach even that ceiling cleanly, because at the conventional level the defense is valid only against someone who already accepts the Step 1 definition — and that acceptance is not forced; it is the point in dispute. The level the defense actually reaches is none: the critical step presupposes the index in the definitional base, and no formal elaboration downstream can compensate for that.
