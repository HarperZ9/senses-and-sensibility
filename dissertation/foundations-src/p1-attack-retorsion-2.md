## Verdict: Survives at conventional; fails to breach thin/thick on the physicalist's terms

### The dilemma applied

**Thin horn.** Strip "enacted from a side of the partition" to what the physical story licenses without importing semantic content: a brain-event with differential causal sensitivity, spatiotemporally located at (x,t). This event is trivially "here and not there" — but that is spatiotemporal location, the very minimum that pratityasamutpada already delivers for any dependently-arisen event. If this is what "occupied at an index" means, it does zero modal work that the Buddhist framework did not already do. The seam ("either conferral is occurring or it has wholly ceased") just means "either the causal event is occurring or it is not," which the Madhyamika concedes without any standpoint residue. No advance.

**Thick horn.** The defense wants more: "to partition is to be enacted from a side" — a structural, constitutive claim about discriminatory acts as such, not merely a description of their causal profile. This is step (b), the load-bearing premise. The physicalist locates the fracture here. Step (b) is an analytic claim: it builds the index into the meaning of "conferral" (discriminatory act = act enacted from a side). Under that definition, yes — conferral analytically involves an index. But the move from "the denial is a physical event with differential causal profile" to "the denial is a conferral in this thick, constitutively-perspectival sense" is precisely the thought-to-being inference that Stroud's gap names. The defense applies the thick predicate to the physical event without supplying the license for that application from the physical story. This is not blatant circularity (the defense does not begin by assuming a substantial standpoint-bearer). It is structurally equivalent: the index is presupposed in the predicate, and the predicate is silently applied.

**On the presupposes/independent question.** The defender's self-audit claims step (b) is "a structural claim about what discriminatory directedness IS" and not question-begging. This is accurate as stated — (b) is a conceptual claim about partitioning acts, not an assumption that any particular physical event instantiates them. But the verdict "independent" would require demonstrating that the thick description is a discoverable feature of the physical event rather than a conceptual imposition from the semantic side. The defense does not supply this demonstration; it supplies only the analytic connection within the thick description. Hence: unclear — not straightforwardly presupposes, not straightforwardly independent.

### What actually survives

The conventional-level retorsion is genuine and not question-begging against the discourse-engaged Madhyamika. A practitioner adducing prasangas, selecting consequences as damaging, framing a reductio is at the samvrti level occupying a conventional standpoint and cannot coherently deny this from within that discourse. The retorsion here earns its result: the Madhyamika in dialogue is sorting (this consequence is damaging / that one is not), and that sorting is indexed at the conventional standpoint of the debater. The defense correctly identifies that the vitanda move relocates rather than eliminates the index at this level.

But under the physicalist lens, "conventional standpoint of the debater" cashes out as: the event is located here, in this causal network, with this differential sensitivity. The Madhyamika accepts this without residue — it is pratityasamutpada under a Latin coat, as the objection charges. The defense has not demonstrated that the index does modal work *beyond* the thin description that the Buddhist framework already provides.

### The defense's honest ceiling

The defense itself marks "conventional" as the claimed level, and this is where the result actually lands. The physicalist cannot break the conventional retorsion — the Madhyamika engaged in discourse is genuinely occupying a conventional index and cannot coherently deny it from within discourse. This much is conceded. What cannot be established on the physicalist's terms is that this conventional occupation involves anything thicker than spatiotemporal-causal locatedness, which means the seam's modal force (the exhaustive disjunction that generates the "forced" contribution) remains unestablished beyond the dependent-origination framework.
