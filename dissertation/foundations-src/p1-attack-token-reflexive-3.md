The circularity audit result is: the defense survives at the conventional level, non-circular.

**The decisive joint.** The denier's own move is to invoke pratityasamutpada — dependent arising — as the ownerless ground of the conferral-act. But pratityasamutpada is precisely a doctrine of conditioned, particular, located arising. The Prasangika cannot invoke it without conceding that the arising in question is a particular event, not a floating type. Once that concession is in, the token-individuation premise (drawn from Peirce/Campbell/Schaffer, entirely general, not conferral-specific) kicks in and the thin index follows without any additional conferral-specific assumption.

**What the thick index would require.** To move from "this token is spatiotemporally located" to "this token is performed FROM a perspective" requires a premise connecting causal location to intentional standpoint. That premise is not supplied. The deflationist can accept the thin result — every event is somewhere — while denying that "somewhere" constitutes a perspective in P1's sense. The defense's self-audit correctly flags this; the gap is real.

**Why the defense is nonetheless non-circular at the level it claims.** Circularity would require the index to enter as a hidden premise of the token-individuation argument itself. It does not. The individuation argument is motivated entirely by general metaphysics of particulars. The conclusion it earns (thin index) is weaker than P1's full claim, but it is genuinely earned, not assumed. The honest ceiling is FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY: the denier enacts a spatiotemporally located token in the very act of denial, indexing herself at the conventional level, and cannot coherently disown this without performing a further located act. The ultimate-level gap remains open and the Madhyamika may hold her ground there — but holding it there is not the same as defeating P1 at the conventional level where the seam actually operates.
