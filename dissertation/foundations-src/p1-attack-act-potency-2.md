## Verdict: Bridge fails against the deflationary lens; FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY via Step 7 only

### The attack

**Lens:** deflationary type-A physicalist / Humean, applying the thin/thick dilemma.

**The dilemma, precisely stated.** For the act/potency bridge to earn P1, the "contrast-vertex" must satisfy two conditions simultaneously: it must be non-trivial (not merely spatiotemporal location, otherwise every physical event has it and the C/Z disjunction does no work) AND it must be earned without presupposing the asymmetric THIS-side structure (otherwise it is question-begging). There is no reading that satisfies both.

---

### Why the bridge fails at Step 2, not merely Step 5

The defender's own presupposition self-audit correctly identifies that the critical step (Step 5: conferral → contrast-vertex) is "framework-dependent" — it earns the index relative to the Aristotelian framework, but presupposes the asymmetry against someone who rejects that framework. The physicalist audit is harsher: the framework-dependence is already question-begging at Step 2, before the critical inference even runs.

The act/potency distinction is not a metaphysically neutral bridge. Aristotelian entelecheia carries the THIS-sided asymmetry as part of its basic structure: an act is the realization of a specific nature (telos), and that specificity is inherently directed. For a type-A physicalist who has already rejected Aristotelian formal causation as a physical kind, the entire bridge medium is unavailable. Invoking act/potency to earn the contrast-vertex presupposes the very scholastic ontology in which natures, essences, and formal determinations give acts their THIS-sided character. This is not circular in the trivial sense — the bridge has genuine content — but it is question-begging against the physicalist who denies that content is real.

**The Stroud-reopening.** The thin/thick dilemma maps directly onto Stroud's gap, which reopens here inside the predicate "conferral" itself. Stroud's original target: the step from "our practices discriminate" to "the world has discriminative structure we project" is not closed by naturalist frameworks. The bridge attempts exactly this crossing: from "a physical actualization occurred" to "the actualization is as-discrimination-at-a-standpoint (contrast-vertex occupied in the thick sense)." The crossing requires a thought-to-being inference — from how the event is structured qua physical to how it is structured qua discriminative act. No physical description mandates the thick reading; the thick reading is carried in by the Aristotelian framework, not derived from the physics.

### What survives

Step 7 — the conventional forcing move — is not a product of the act/potency bridge. It runs independently: the denier's own enacting act instantiates a contrast-vertex at samvrti, conceded by the Madhyamika on conventional validity grounds. The type-A physicalist has more difficulty here than with Steps 2–5, because pragmatic self-refutation arguments operate at a level where even deflationists typically grant conventional indexing (they grant that utterances are located, that denial-acts are event-types distinct from their negations, etc.). The physicalist cannot dismiss Step 7 on thin/thick grounds alone — the pragmatic structure does not require a thought-to-being crossing of the Stroud variety.

Accordingly: **the bridge argument (Steps 1–6) fails against this lens**. The defense reaches FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY, but only via the pragmatic self-refutation of the denier (Step 7), not via the act/potency bridge. The bridge either trivializes the index or presupposes its thickness. Against a committed deflationary physicalist, this is a real ceiling: the contribution at the ultimate level is conceded (occurrence without index is just pratityasamutpada or Humean succession — neither requires a contrast-vertex in any non-trivial sense), and what is forced at the conventional level is forced by a different mechanism than the one the defense deployed.

### On the defender's presupposition self-audit

The defender's verdict of "borderline" is too generous from this lens. "Borderline" correctly identifies framework-dependence but treats the Aristotelian framework as a neutral platform that earns the result for Aristotelians, failing only for Madhyamikas who "reject the asymmetry wholesale." The physicalist attack is more damaging: the framework is not a neutral platform but is itself the contested move, because it encodes the scholastic ontology the physicalist has already burned down. The framework-dependence is therefore not a case of "earns for sympathetic interlocutors, fails for hostile ones" — it is a case of "presupposes the very ontological structure the dilemma demands the defense not presuppose."

The honest verdict is: **the critical step presupposes the index** (as the asymmetric THIS-side) by invoking a bridge medium (Aristotelian entelecheia) that carries the asymmetry as a package, making the inference question-begging against the physicalist rather than merely framework-relative in a neutral sense.
