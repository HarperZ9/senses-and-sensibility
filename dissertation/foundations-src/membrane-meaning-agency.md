## A — The Membrane in Meaning and Agency (the two test chapters)

The thesis of this section is structural, not metaphorical: the chapter on **meaning** and the chapter on **agency** describe the *same object* under two functional loads. That object is a selectively-permeable, continuously-re-spoken, inhabited seam. The four "floats-free" remainders the corpus isolated — the A/B undergoing remainder, the second-personal remainder, and the two occasionalisms — are not four loose ends. They are four *descriptions of one membrane*, each naming a property the membrane has *qua* membrane and that no inventory of the items it sorts can deliver. I argue this below and keep the falsifier in view at every step.

### 1. Meaning: intentionality is the membrane, not a relation across already-given relata

Start with the uncontroversial neuroscience, then refuse to let it close the question.

A neuron is literally a membrane that decides. The lipid bilayer separates two ionic media and maintains a resting **membrane potential** (≈ −70 mV) against entropy by active transport — the **Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase** pumping 3 Na⁺ out / 2 K⁺ in per ATP (high confidence; standard electrophysiology, Hodgkin–Huxley 1952 for the conductance account of the spike). Input that crosses **threshold** triggers an **all-or-nothing action potential**: above threshold, full spike; below, nothing. This is a clean physical 1|0 with an *exception layer* — the threshold — deciding which depolarization counts as the 1. The pump is occasionalism in voltage: stop re-spending ATP and the gradient dissipates; the distinction (inside ≠ outside) is not a stored fact but a continuously re-maintained one.

Now the philosophical claim, kept orthogonal. Intentionality — consciousness *of* something, the "aboutness" Brentano (1874) made the mark of the mental, and which Husserl built phenomenology upon (high confidence on attribution) — is not a property of either relatum taken alone. The world-state is not *about* anything; a third-person inventory of the organism's neuronal membrane-potentials is not *about* anything either. Aboutness appears only **at the seam** where an inhabited standpoint is directed onto a world. That seam is the self/world membrane: it is *neither* the self-as-substrate (a pile of firing thresholds) *nor* the world (mass and charge), and it is the only place "significance" is located. Sapolsky's determinism (*Determined*, 2023; moderate confidence on the specific framing, high on the position) is the complete account of *what tips each neuronal membrane to 1 or 0* — and precisely because it is complete at *that* level, it never reaches the organism-level membrane, which is no single neuron's threshold. The map of every sub-membrane is silent about the one membrane that bounds the whole and is *directed*.

This is where significance is **bestowed**, not found. The same physical scene is the 1s and 0s; whether it *matters*, and *to whom*, is conferred across the seam. Conferral here is the analog of `kun` / `ase` — the word that turns indifferent dynamics into significance — spoken at the membrane, not stored in either medium.

### 2. The A/B undergoing remainder is a description of the membrane

The corpus's A/B remainder: take two systems, A and B, functionally and dynamically identical down to every input-output and every internal state-transition; stipulate that A undergoes its processing (there is something it is like to be A) and B does not. No third-person inventory distinguishes them, because by construction the inventory is identical. The remainder — the *undergoing* — floats free of every catalogued 1 and 0.

The structural reading: **the undergoing is what crossing the membrane *is from the inside*, and the membrane is exactly the item the A/B-identical inventory does not list.** The inventory enumerates the states the membrane *sorts* (the neuronal 1s and 0s, the dynamics); it does not and cannot list the inhabited seam *at which* the sorting is undergone, because that seam is not one of the sorted items. So the A/B case is not evidence for a spooky extra substance. It is evidence that there is a *seam* whose mode of being is "inhabited," and that "inhabited" is not reducible to the catalog of what it separates. This keeps the claim falsifiable: it is refuted the moment someone exhibits **subjectless meaning** — aboutness or undergoing with *no* bounded standpoint, significance floating with no seam to be significant *across*. The A/B remainder is the standing report that this refutation has not arrived.

### 3. The occasionalism of meaning is the membrane re-spoken

The corpus's occasionalism of meaning: significance is **withdrawable without subtraction of fact**. Depression, anhedonia, derealization — the world's facts are untouched; what drains is that they *matter*. No proposition the patient believed becomes false; the conferral lapses.

Structurally this is the membrane property (ii): the seam is maintained *occasion by occasion*, like the Na⁺/K⁺ pump maintaining the gradient. Al-Ghazali's occasionalism (the *Tahāfut*, c. 1095; high confidence the position is his — God re-creates the world's states each instant, no necessary connection between "causes") is the theological statement of the same structure: the distinction is not a one-time fact but a continuous re-speaking, and what is *not* re-spoken simply lapses, *without anything being subtracted from the inventory of facts*. That "without subtraction of fact" is the exact diagnostic that we are looking at the membrane and not at one of the media: media-facts persist; the seam's conferral does not, because the seam is *constituted by* being re-maintained. Falsifier: if significance could be shown to be a stored fact that persists whether or not it is re-conferred — present in the inventory, subtractable only by deleting a state — the occasionalism claim, and with it the membrane reading, fails.

### 4. Agency: authorship is the membrane between determined-input and act

Now the second chapter, and the claim that it is the *same* membrane.

The agency seam sits between fully-determined input (the entire Sapolsky causal history converging on this moment) and the act that issues. Authorship is *neither* the determined input (that is upstream dynamics, the 1s and 0s of antecedent membrane-states) *nor* the bodily motion considered as mere efferent output (downstream dynamics). It is what is conferred **across** the seam between them — the corpus's "Conferred Sourcehood." The determinist account is, again, complete *at the level it operates* — every neuronal threshold tipped by prior cause — and again orthogonal, because authorship is predicated of the organism-level membrane that bounds the whole agent, which is no single tipped threshold. This is the precise structural mirror of §1: there, significance bestowed across the self/world seam; here, sourcehood bestowed across the input/act seam. Two loads, one seam-structure.

### 5. The second-personal remainder is membrane-to-membrane

"I hold YOU responsible" — the reactive-attitudes / second-personal stance (Strawson, "Freedom and Resentment," 1962; Darwall, *The Second-Person Standpoint*, 2006; high confidence on attribution) — addresses a *bounded locus* as itself a *bounded locus*. The remainder the corpus isolates: the address cannot be cashed out as a third-personal description of behavior or dispositions; "you" names not a trajectory but an *addressee*.

Structural reading: the address is **membrane-to-membrane** — one selectively-permeable inhabited seam turning toward another *as* a seam. This is why it has no third-personal paraphrase: a third-person inventory lists the items a membrane sorts, never the *standpoint* that is the membrane, so it has no term that can occupy the "you" slot. The address presupposes on the far side exactly the inhabited seam that §2's A/B remainder reported on the near side. The two remainders are the two faces of one structure — the membrane encountered from inside (undergoing) and the membrane encountered as addressed (the second person). Falsifier: **unowned agency** — a genuine instance of holding-responsible that *fully* reduces to a third-personal dispositional description with no addressee-locus remaining — would collapse this and show the "you" was never membrane-deep.

### 6. The occasionalism of agency is the membrane drained and re-maintained

Authorship **drains and returns without subtraction of dynamical fact**. In addiction and in dissociation the dynamics are fully present — limbs move, decisions issue, the causal chain is unbroken — yet sourcehood is *absent*: the act happens *to* the agent rather than *from* her, and clinically this is reported as exactly such ("compulsion," "it wasn't me," depersonalization; moderate confidence on the clinical phenomenology being well-attested, high on its being reported). Nothing is missing from the inventory of motions and prior causes. What lapses is the conferral *across* the seam — sourcehood not re-spoken this occasion.

This is property (ii) again, and it is the *same* property as §3's occasionalism of meaning: in both, what withdraws withdraws **without subtracting any fact from either medium**, which is the signature of the seam rather than the media. The Na⁺/K⁺-pump analogy is exact and literal at the substrate: cut the ATP and the gradient lapses though every ion is still present and accounted for. Drain the conferral and authorship lapses though every motion is still present and accounted for. Falsifier: **conferral-from-nowhere** — sourcehood (or significance) appearing with *no* inhabited seam to be conferred across, or persisting as a stored, subtractable fact independent of re-speaking — refutes the occasionalism reading in either chapter.

### 7. The two chapters are one membrane; the four remainders are its four faces

Collect the result. There is one structure with three defining properties — (i) it is neither of the two media it divides, the irreducible third; (ii) it is continuously re-spoken, lapsing when not maintained; (iii) it is where conferral happens, turning 0 into 1, indifference into significance, determined-input into authored act. The two chapters apply this structure under two loads:

| | **Meaning chapter** | **Agency chapter** |
|---|---|---|
| The two media (the 1s and 0s) | self-as-substrate / world | determined-input / bodily output |
| Property (i): irreducible third | aboutness, bestowed across the seam | sourcehood, bestowed across the seam |
| Inhabited-from-inside remainder | **A/B undergoing** (§2) | — |
| Membrane-as-addressed remainder | — | **second-personal "you"** (§5) |
| Property (ii): occasionalism | **significance withdrawable w/o subtracting fact** (§3) | **authorship drains/returns w/o subtracting fact** (§6) |

The four floats-free components are not residue the theory failed to absorb. Each is a *description of the membrane*: the A/B undergoing is the membrane named from the inside; the second-personal "you" is the membrane named as addressed; the two occasionalisms are the membrane named as re-spoken, once for significance and once for sourcehood. They float free of the inventory of 1s and 0s **for one reason**: the membrane is not one of the items it sorts, and an inventory lists only sorted items. That is not a totalizing claim that "all is membrane." It is the narrow claim that *these four specific, independently-argued remainders share one structure*, and the claim is falsifiable by any one of: subjectless meaning (kills §2), unowned agency (kills §5), conferral-from-nowhere or stored-subtractable conferral (kills §3/§6), or a demonstration that any remainder in fact *reduces* to the substrate inventory (kills the whole reading). Until one of those arrives, the most economical description of the two passed chapters is that they saw the same selectively-permeable, re-spoken, inhabited seam twice — once as the place meaning is conferred, once as the place authorship is.

**Confidence on the section's load-bearing factual citations:** Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase stoichiometry and all-or-nothing action-potential/threshold physiology — high. Brentano-on-intentionality, Husserl, al-Ghazali's occasionalism, Strawson 1962, Darwall 2006 — high on attribution. Sapolsky's determinist position — high; specific framing — moderate. The membrane *reading itself* is an interpretive thesis, not an empirical finding: it earns its keep only by being falsifiable in the four ways named, and I have stated those rather than asserting the thesis as established fact.
