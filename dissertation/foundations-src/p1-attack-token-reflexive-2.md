## Verdict: FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY — defense does not survive as a proof of P1's thick claim

### What the defense earns (conceded without reservation)

The token-reflexive bridge is not circular. The premise "tokens require spatiotemporal individuation" is sourced from a general metaphysics of particular events (Peirce 1906; Campbell 1990; Schaffer 2001; Varzi SEP "Events" 2025) that applies independently of anything specific to conferral. The denier's own assertion is a token event. It is spatiotemporally located. She cannot coherently deny this without performing a further located token. The thin index — occurrence entails spatiotemporal locatedness — is established without presupposition. That much survives.

The Madhyamika cannot honestly claim the located-token result is wrong, because pratityasamutpada itself places arising-events within causal-spatiotemporal dependence. The locatedness concession is not a defeat for Madhyamaka; it is a restatement of dependent arising, which is precisely the problem.

### Where the defense fails under this lens

P1's doing word is "standpoint," not "location." The forcing argument requires that the index is a PERSPECTIVE from which sorting is performed as THIS sorting — not merely a coordinate that individuates one physical event from another. The defense's steps 1–3 establish:

> occurrence → token → spatiotemporal position

They do not establish:

> spatiotemporal position → perspective-pole of the discrimination

The gap is not cosmetic. Consider a thermostat: its state-transition at t, l is a token event, spatiotemporally individuated, numerically distinct from yesterday's transition, satisfying every condition in steps 1–3. No one reads off from that description a "standpoint," a perspective from which the threshold-crossing is performed AS THIS discrimination and not its negation. The type-A physicalist's commitment is precisely that intentional/normative vocabulary — sorting-as-such, perspective, discrimination-as-such — is not entailed by any purely physical description of a token event. To infer "standpoint" from "spatiotemporal location" is to cross from physical to intentional predication. That crossing is a thought->being inference, and Stroud's gap reopens inside the predicate "conferral."

### The thin/thick dilemma lands

The defense's own self-audit identifies this joint precisely:

> "the move from 'position individuates tokens' to 'this position is a standpoint-index in the sense P1 requires' is not identical to the metaphysical claim about spatiotemporal individuation."

This is the dilemma fully instantiated:

- **Thin reading**: index = spatiotemporal location. Derivation is clean and non-circular. But "standpoint" just means "causal location," which the Madhyamika already grants under pratityasamutpada. P1 is vacuously true, does no modal work, and the seam — "conferral with 0 residue is p AND NOT-p" — loses its bite. Every dependent arising is "indexed" in this sense; the disjunction (C) vs. (Z) collapses into dependent arising regardless of whether a standpoint in the discriminative sense is present.

- **Thick reading**: index = perspective-pole of the discrimination. This is what P1 needs. But the inference from token-individuation to perspective-pole is not delivered by Peirce, Campbell, or Schaffer. It requires additional premises — phenomenological or intentional — about what it is for an event to be a sorting rather than merely a transition. Those premises are not supplied, and supplying them without begging the question is exactly the problem P1 was set to solve.

### Why "not presupposes" does not save the defense

The presupposition verdict is correctly "independent": the thin derivation runs independently, without circularity. But independence is not sufficiency. The defense was asked to demonstrate the INCOHERENCE of "indexed-at-nothing occurrence" WITHOUT ALREADY ASSUMING THE INDEX. It demonstrates the incoherence of "spatiotemporally unlocated occurrence" — a genuine result, but not the target. The incoherence of "standpoint-free conferral" in P1's thick sense remains undemonstrated. The honest ceiling is FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY: the thin index is forced at the conventional level; the thick index is not forced at any level by the argument as constructed.

### The one move that could upgrade the result

The defense would need to argue that "sorting A from not-A" is not just any token event but a token event of a kind that, by its functional or causal role, constitutively involves a perspective-pole — that the token-type "conferral" is individuated not only by spatiotemporal position but by the orientation of its causal role (cf. functionalist accounts of intentionality, Dretske 1988, Millikan 1984). This would be a naturalistic reduction of the standpoint rather than an assumption of it. Whether that move avoids a new Stroud gap is a live question, but it is the direction in which the argument would need to develop. As stated, the token-reflexive strategy does not take that step.
