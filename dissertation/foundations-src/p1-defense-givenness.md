# Defense of P1 — Phenomenological Givenness Strategy

## Premise under test

P1: Any act of conferral — sorting A from not-A, meaning, designating, applying a convention — is performed AS THIS DISCRIMINATION AND NOT ITS NEGATION, indexed at a standpoint. An occurrence indexed at nothing is no occurrence.

Strategy: argue that the phenomenological structure of every act — Husserl's absolute Here / Nullpunkt; Henry's auto-affection — constitutively occupies an index, such that "conferral occurs" entails "an index is occupied."

---

## The Two Objectors

**Objector 1 (Prasangika Madhyamaka):** grants that something occurs, ownerlessly and dependently (pratityasamutpada); denies that "occurs" carries any indexed residue. MMK ch. 2 (the goer/going analysis — SEP, Nagarjuna entry, citing MMK 2:3-11, [high] confidence) dismantles the from-here: there is no goer behind the going, no standpoint behind the sorting. Vigrahavyavartani: "I have no thesis" — the emptiness-claim makes no self-standing assertion and is not offered from a privileged vantage. Sunyata-sunyata: even "occurrence" and even "the from-here" are empty of svabhava.

**Objector 2 (Deflationary physicalist / Humean):** grants the physical event (thin reading); denies it has the thick form — sorting-as-such, at a standpoint — without an inference that crosses from thought to being. Dilemma: either "occupied-at-an-index" means only "spatiotemporally located" (trivial — every event is) or it means something thicker (question-begging — imports the standpoint by fiat).

The phenomenological strategy is primarily directed at Objector 1, the harder case. It also deflects Objector 2 by arguing that the index is neither the thin spatiotemporal reading nor an imported entity, but the internal form of the act as act.

---

## The Argument

### Step 1 — Conferral is an act, not a mere causal event

Sorting A from not-A is normatively directed: it applies a standard and tracks correctness. This is not a property added to the physical process but the condition under which we describe it as conferral rather than as mere differential response (a thermostat sorts temperature bands; no conferral occurs — there is no normative application). The act-character of conferral is built into the concept: to say "conferral occurs" is already to say more than "a causal process of differential response occurs." Call this the act-character claim.

### Step 2 — Every act has a phenomenological form: given as-from-here (Husserl)

Husserl, *Ideas II* (Hua 4/158; verified against SEP entry on Husserl, substantive revision Aug 8, 2025 [high]):

> "I have all things over and against me; they are all 'there' — with the exception of one and only one, namely the body, which is always 'here'."

The SEP gloss: "As an experiencing, embodied subject, I am the point of reference, the experiential zero-point or absolute here, in relation to which all of my perceptual objects, be they near or far, left or right, up or down, are uniquely related."

Key features of the Nullpunkt:
- It is not itself perceived as an object (Körper) but is the pre-reflectively lived body (Leib) that conditions every spatial act.
- It is not a further entity alongside the act; it is the structural zero from which the act's orientation is ranged.
- There is no "view from nowhere" — all experience is experience from the lived body as absolute Here.

Confidence in Husserl attribution: [high] — verified against SEP citing Hua 4 directly.

**Application:** every act of conferral, as an act, is an act from the absolute Here. The zero-point is not observed from outside but is the unthematic structural condition of any act being directed rather than undirected.

### Step 3 — Henry radicalizes: the act is self-given (auto-affection)

Michel Henry, *The Essence of Manifestation* (1963); *Material Phenomenology* (1990). Verified via SEP entry on Henry (first published Oct 7, 2016; revised Aug 19, 2024 [high]):

> "appearing itself must appear... that which makes things appear has itself to appear. Otherwise, nothing would appear at all."

Since intentionality is always of something other than itself (ek-static), it cannot make itself appear. The self-appearance of appearing must be non-intentional, immanent: "an auto-affection that refers to nothing but itself." Henry's "life" (phenomenological sense, not biological) is this self-feeling, this pathos: subjectivity is auto-affection, "the essence and origin of every possible phenomenon."

**Application to P1:** auto-affection means that the act does not merely occur at an index — it is the index's own self-presence. The act of conferral, when it occurs, is not merely located from-here; it is self-felt as occurring. The index is not a further entity appended; it is the immanent form of the act's self-presence.

Confidence in Henry attribution: [high] — verified against SEP.

Note on scope: Henry applies this to life / living subjectivity. Whether it applies to all acts of conferral regardless of substrate is a question the defense must handle (see Objection handling, below).

### Step 4 — The index is the act's internal form, not a posit

The deflationary objector charges: you are adding an index to the bare event. The Husserl-Henry reply inverts this: they are stripping back to ask what "occurring" means for an act (as distinct from a rolling stone).

Answer: occurring, for an act, means being given. Being given means being given as-from-somewhere / as-self-felt. There is no thinner sense of "the act occurs" that strips givenness entirely out while leaving an act rather than a mere physical event.

Analogy: the soreness of a pain is not an entity added to a neural event; it is the internal phenomenal form of the experience as experience. Strip it out and you have described a neural event, not an experience. Similarly, strip the from-here out of an act and you have described a causal differential response, not a conferral.

This is not a further posit; it is a conceptual analysis of what distinguishes an act from a mere process.

### Step 5 — Conclusion: an index is occupied

When conferral occurs: it occurs as an act (Step 1); every act carries the Husserlian Nullpunkt as its structural from-here (Step 2); Henry shows this structure is auto-affective — self-presencing, not merely located (Step 3); this from-here is the internal form of the act as act, not a further entity (Step 4). Therefore: whenever conferral occurs, the absolute Here — the zero-point of orientation / auto-affection — is operative as the act's own structure. An index is occupied.

## Confronting the Hard Counter: MMK Ch. 2 and the Ownerless-Occurrence Reply

The Prasangika reply: the goer/going analysis (MMK 2:3-11, SEP [high]) shows that "the goer" cannot be posited independently of "the going," nor vice versa. They are mutually empty. Applied to P1: "the act" and "its from-here structure" are mutually dependent concepts; neither is a self-standing anchor. The from-here has no svabhava. Further: sunyata-sunyata — even "occurrence" and even "the zero-point" are empty of svabhava. The Vigrahavyavartani: Nagarjuna possesses no thesis of his own; the refutation of svabhava proceeds dialectically, not from a privileged vantage.

**The defense has two responses. Only one is honest.**

**Response A — The conventional-level earning (the honest response):**

Even if "act" is empty of svabhava, the Madhyamika cannot, at the conventional level (samvrti-satya), refuse the distinction between an act and a mere causal event. Nagarjuna himself requires this distinction to teach, to argue, to correct opponents — and the two-truths doctrine (which he employs throughout) permits conventional designations. At the samvrti level, "conferral is an act" is true. And at the samvrti level, acts as conventionally characterized carry the from-here structure, because that is what "act" means conventionally.

Moreover: the Madhyamika who argues that the from-here is empty must argue it from somewhere. The performance of the denial is itself from-here. This is not a trivial performative argument ("you said something, so you have a standpoint") — it is a structural claim: every conventional act of arguing, denying, or sorting instantiates the from-here at the conventional level, including the act of insisting that the from-here is empty. The denier cannot exempt his denial from the conventional structure he invokes to deny.

This earns the index at the conventional level without vicious circularity. It does not assume the index is ultimately real; it shows that any conventional speaker — the denier included — operates with the from-here as a structural feature of conventional action and discourse.

**Response B — The ultimate-level earning (rejected as dishonest):**

One might try to argue that Henry's auto-affection is itself non-empty — an ultimate, self-standing ground of appearing. This fails. Applying sunyata-sunyata: auto-affection, if posited as ultimate, is itself empty of an inherent self-affector. "What is auto-affection empty of?" — of a substantial self-aff ecting entity. The Madhyamika dissolves it. Henry's auto-affection, if offered as an ultimate irreducible, is svabhava under a phenomenological description. Response B collapses and the defense must not use it.

## The Critical Self-Audit

**The load-bearing inference:** "conferral occurs" → "the act has the internal form of givenness-as-from-here" → "an index is occupied."

**Does this presuppose the index?**

At the ultimate (paramarthika) level: yes. Positing givenness-as-from-here as an internal form that is not itself empty is to import svabhava. The Madhyamika does not grant this. The step presupposes the index at the ultimate level.

At the conventional (samvrti) level: no, not viciously. The step appeals to what the denier must do — enact a from-here — not to what the denier must grant as an ultimate truth. The performative argument is not question-begging because it does not assume the index is ultimately real; it shows the denier is indexed at the conventional level by their own enacting.

**Verdict:** borderline. Genuinely demonstrative at samvrti; genuinely question-begging at paramarthika.

---

## Level Reached

**FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY.**

The strategy demonstrates that at the conventional level, any conferral — including the Madhyamika's own act of denying — occupies an index. The Madhyamika concedes the conventional level (this is required for the two-truths doctrine to function). The strategy does not defeat the Madhyamika at the ultimate level; the Madhyamika retains the reply that the from-here is itself empty, and at paramarthika the index dissolves.

This is the honest ceiling. The dissertation's forcing argument works at the conventional level — which is the level at which all conferral, language, and action actually occur. The ultimate 0 is conceded to the Madhyamika. The seam is real at samvrti; it is dependently-arisen-and-empty at paramarthika.

---

## Scope Qualification: Henry's Auto-Affection and the Substrate Question

Henry's auto-affection is developed for living subjectivity. A deflationary objector may press: does this apply to any system performing conferral, or only to phenomenally conscious living beings? If only to the latter, the argument begs the question by assuming the conferring system is phenomenally conscious — precisely what is at issue for AI systems (cf. the trained-on-testimony confound in Movement IV of the thesis).

Honest response: the defense does not require that every conferring system be phenomenally conscious. It requires only the weaker claim that any process we correctly describe as conferral (rather than as a mere differential response) carries the act-character and therefore the conventional from-here structure. The from-here at the conventional level is analytic to the concept "act," not to the further empirical claim of phenomenal consciousness. This weaker reading is sufficient for P1's purposes: P1 does not assert that every conferring system has a phenomenal inner life; it asserts that every conferral is indexed at a standpoint. The standpoint at the conventional level is the structural from-here that the concept "act" carries, not a phenomenal quale.

---

## Self-Grade: Moderate

**Why not strong:** the strategy does not achieve the highest verdict (ultimate). The critical step is borderline: it is genuinely demonstrative at samvrti but presupposes the index at paramarthika. A reader who demands ultimate proof will not be satisfied. The Henry-as-ultimate-ground gambit collapses under sunyata-sunyata and must be discarded. The honest ceiling is conventional, and the defense must own that.

**Why not weak:** the conventional-level result is real. The performative argument — the denier occupies an index in the very act of denying — is not trivial and is not question-begging at the samvrti level. The distinction between the act-form and the mere causal event is a genuine conceptual result, not an arbitrary posit. The Husserl Nullpunkt citation is primary-source verified (Hua 4/158 via SEP). The Henry auto-affection is primary-source verified (SEP entry). The Nagarjuna MMK ch. 2 goer/going analysis is primary-source verified (SEP). The argument has content and does real philosophical work.

**The contribution is a real bounded result:** P1 holds at the conventional level; the seam is genuine at samvrti. The dissertation's forcing argument is valid conditional on working at the conventional level — which is the level of all actual conferral. The ultimate is conceded to the Madhyamika without surrendering the conventional, and the conventional is enough to drive the forcing argument.
