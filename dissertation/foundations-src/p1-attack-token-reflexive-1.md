The defense holds at the conventional (samvrti) level — FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY stands — and fails against the Prasangika at the ultimate (paramartha) level.

**What is granted.** The Prasangika cannot coherently deny that the denier's own act of asserting ownerless-occurrence is a conventionally located event. Steps 1-3 earn the thin index (spatiotemporal location as a conventional individuation-marker) without circularity in the ordinary sense. This is exactly pratityasamutpada: dependent arising locates events without grounding them in svabhava. The FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY result stands. The denier performs an indexed act at the samvrti level while claiming the ultimate level is index-free.

**Where the bridge breaks.** The defense tries to ride the thin concession toward the thick claim P1 requires: that the "index" is not merely causal location but a standpoint that makes this conferral numerically this one as a discrimination-act. To close that gap, the defense invokes Campbell (1990) and Schaffer (2001) on spatiotemporal individuation of tropes as if this were a neutral, framework-independent premise. It is not. Those accounts treat spatiotemporal relations as real grounding relations — the trope's numerical distinctness is *grounded in* its location as an intrinsic anchor. That is svabhava under a different name. MMK ch. 2's goer/going analysis applies: the individuation-ground and the individuated token are mutually dependently designated; neither is found to be self-standing on analysis. Importing Campbell/Schaffer as "independently motivated" is only independent within a realist metaphysical background the Prasangika rejects by design.

**Sunyata-sunyata applied to token-hood itself.** Even "being a token" is a dependent designation (prajnapti). It does not carry intrinsic individuation-conditions the way the defense requires. The Prasangika can accept that tokens and types are useful conventional distinctions (samvrti-satya) while denying that "token-hood" is an intrinsic property that necessitates a svabhava-laden individuation-ground. The inference from "is a token" to "has a real spatiotemporal individuation-ground" only goes through if token-hood is intrinsically determinate — which is precisely what sunyata-sunyata denies.

**The VV "no thesis" counter-cut.** The defense claims its premises are "independently motivated," but VV's force is that any thesis — including the claim that token-individuation by spatiotemporal position is a metaphysical necessity — is itself empty. The Prasangika does not need to offer a rival account of individuation; it only needs to press that the account the defense invokes is substantive, not neutral, and is doing the work of the index by another name.

**Summary verdict.** The critical step (occurs → occupied-at-index in P1's thick sense) presupposes the index when examined through the Prasangika lens, because it depends on a realist individuation framework that imports svabhava as an unexamined premise. The defense reaches FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY — genuine, conceded, not trivial — but not the ultimate-level result P1 requires for the seam to be forced with full modal weight.
