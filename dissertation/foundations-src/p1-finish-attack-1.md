## Prāsaṅgika Madhyamika Adversarial Audit of the Finished P1 Defense

**Verdict on the ceiling: CONFIRMED — FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY-ONLY is the honest ceiling.** The draft's explicit verdict apparatus (Section 5) is honest and matches the binding adjudication: the thin index is earned non-circularly; the thick index is declared, sustained only dialectically, and conceded to the Prāsaṅgika at paramārtha. **BUT the draft contains live overclaims of the thick index in Sections 3 and 4 that must be fixed.** The single worst failure pattern: the defense lets the contested thick joint (B: 'discriminatory directionality is a real relational property the MMK dissolution cannot reach') inherit the security of the granted conventional claim (A: 'anyone arguing is conventionally committed to discriminatory directedness'). The Prāsaṅgika grants A freely and denies B at paramārtha. Three sentences assert B unhedged.

### The overclaims, ranked

1. **WORST — Section 3, Occupy-Not-Dissolve:** *'The act is still this partition and not its complement as a structural, relational property of the act, independent of whether a substantial subject stands behind it.'* Asserted flatly. This is the retorsion's Step 6 imported as a settled metaphysical result. Per the verdict (line 58) this is 'the exact spot the index re-enters.' śūnyatā-śūnyatā empties the perspectival-character concept; the draft states it as a property the act HAS.

2. **WORST — same passage:** *'the MMK dissolution does not reach it.'* Claims structural immunity for the thick joint. The Prāsaṅgika holds the dissolution DOES reach it at paramārtha. This wins the ultimate by stipulation. (The source retorsion doc's own Step 7 concedes śūnyatā-śūnyatā 'does dissolve even this perspectival property' — the draft reproduces Step 6 without carrying Step 7 into the same breath.)

3. **A DISGUISED BID TO WIN THE ULTIMATE — Section 3, Hardest Pressure:** the entire 'symmetry at the conventional level' / *'the thick index cannot be refuted from the ultimate level ... any more than it can be proved there'* argument. This manufactures a standoff at paramārtha. There is no standoff. The Prāsaṅgika does not assert the dissolution as a paramārtha thesis from within discourse — she does not need to; the 0 is what remains when proliferation ceases (the silence-exit). At paramārtha the thick index is not 'unrefuted' — it is DISSOLVED, exactly as the thin index is (verdict lines 74, 80). Treating it as 'cannot be refuted there' grants it a foothold at the ultimate level it does not have. This is the most dangerous passage because it dresses an overclaim as even-handed honesty.

### Does it honestly concede paramārtha?

**Yes, in the dedicated subsection** ('Where Prāsaṅgika Madhyamaka Wins') — that passage is clean, correct, and non-negotiable: the thin index is empty at paramārtha; the thick is dissolved along with everything the conventional forcing uses. **But the concession is undercut** by the 'symmetry' argument earlier in the SAME section, which tries to keep the thick index alive at parity at the ultimate level. The concession and the smuggle coexist; the smuggle must be removed so the concession stands alone.

Even though the concession eventually arrives, the overclaim stands first and frames the reader. Every samvrti-only clamp must be inline and immediate, not deferred to a later subsection.
