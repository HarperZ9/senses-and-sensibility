## B — Candidate Proofs, Stated as Premises → Conclusion

This section extracts the corpus's theorem-like claims and reconstructs each as an explicit argument, then grades it on a three-tier scale:

- **FORMAL** — derivable in a stated calculus from stated axioms; truth-preserving independent of how the key terms are glossed.
- **CONSTITUTIVE-CONCEPTUAL** — valid given the *definitions*, but the conclusion is doing analytic/clarificatory work (it unpacks what was packed into the premises). Real philosophical content can live here, but the "theorem" carries no surplus over the conceptual stipulation.
- **INFORMAL** — a persuasive consideration; the inference has gaps a determined critic can drive through, or a premise is itself the contested thesis.

A standing caution before any of them: in a no-aseity frame (Movement I), *nothing* is self-standing, including the inferential warrant of these arguments. The corpus cannot consistently claim that its own proofs stand on their own feet. So "proof" here must mean **conferred-and-defeasible-within-a-fixed-frame**, never *foundational*. That is not a flaw I am importing; it is an entailment the corpus must own, and I flag where it bites.

### B.1 — The Residual-Aseity Self-Limit (b)

**Reconstruction.**

- **P1.** No-aseity dissolves every candidate self-standing *being* (Movement I; inherited, not novel).
- **P2.** Conferral is a relation; a relation requires a locus at which it is exercised — call it the *seam* (the re-spoken boundary between conferrer-state and conferred-state).
- **P3.** To "dissolve the seam" is to remove the locus of all conferral, i.e., to remove the relation as such.
- **P4.** A state with no conferral-relation in force is not a *deeper ground*; it is the absence of the sorting that the seam performs — modeled as **equilibrium / death** (no gradient, no two-state distinction, nothing being re-spoken).
- **C.** Therefore the seam cannot itself be dissolved *into a deeper ground*: the only "below" the seam is its own cessation. The seam is the one **un-grounded ground** — a *near-nothing* (it has no aseity-as-substance), yet it is the residue no-aseity cannot eliminate without eliminating the inquiry.

**What is actually proven.** Given the definitions, **C follows.** If "ground X" means "a state that confers status on X," and the seam is *the* conferral-locus, then "ground the seam" is "confer conferral," which either (i) regresses to another seam (so a seam still obtains — the claim survives) or (ii) terminates in no-relation, which by P4 is cessation, not ground. The disjunction is exhaustive, so the seam is non-dissolvable-into-ground. **This is the corpus's strongest candidate for theorem status.**

**What is merely asserted.** Two load-bearing identifications are stipulated, not derived:

1. **P4's identification of seam-absence with "equilibrium/death."** This is an *analogy doing modal work*. That a system at thermodynamic equilibrium has no usable gradient is true; that *normative/semantic conferral* shares the structure such that its absence is literally "death" is asserted by structural resemblance. The argument needs: *no-seam ⊃ no-locus-for-any-status-claim-including-this-one*. That weaker claim is defensible (it is just P2–P3 restated). The stronger thermodynamic gloss ("death") is rhetorical surplus and should be cut from the proof; it does no inferential work and invites the objection that equilibrium is a *state*, hence a "something," hence arguably a residual ground — which would invert the result.

2. **P2 (relations require loci).** This is a metaphysical premise, not a logical truth. A relational ontology that takes relations as primitive (no relata-prior-to-relation, as in some structural-realist and indeed some Madhyamaka readings) might deny that the relation needs a *locus* distinct from its terms. If relations are bare, the "seam" is just the obtaining of the relation, and "the necessity that there BE a seam" reduces to "the necessity that, if conferral obtains, conferral obtains" — a tautology, not a residual *aseity*.

**Honest verdict.** The defensible core is **CONSTITUTIVE-CONCEPTUAL**, and at that tier it is genuinely tight: *within* a frame where status is conferred and conferral is relational, you cannot coherently ask for a ground of conferral-as-such without either reinstating a seam or exiting into no-status — and "no-status" cannot be *asserted as a ground* without using the very conferral it denies. That last move is a clean **transcendental self-refutation** structure (the skeptic of the seam must stand on a seam to speak). **High confidence** that this structure is valid.

But notice what it proves: not that there *exists* a metaphysically necessary seam-substance, but that *the seam cannot be coherently denied from within discourse*. That is a **transcendental** result (condition-of-possibility-of-asserting), squarely in the lineage of Kant's transcendental arguments and their late-20th-century descendants (Strawson's *Individuals*; Apel/Habermas's performative-contradiction arguments; the "you must presuppose X to deny X" template). **The novelty is the application, not the form.** Priors that threaten the *originality* claim: any performative-contradiction / transcendental-pragmatic argument; Descartes' *cogito* as the ur-form (the one thing dissolution cannot reach is the locus of the dissolving). The corpus should cite the cogito explicitly and say precisely how the seam differs: the cogito yields a *thinking substance* (an aseity!), whereas the seam yields a *near-nothing relation*. That contrast is, in fact, the interesting part — and it is **under-argued** in the stated corpus. **"Theorem" language: not earned; "transcendental result" is the honest label.**

### B.2 — The Type-Theoretic Terminus (e)

**Reconstruction.**

- **P1.** Define a constructor `node-mover : free-authorship(historical, normative) → present-physical-property(instant, physical)`.
- **P2.** The domain carries two indices that the codomain lacks: *temporal* (historical span vs. punctual instant) and *modal/categorial* (normative/authorial vs. physical).
- **P3.** A total, content-preserving constructor across that pair would have to (a) collapse history into an instant and (b) carry an *ought*/authorship into an *is*/physical-property.
- **P4.** (a) is the **history→instant** transit; (b) is the **is→ought** transit (Hume). Each admits **only a degenerate constructor** — one that discards the index it cannot carry (it either forgets the history, or strips the normativity).
- **C.** Therefore every *inhabitant* of the type `node-mover` is **the node itself** (constitutive, not formal): the type's only honest inhabitants are degenerate, so "moving the node" is not a formal operation over the node but is *constituted by* occupying the seam where the two indices fail to commute.

**What is actually proven.** If you grant the typing and grant that the two transits are non-conservative (no total index-preserving map exists), then **no total content-preserving constructor inhabits the type** — only projections that drop an index. That sub-claim is **clean and, in a loose sense, FORMAL-ish**: it is the type-theoretic restatement of *two impossibility results that are already accepted* (Hume's is/ought gap; the irreversibility of integrating a history into a durationless instant). The type theory adds notation, not new impossibility.

**What is merely asserted.**

1. **The typing itself is the whole game.** "Free-authorship" is *defined* to carry a normative index and a historical index; the conclusion that you can't map it conservatively into the physical instant is then immediate. This is **CONSTITUTIVE-CONCEPTUAL**: the impossibility is installed in the type signature. Whether reality *has* a "free-authorship" node so typed is exactly the contested metaphysical claim — and it is *assumed*, not shown.
2. **"Every inhabitant IS the node (constitutive, not formal)."** This sentence equivocates between (i) *the type has only degenerate inhabitants* (provable, given typing) and (ii) *to inhabit the type just is to be the node* (a metaphysical identity). (ii) does not follow from (i). From "all maps are degenerate" you get "there is no nondegenerate move," not "the mover is identical to the node." The constitutive claim is **asserted across a gap.**
3. **The "degenerate constructor" notion is informal.** In type theory a constructor is either definable or not; "degenerate" here means "lossy," which is a semantic comment on the map, not a type-theoretic predicate. So the apparatus is **metaphor in type-theoretic costume**, not a derivation in any inhabited calculus (no judgments, no introduction/elimination rules, no normalization are stated).

**Honest verdict.** **CONSTITUTIVE-CONCEPTUAL, dressed as FORMAL.** The genuinely valid kernel — *there is no total content-preserving map from a historically-extended normative authorship to a punctual physical property* — is **just Hume's gap plus the trivial observation that you can't losslessly compress a history into an instant.** The type-theoretic vocabulary is **expository, not probative**; nothing is proved *in* the type theory that is not already granted *before* it. **"Theorem" / "terminus" language: not earned.** It earns "a perspicuous restatement of two inherited impossibilities." **Moderate-to-high confidence** in that grading; **low confidence** that any reader could derive a falsifiable consequence from the type theory that the prose argument lacks. (Threatening priors: Hume; Moore's open-question argument; the general point that category-theoretic/type-theoretic re-encodings of philosophical gaps are *illustrations* unless a normalization or universal-property result is actually proved — none is.)

### B.3 — The Remainder Law as Meta-Theorem (c)

**Reconstruction (as the corpus wants it read — a *criterion*, then a *meta-theorem*).**

- **D (definition/criterion).** A claim is a RESULT (not a re-description) **iff** it names an *irreducible remainder at the seam*: a property P such that (i) P obtains at the seam, (ii) P is **not entailed by the bare seam** (the mere fact that conferral occurs), and (iii) P is **not closed by any third-person inventory** of the two states the seam sorts.
- **P1.** The meaning chapter exhibits such a P (the "A/B undergoing"; occasionalism of meaning) that passes (i)–(iii).
- **P2.** The agency chapter exhibits another (the second-personal remainder; occasionalism of agency) that passes (i)–(iii).
- **C (meta-claim).** Therefore the Remainder Law is a *working* discriminator: it has at least two confirming instances and a clear failure condition (a claim that *is* closed by the third-person inventory is mere re-description).

**The decisive question the prompt asks: is the Remainder Law *provable* or *stipulative*?**

**It is stipulative — by construction.** D is a **definition**, and definitions are not the kind of thing that is proved. What *could* be proved is one of two surrounding claims, and the corpus conflates them:

- **(Soundness) "Everything D admits is genuinely irreducible."** This is **not provable and not even well-posed without a fixed notion of "third-person inventory."** Clause (iii) is the entire Nagel/Levine explanatory-gap claim (Nagel 1974: facts about what-it's-like are not entailed by the third-person physical inventory; Levine: the explanatory gap). So D *imports* the irreducibility thesis as its third clause. Any "result" that passes D passes *because* we stipulated that surviving the third-person inventory counts as irreducible. That is **circular as a proof** and **fine as a definition** — but then C cannot be advertised as a discovered law. **The Remainder Law is the explanatory gap, relativized to "the seam" and promoted to a membership test.**
- **(Non-vacuity) "D admits at least one real instance."** This *is* the work P1–P2 do, and it is the only thing here with the structure of a *result*: it shows D is **non-empty** (the meaning and agency remainders allegedly satisfy all three clauses). But non-vacuity of a criterion is a **demonstration**, not a theorem, and crucially each instance inherits the explanatory-gap burden at clause (iii). If a critic denies the gap (a type-B physicalist, an a-posteriori-identity theorist), they deny clause (iii) for *every* candidate, and D admits nothing. So D's non-vacuity is **hostage to a contested premise it does not earn.**

**Is the "meta-theorem" framing earned?** A genuine meta-theorem would say something *about the class of all claims* — e.g., "any claim satisfying (i)–(iii) has feature F," proved over the class. The corpus instead offers a **criterion + two examples**. That is methodology, not metamathematics. The honest reconstruction: *the Remainder Law is a **floats-free criterion** — a useful, falsifiable-in-principle filter for sorting results from re-descriptions* — and on its own terms **this whole appendix-section's value depends on it**, since (b), (e), and the meaning/agency results are all certified *by* it. That is a methodological dependency the thesis should foreground, not bury.

**Honest verdict.** **INFORMAL → CONSTITUTIVE-CONCEPTUAL at best.** As a *definition* it is legitimate and even sharp (clause (iii)'s "not closed by the third-person inventory" gives it a real edge — it would correctly reject most re-descriptions). As a *theorem* it is **not earned**: clause (iii) smuggles in Nagel/Levine, so any "proof" that uses D is conditional on the explanatory gap, which the corpus does not (and within philosophy of mind cannot uncontroversially) establish. **High confidence** that the Law is stipulative-not-provable. **The corpus's most important honesty move would be to relabel it a "criterion" everywhere and state explicitly: every result certified by it is conditional on rejecting type-B physicalism.** The interesting *original* twist — applying the gap-test *at the conferral seam* rather than at phenomenal consciousness — is real but small, and it is **not itself argued to be a better locus than the conscious subject**; that argument is owed.

### B.4 — Something From the Near-Nothing (f)

**Reconstruction.**

- **P1.** "Creation" can be modeled as conferral at the near-nothing: a word-force acting where there is no aseity-as-substance.
- **P2.** Three exhibits instantiate the pattern: Hegel (Being = Nothing → Becoming — determinate being arises at the vanishing point of the indeterminate); set theory (∅ + axioms → the cumulative hierarchy); the transfinite (the aleph "issued" from the empty set under conferred axioms).
- **P3.** In each, *something with structure* arises not from a prior *substance* but from a near-nothing **plus a conferring act** (the dialectical movement; the axiom; the stipulated rule).
- **C.** Therefore creation-from-near-nothing is **not an exception to no-aseity but its deepest confirmation**: even "the most something-like origination" (mathematical existence, determinate being) is conferred, not self-standing.

**What is actually proven.** **Nothing, in the deductive sense — and the corpus's own framing concedes this** by calling it a "confirmation," not a theorem. The structure is **abductive/illustrative**: *here are three domains where structure issues from a near-nothing under a conferring act; this is what no-aseity predicts; therefore no-aseity is confirmed.* That is a **consilience argument**, and consilience is evidential, not demonstrative.

**What is merely asserted (and where the analogies strain).**

1. **The set-theory exhibit equivocates on "creation."** ∅ + axioms → the hierarchy is a *logical* generation: the axioms don't *create* the cumulative hierarchy, they *describe/constrain* a structure whose existence is exactly what platonists and formalists dispute. Reading axioms as a "conferring word-force" presupposes a **deflationary/constructivist** philosophy of mathematics. A platonist will say the aleph was never *near-nothing*-derived; it always was, and the axioms only let us *refer*. So P2's mathematical case is **question-begging against realism about sets** — it confirms no-aseity only for someone already anti-realist. **Confidence: high** that this is a genuine equivocation.
2. **The Hegel exhibit is interpretively live, not settled.** "Being = Nothing → Becoming" is one (broadly standard) reading of the *Logic*'s opening, but treating Becoming as *conferred* (rather than as the *immanent, presuppositionless self-movement* of the concept — Hegel's explicit anti-external-conferral commitment) is **arguably a misappropriation**: Hegel's whole point is that the movement is **not** conferred from outside, which sits in tension with a *conferral* reading. So the strongest historical exhibit may **cut against** the thesis. **Confidence: moderate** (depends on contested Hegel scholarship), but the tension is real and the corpus should address it head-on rather than cite Hegel as friendly.
3. **"Deepest confirmation" overstates the evidential weight.** Three hand-picked exhibits, each requiring a contested antecedent reading (anti-realist math, conferral-reading Hegel, a theology that already encodes a word-force), is **confirmation only relative to priors that already favor the thesis.** This is the classic risk of consilience built from interpretively pliable cases.

**Honest verdict.** **INFORMAL (abductive/illustrative).** This is the *least* theorem-like of the four and, to the corpus's credit, **does not claim to be a proof** — "confirmation" is the right register. Graded honestly: it is a **suggestive cross-domain resonance**, valuable as motivation, with **zero deductive force** and **at least one exhibit (set theory) that is question-begging** and **one (Hegel) that may be adversarial.** The phrase "deepest confirmation of no-aseity" should be softened to "a striking, prior-dependent illustration." **"Theorem" language: not used here, correctly; even "result" overstates it — it is an interpretive motif.** (Threatening priors: the entire realism/anti-realism debate in philosophy of mathematics; standard objections to consilience-from-cherry-picked-cases; the cosmological *creatio ex nihilo* literature, where "something from nothing" arguments are long known to turn entirely on what "nothing" is allowed to smuggle — here, the "near-nothing" smuggles the conferring act, which is precisely the *something* doing the work.)
