# Defense of Premise P1: "To Confer Is to Occupy an Index"

## A Complete Viva-Ready Defense

---

## 1. The Premise and the Two Indices

### The Premise

P1 states: any act of conferral — sorting A from not-A, designating, applying a convention, discriminating this from that — is performed as *this* discrimination and not its negation, indexed at a standpoint. An occurrence indexed at nothing is no occurrence.

The dissertation's forcing argument, the exhaustive disjunction C/Z (conferral-occurring versus conferral-wholly-ceased), is valid only if P1 holds. The defense of P1 is therefore not a peripheral strengthening move; it is the seam the entire structural argument depends on. If P1 collapses to a restatement of *pratītyasamutpāda* — merely saying that conferral is a dependently-arisen event, located as all events are located — the disjunction loses the modal force it needs. P1 must hold in a sense *stronger* than "is a dependently-arisen event."

The entire defense turns on one diagnostic question: which sense of "stronger" is actually earned, and at which level?

### The Two Indices

The disambiguation that governs every load-bearing sentence below. It is not announced once and then forgotten; it is stapled to each claim, because the single failure mode of this defense is letting the thick reading inherit the thin reading's security.

**THIN index.** The event is a concrete particular at a determinate spatiotemporal position. This is what every dependent event has. It entails that "conferral-at-no-position" is conventionally incoherent — a type with no token does not occur — but it does not by itself add anything beyond what the Mādhyamika already grants under *pratītyasamutpāda* (dependent arising with spatiotemporal coordinacy). The thin index is what the forcing argument *earns*, non-circularly, at *samvrti*.

**THICK index.** A standpoint or perspective *from which* sorting is performed *as this discrimination and not its negation*. This is what P1's modal strength would require if it were to do work that the bare dependent-arising account cannot do. The thick index is not merely "located at L"; it is "sorts from L as this sorting." It would be defined by intentional or perspectival structure that the thin index does not have. The thick index is a *declared premise* — never derived at any level, including the conventional.

The entire adjudicated verdict reduces to a single finding about this gap: **the thin index is earned non-circularly; the thick index is not.** Establishing the thin forcing cleanly — and then mounting the strongest *honest conventional* dialectical pressure on the thick joint, while conceding it is empty at *paramārtha* — is the work of this defense. Nothing below proves, derives, or forces the thick index.

## 2. The Conventional Forcing, Fully Worked

### The Strategy: What Counts as Non-Circular

The admissible standard, stated by the dossier itself: demonstrate the incoherence of "indexed-at-nothing occurrence" *without already assuming the index.* The two objectors who must both be answered are (i) the Prāsaṅgika Mādhyamika, who grants occurrence but denies any residue beyond ownerless dependent arising, and (ii) the deflationary type-A physicalist, who grants occurrence but presses the thin/thick dilemma: either the index is trivially spatiotemporal (idle) or it is non-trivially perspectival (smuggled). A defense wins the conventional forcing only if it draws its premises from what *both* objectors already grant.

### The Token-Reflexive Argument

**Step 1 — Tokens are what occur; types do not.**

C. S. Peirce introduced the type/token distinction in 1906 with a formulation that has become the settled metaphysical baseline. A type "does not exist; it only determines things that do exist." A token is "a single event which happens once and whose identity is limited to that one happening... significant only as occurring *just when and where it does*." (Peirce 1906, Collected Papers, cited in Liebesman 2026, SEP "Types and Tokens." Confidence on the SEP exposition: high. Confidence on primary volume/page from the Collected Papers: moderate without independent verification of the primary source.)

This is not a special claim about conferral. It is the ordinary metaphysics of occurrence. If a conferral occurs — if the sorting act *takes place* — it takes place as a token event. The type "conferral in general" does not occur; it determines things that do.

**Step 2 — Token identity requires spatiotemporal location.**

What individuates two exactly similar token events as numerically two rather than one? The main accounts surveyed in the SEP entry on Tropes (Maurin 2023, high confidence) are:

- *Spatiotemporal individuation (SI):* for any tropes a and b that exactly resemble each other, a ≠ b iff a is at non-zero spatiotemporal distance from b (Campbell 1990: 53f.; Lowe 1998: 207; Schaffer 2001: 249, cited in Maurin 2023). On SI, the spatiotemporal position *just is* what individuates the token.

- *Primitivist individuation (PI):* numerical distinctness is primitive, not grounded in position. Most contemporary trope theorists prefer PI (Ehring 2011; Campbell 1990: 69, cited in Maurin 2023). But PI does not deny that tokens occupy positions; it denies only that their positions *explain* their distinctness. A token that occupies no spatiotemporal position at all would, under *any* account of individuation, be indistinguishable from an uninstantiated type.

The SEP entry on Events (Varzi 2025, high confidence) confirms: concrete events are "spatiotemporally located" and contrast with universals precisely in this respect. An occurrence at no spatiotemporal position satisfies the definition of an uninstantiated type, not a token.

Additionally, on Jaegwon Kim's property-exemplification view of events (Kim 1966, 1969; cited as foundational throughout the SEP Events entry): an event is an object's exemplifying a property at a time. The time-constituent is part of the event's structure, not a subsequent label. A conferral-event therefore has a *thin* index as a constitutive component, not as an inference attached afterward.

**Step 3 — Peirce's formulation directly supports the thin reading of P1.**

Peirce's token is "significant only as occurring *just when and where it does*." This is P1's "indexed at a standpoint" rendered in the metaphysics of particulars — but in its *thin* reading. A conferral event is significant as *this* conferral — numerically this token — because it occurs just when and where it does. A conferral with zero positional residue would have no identity conditions distinguishing it as a token from any qualitatively identical conferral, or from the uninstantiated type "conferral in general." Uninstantiated types do not occur. (This earns location; it does not yet earn the *from-here* perspective. See the antecedent caveat below, and Section 3.)

**Step 4 — The performative regress.**

The Prāsaṅgika denier asserts: "conferral occurs ownerlessly, with zero index residue." This assertion is itself a conferral act — a discrimination act (distinguishing the Mādhyamaka position from svabhāva-positing alternatives), performed at a moment, in a conversation, by this speaker. *She grants she is asserting/discriminating* — the antecedent is conceded by her own act. By Steps 1–3, that assertion is therefore a token event. Its being *this* assertion — numerically this one rather than a qualitatively identical assertion made yesterday or by another speaker — requires, at minimum, that it occupy a position. The denier cannot perform the denial without instantiating a token, and instantiating a token requires being located.

The regress is genuine and not question-begging. It does not assume there is a standpoint and then infer there is one. It takes as a premise the structural description the opponent herself must grant — that the denial is an occurring act she performs — and applies the general metaphysics of token individuation. If she then denies *that* denial is a located token, she performs a further token act of denial. The regress closes the loop: every act of denying the index is itself a *thin*-indexed act, at the conventional level.

The denier therefore enacts a thin index at the *samvrti* (conventional) level even while denying any ultimate-level index. What the regress forces is location; it does not force the *from-here-as-this* perspective.

### The Antecedent the Forcing Earns, and the One It Does Not

A precise scoping that strengthens the result by stating its boundary honestly. The inference chain — *occurs → is a conferral-token → individuated as this token → occupies a thin index* — is valid. But its first link has an antecedent: the located event must be identified *as a conferral-token* rather than as substrate dynamics (heat-dissipation, neural firing) at the same coordinate. Identifying a physical event *as* a conferral requires the event to instantiate the type "conferral" as a real property over the substrate — a fact type-A physicalism denies (deflation-internal source, lines 56, 76–80; verdict line 56).

This does not weaken the forcing where it matters. For the *denier's own act*, the antecedent is granted by her: she admits she is asserting, discriminating, sorting the Mādhyamaka position from its rivals. So the performative regress is airtight against anyone who is *arguing* — every interlocutor conducting the C/Z dialectic concedes they confer. What the forcing does *not* deliver is a general proof that physical events *qua* physical carry a conferral-index; that would require instantiating the conferral-type over the substrate, which the deflationist refuses. **The seam binds discourse-participants — which is exactly and only what the C/Z disjunction needs.** Scoped this way, the conventional result is genuine and unembarrassed: it forces the thin index on everyone in the argument, and claims nothing about events outside it.

### Why This Is Non-Circular — for the Thin Index

The circularity audit asks: does the argument derive "occupied-at-a-thin-index" from premises that already contain the index? The answer is no, in the technically relevant sense. The derivation chain is:

> *occurs (as a conferral-token, granted by anyone arguing)* → *is individuated as this token* → *occupies a spatiotemporal position* → *occupies a thin index*

Each link is drawn from the general metaphysics of particulars applied to all occurrent events without prejudice to conferral specifically. Neither the Peircean type/token distinction nor spatiotemporal individuation makes any mention of conferral, standpoints, or discrimination. The thin index falls out of what it is to be an occurring token, not out of a prior assumption about what conferral is. This is the definition of a non-circular derivation: the conclusion is reached from premises the opponent already grants on independent grounds.

The non-circularity badge attaches to the **thin index only.** It does not transfer to any thick-index material in Section 3. Wherever "non-circular," "derived," "earned," or "opponent-granted" appears in this defense, read "thin / *samvrti*."

The deflationist-internal route (the co-equal twin argument) confirms the same ceiling by a different and even more economical path: it draws *only* on the deflationist's own commitments — SI, the Kim property-exemplification view, Peirce — without importing any framework the deflationist could reject as foreign. Its circularity score is identical: index=independent, *for the thin index.*

### What the Conventional Forcing Kills

"Conferral with a 0 residue" — conferral occurring at no thin index — is conventionally incoherent. Not merely exotic or theoretically unattractive: *incoherent* at the *samvrti* level, because it is equivalent to asserting that a type is occurring while no token of that type is occurring. Peirce: the type "does not exist." A type without a token is not an occurrence; it is an abstract pattern that determines possible occurrences. To assert that conferral is presently occurring while denying it occupies any index is to assert that a token-occurrence is taking place without any token. That is a formal contradiction at the conventional level.

The C/Z disjunction — conferral-occurring versus conferral-wholly-ceased — is therefore exhaustive at the *samvrti* level. Anyone operating within philosophical discourse, making arguments, tracing consequences, performing evaluative discriminations, is on the conferral-occurring side; that side carries a conventional thin index. The disjunction does the modal work P1 needs *at one level, on the thin index.*

## 3. The Thick Joint — The Honest Conventional-Dialectical Pressure

> **Guard for the whole section.** Nothing in Section 3 derives, proves, or forces the thick index at any level, including the conventional. It mounts the strongest *conventional* dialectical *pressure* on the one who dissolves to zero, and it concedes that the Prāsaṅgika dissolves the thick index — and the thin index with it — at *paramārtha*. Every strong sentence below is to be read under this guard.

### What the Thick Index Would Require

The conventional forcing establishes that conferral-as-token occupies a spatiotemporal position. This is the thin index. What the C/Z disjunction needs for its *strongest* form is the thick index: a standpoint *from which* sorting is performed *as this discrimination and not its negation* — the "from-here" structure, the perspectival determinacy, the fact that the act is enacted as *this* partition and not its complement.

The move from "located at L" to "sorts from L as this sorting" reads intentional or perspectival structure off a physical description. This is Barry Stroud's gap reopened inside the predicate "conferral" itself. (Stroud 1968, "Transcendental Arguments," *Journal of Philosophy* 65, cited in SEP "Transcendental Arguments," Stern 2023, high confidence.) Stroud's objection to transcendental arguments: they show that we must *think* there is an X, not that X obtains. The gap between "must be treated as perspectivally determinate" and "is perspectivally determinate" is exactly the gap between the thin and thick indices.

The thick index is not derived. This must be stated plainly and without qualification. The candidate who claims to have derived it — at any level — is claiming more than the dossier earns.

### The Burden-Shift

The strongest honest dialectical move is not to claim the thick index is proved but to raise the cost of the move that dissolves it to zero.

The dissolution move — "conferral occurs, but no standpoint is occupied; the act is ownerless dependent arising, nothing more" — is not a neutral default position. It is a substantive claim, and it has a performative cost *while it is being argued*. To press the dissolution claim *in discourse* is to perform an act of evaluation: selecting this-consequence-as-the-correct-characterization and not its alternative. That evaluative discrimination is itself a sorting act. It is not a first-order assertion about the world, but it is a partition of the space of philosophical options. Performing it requires something to count as this discrimination and not that one.

The burden-shift runs as follows: the dissolutionist must enact the partition *while arguing*. The vitanda move (Nāgārjuna's "I have no thesis" from the *Vigrahavyāvartanī*, Westerhoff 2010, cited in SEP "Nāgārjuna," Westerhoff 2026, high confidence on the SEP exposition, moderate confidence on primary verse attribution) relocates the sorting rather than eliminating it at the conventional level. The Mādhyamika running a *prasaṅga* selects consequences as *unwelcome* — as reductios the interlocutor ought to reject. To display a consequence as unwelcome, she must evaluate: this-consequence-is-damaging and not acceptable, by the interlocutor's lights. That evaluative tracking — sorting what-the-interlocutor-finds-unwelcome from what-she-does-not — is performed from a conventional interpretive standpoint. The vitanda moves the *thin* index from the first-order assertion level to the evaluative-interpretive level. It does not eliminate the conventional index *while she argues.*

What this forces is the THIN index on the dissolutionist (she is arguing, therefore she instantiates a located token). It is only *dialectically suggestive*, not forcing, at the thick level. The denier who claims to enact the partition from genuinely no standpoint, while visibly arguing in discourse, is in a pragmatically unstable position *at the conventional level*: she is either arguing — in which case the performative regress applies and she is thin-indexed — or she has exited discourse entirely into non-conceptual silence, in which case the pressure lapses (see below). The philosophical *claim* "conferral is indexed at nothing" cannot be established from non-conceptual silence, because the establishment of a philosophical claim is itself a thin-indexed act. But establishing-a-claim is not what the silence-exit attempts; the silence-exit makes no claim at all.

The payoff of the burden-shift, stated with the load-bearing qualifier travelling with it: the *from-here-as-this* structure is operationally **presupposed-in-discourse** at every moment the argument is conducted — which establishes that the arguer must *treat* the act as from-here-as-this, **not** that the from-here structure *obtains*. This is precisely Stroud's must-treat-X / X-obtains gap, and it is **not** crossed here. The presupposition is present as conventional performance, for as long as and only as long as the argument is conducted; it lapses the instant discourse lapses.

### The Occupy-Not-Dissolve Stance

The positive formulation of the conventional-dialectical pressure is: *at the conventional level, while the argument is being conducted,* the act of conferral occupies the seam between the partitioned and its complement; it cannot be dissolved into neither side without ceasing to be a conferral. This is the occupy-not-dissolve stance — and it is a *samvrti* stance only.

The argument from the retorsion strategy (Step 6): even after the Nāgārjunan mover/motion analysis (MMK chapter 2, discussed in SEP "Nāgārjuna," Westerhoff 2026), a directed motion retains its *direction* as a relational property of the event. The dissolution of the direction-substance does not dissolve the direction. Similarly: the dissolution of the sorter-substance does not dissolve the discriminatory direction of the sorting.

Stated at its correct level: **at the conventional level**, while the argument is being conducted, the act is enacted as *this* partition and not its complement; this discriminatory directedness is a conventional relational *designation* of the sorting act, not an ultimately real property — *śūnyatā-śūnyatā* empties it along with the sorter-substance. The "independent of whether a substantial subject stands behind it" point is a *samvrti* point: conventional events retain conventional relational properties even with no substance behind them. It does **not** mean the property survives the ultimate dissolution.

Two Mādhyamaka tools must be kept distinct here, exactly as the verdict keeps them distinct:

- The **goer/going analysis of MMK chapter 2** targets *svabhāva* — intrinsic, substance-level existence independent of relations. It does **not** reach discriminatory directionality *at the conventional level*; conventional relational properties are not denied at *samvrti*. The SEP "Nāgārjuna" entry (Westerhoff 2026) confirms: "the Mādhyamika does not deny that there are a variety of sensory and mental events that happen in close temporal and causal connection."
- *Śūnyatā-śūnyatā*, the deeper move, **does** reach discriminatory directionality — and empties it, along with everything else, **at *paramārtha***. This relational-directionality survival is therefore conventional only. The retorsion source concedes this explicitly at its own Step 7: "at the ultimate level, *śūnyatā-śūnyatā* does dissolve even this perspectival property." The directionality argument buys *samvrti* durability against the chapter-2 substance-analysis, and nothing more.

**Caution — the unearned step is here.** Discriminatory *direction* is a thin-plus conventional relational property that the chapter-2 analysis leaves standing at *samvrti*. It is **not yet** the thick *from-here perspective*. The step from "directed" to "sorts from a perspective as this sorting" is the deflationist's horn (b) / Stroud's gap, and it is **not** discharged by the goer/going reply. Direction must not be allowed silently to carry the thick load. The verdict (line 58) names this exact spot — "perspectival determinacy survives substance-dissolution" — as "the exact spot the index re-enters: it is either own-being under a relational label (contradicting emptiness) or mere conventional designation." The defense takes the second disjunct openly: it is conventional designation, and at *paramārtha* it is empty.

What the opponent must concede, *if she remains in discourse*: that her own argumentative acts have discriminatory direction at the conventional level; that she is selecting this-consequence-as-damaging rather than that one; that this selection is *this* selection and not its complement *while she makes it*. These are conventional-level concessions. They do not establish that the selection has ultimate reality, and they do not cross from direction to perspective. They establish only that the thick-*seeming* structure — the *from-here-as-this* — is operationally presupposed-in-discourse, as conventional performance, for as long as and only as long as the argument is conducted.

### The *Śūnyatā-Śūnyatā* Point, Stated Without Manufactured Symmetry

There is a real observation in the neighborhood of *śūnyatā-śūnyatā*, but it must be stated as the asymmetry it is, not as a standoff.

The observation: *śūnyatā-śūnyatā* (emptiness is itself empty) is, by its own logic, a conventional therapeutic device, not an ultimate description of reality. The SEP "Nāgārjuna" entry states: "the theory of emptiness is not to be regarded as an ultimately true theory either." It follows that if a Mādhyamika *asserts*, as a thesis from within discourse, "the thick index is dissolved at the ultimate level," that assertion is itself a conventionally indexed act — the thin result applied to her. This buys symmetry of **conventional standing** between the occupation move and the dissolution move: both, *while conducted in discourse*, are thin-indexed conventional acts.

But this buys **no thick standing whatsoever**, and it manufactures **no standoff at *paramārtha***. The honest situation is asymmetric, and the earlier draft's "cannot be refuted there any more than proved there" framing must be struck, because it grants the thick index a foothold at the ultimate level it does not have. The correct statement:

- The Prāsaṅgika does **not** assert the dissolution as a *paramārtha* thesis from within discourse — and she does not need to. The 0 at *paramārtha* is not a competing claim that could win or lose an argument; it is what remains when conceptual designation ceases (the non-conceptual-silence exit; retorsion Step 7). She wins the ultimate not by a successful counter-thesis but by the cessation of proliferation.
- At *paramārtha* the thick index is **neither proved nor merely unrefuted — it is dissolved, exactly as the thin index is.** Nothing is established there; that is the whole point of *śūnyatā-śūnyatā*. The thick residue is zero (verdict lines 74, 80). It is not "unrefuted" with a protected standing; it was never earned there, and at *paramārtha* everything — thin index included — is empty.

The only pressure that genuinely survives is this: *while* the Mādhyamika argues, she conventionally enacts discriminatory directedness and cannot consistently deny it *while arguing*. The moment she exits to non-conceptual silence, the pressure lapses and she wins the ultimate cleanly. There is no symmetric arena at *paramārtha* in which the thick index sits unrefuted. This is the tightest the dialectical pressure can honestly be made, and it stays strictly at *samvrti*.

### Exact Concession: Where Prāsaṅgika Madhyamaka Wins and What It Costs

The Prāsaṅgika wins at the ultimate level. This concession is honest, clean, and non-negotiable.

*Śūnyatā-śūnyatā* empties occurrence, token, position, and perspectival character at the *paramārtha* level. Even "occurrence = tokening" and "tokening = spatiotemporal individuation" are *samvrti* truths that have no ultimate-level residue. The thin index is empty at *paramārtha*. The thick index is not merely unearned there — it is dissolved, along with everything the conventional forcing uses to establish the thin index. There is no level-jump by which the conventional-dialectical pressure of Section 3 reaches into *paramārtha*; it does not, and it does not pretend to.

The cost to the Mādhyamika: she concedes the *samvrti* level entirely. At every moment of philosophical discourse, her own argumentative acts are conventionally (thin-)indexed. The seam holds conventionally. The exhaustion of C/Z is conventional. The claim "conferral with 0 residue is incoherent" is true at *samvrti*. She wins at *paramārtha* by a move — the silence-exit — that costs her every conventional result. This is precisely what the Buddhist two-truths framework is designed to handle. It is not a trap; it is her framework working as intended.

The cost to the dissertation: the thick index, P1's strongest content, is a declared premise at the ultimate level — and undischarged even at the conventional level, where it is only pressured, never derived. The contribution is bounded to the conventional level, and within that level to the thin index plus dialectical pressure on the thick joint. The C/Z disjunction carries its full force within *samvrti* discourse. It does not force a *paramārtha* result.

Neither side is embarrassed by this accounting. The Mādhyamika gets *paramārtha*; the dissertation gets *samvrti*. The question is whether *samvrti* is enough for the contribution, and the answer is yes — it is a genuine bounded result, not a restatement of triviality.

## 4. The Fallback Ladder

The defense holds at three levels, in descending order. The thick index is **never** the achieved top rung; Level 1 is the strongest *conventional-dialectical* pressure, and it concedes *paramārtha* exactly as Level 2 does.

**Level 1 — Conventional-dialectical pressure on the dissolutionist (strongest honest claim, still *samvrti*-bounded).** This level does **not** establish the thick index. It establishes only that the opponent who *remains in discourse* is conventionally committed to discriminatory directedness and cannot consistently deny it *while arguing*. The honest formulation for the thick joint is **sustained dialectically** — anyone who remains in discourse is committed to *treating* the act as this-partition-from-here — but *treating-as is not occupying*, and the dialectic raises the cost of denial without deriving the thick index. The opponent **can** eliminate the thick index without pragmatic self-contradiction, by conceding thin location and denying perspectival structure (the deflationist's clean horn (a)) or by exiting to non-conceptual silence (the Prāsaṅgika's silence-exit); the self-contradiction bites only on denial of the **thin** index. The word "forced" is therefore reserved for the thin result and is not used here. This level concedes *paramārtha* exactly as Level 2 does. The thick index is never derived at any level.

**Level 2 — Conventional forcing (derived, non-circular, opponent-granted — thin index).** If the conventional-dialectical pressure is pressed to its limit and the committee insists that the perspectival structure is not established even dialectically, the conventional result stands independently. The thin index is *derived*, not declared. The derivation is non-circular. "Conferral with 0 residue" is conventionally incoherent. The C/Z disjunction is exhaustive at *samvrti* for anyone arguing. This is the verdict: **P1-FORCED-CONVENTIONALLY.** It is a genuine philosophical contribution: the seam is not merely stipulated, not merely a relabeling of *pratītyasamutpāda*, and not merely a declared premise the committee must swallow. A real forcing argument exists, even if it reaches only the conventional level and only the thin index.

**Level 3 — Membrane framing (authn ≠ authz).** If even the conventional forcing is pressed — if the committee denies that the token-reflexive argument earns more than *pratītyasamutpāda* restated — the contribution rests on the membrane framing itself: the operational distinction between authentication and authorization, between *is* and *ought*, between the discrimination that a system performs and the standing that warrants it. The membrane operationalizes the autonomy of the deontic: it is the *structural* realization of the seam P1 describes. This is the floor — the contribution does not collapse to nothing if P1's full conventional forcing is contested, because the membrane distinction is a real, non-trivial observation about the architecture of normative systems, independent of the metaphysics of conferral. But this is the fallback of last resort. The defense does not lead with it.

## 5. What Is Established, What Is Declared

### The Honest Ceiling: P1-FORCED-CONVENTIONALLY

**What is established by non-circular argument from opponent-granted premises (thin / *samvrti*):**

At the *samvrti* (conventional) level, any occurring conferral act — performed by anyone who is arguing, who thereby grants she confers — is a token event, individuated by and occupying a spatiotemporal (thin) index. The denier's own act of denial instantiates a token. "Conferral with a 0 residue" is conventionally incoherent: it is the claim that a type is occurring with no token, a formal contradiction at the conventional level. The C/Z disjunction is exhaustive at *samvrti* for discourse-participants. The circularity audit clears this derivation, for the thin index only: the index is derived from the general metaphysics of token individuation, not assumed from premises that contain conferral-specific standpoint structure.

**What is declared, not derived (thick / unearned at every level):**

The thick index — the standpoint *from which* sorting is performed *as this discrimination* — is a declared premise. The move from "located at L" to "sorts from L as this sorting" requires intentional or perspectival structure that the token-individuation argument does not deliver. This is Stroud's gap reopened inside the predicate "conferral." The step is not earned, even conventionally, without a separate phenomenological argument this defense has not built and that would introduce its own vulnerability points. The candidate defends the thick joint with *conventional dialectical pressure* (Section 3) — sustained, not forced — and concedes that the Prāsaṅgika dissolves both the thick and the thin index at *paramārtha*.

**What the contribution is not:**

- Not P1-PROVEN: no defense earns the thick standpoint-index without presupposing it or invoking a structure — *entelecheia*, the Spencer-Brown crossing, the Husserlian act, "perspectival determinacy as a real relational property" — that *śūnyatā-śūnyatā* dissolves at *paramārtha*.
- Not P1-FAILS: the seam is not merely *pratītyasamutpāda* renamed with nothing added. A genuine, non-circular, opponent-grantable forcing argument survives at *samvrti* on the thin index.
- Not P1-DEFENSIBLE-AS-DECLARED-PREMISE-ONLY: a declared premise is one no defense earns independently. Here a defense does earn the thin index independently. P1 is more than a stipulation — but only at the conventional level, and only in its thin reading.

**Restatement for the viva:**

P1 is forced conventionally. The token-reflexive argument and its deflationist-internal twin establish that any occurring conferral act — for anyone who is arguing, who grants she confers — occupies a thin index at *samvrti*, non-circularly, from premises both the deflationary physicalist and the Prāsaṅgika already grant. The performative regress closes the loop: the denier cannot enact the denial without instantiating the thin index she denies. The thick index — P1's strongest modal content — is **sustained dialectically as a conventional commitment of anyone who argues, never proved, never derived, and never forced**: anyone who conducts the argument must *treat* the act as from-here-as-this, but treating-as is not occupying (Stroud's gap, uncrossed). The Prāsaṅgika who exits to non-conceptual silence wins at *paramārtha*, where the thick index and the thin index are both empty — not in a standoff, but dissolved. The honest ceiling is exactly FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY-ONLY. The contribution does not exceed it, and it does not fall below it.
