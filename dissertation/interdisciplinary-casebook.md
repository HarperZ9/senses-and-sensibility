# Interdisciplinary Casebook: Thirteen Thinkers, Two Clusters, One Bridge

## 1. The Unifying Insight

The roster is not a reading list; it is a courtroom split into two dockets joined by a single ruling. The first docket is the **determinism prosecution** — Sapolsky, Schopenhauer-via-Einstein, Tesla, Lincoln, Hawking, Haidt, Caesar — every one of whom, in his own register, denies the self-authoring will and presses the case that states merely succeed states with no uncaused causer in the regress. The second docket is the **lived-locus casebook** — van der Kolk, Schwartz, Frankl, Jung, with Libet and Schurger as its neural substrate and Sagan, Harari, and Hawking-Mlodinow as its cosmic and civilizational frame — the clinical and historical record of significance being withdrawn and re-inhabited at a thrown standpoint while the facts hold steady. The single ruling that joins them is **Movement VI's orthogonality result**: determinism is a thesis about *dynamics* (how states produce successor states) and supplies neither the is→ought transit nor the history→instant transit; it neither locates nor dissolves the *inhabited standpoint* at which significance-for is re-bestowed. So the prosecution is granted in full — every link in every regress conceded — and the casebook is read for what it actually shows: that conferred sourcehood and the occasionalism of meaning live precisely where a complete causal story is silent, in the A/B remainder of undergoing that no functional redescription closes. The list, read correctly, is the prosecution and the casebook bridged by the one move that makes granting the prosecution cost the thesis nothing.

## 3. The Determinism Cluster, Integrated

Seven thinkers are convened under one charge: each denies the self-authoring will. The chapter does not contest them — it **grants** them in full, and the grant costs the thesis nothing, because every one is making a claim about *dynamics* while Conferred Sourcehood is a claim about an *inhabited standpoint*.

**Sapolsky is the central foil the whole of Part III is built against.** His regress — genes, hormones, childhood, culture, the prior neuronal state, "turtles all the way down," no uncaused causer — is the strongest contemporary statement of the position the chapter must absorb without being absorbed by. Grant every link. The thesis never asserted libertarian self-origination; Part III defines conferred sourcehood as *neither* self-origination *nor* determinism's dissolution, so Sapolsky's regress refutes only the first disjunct. The inhabited standpoint is not a gap in the causal chain he could close — it is the locus the causal story is *about*, and a complete account of how a state was produced does not deliver what it is *like to undergo* the bestowing. His ethics-without-blame is the sharpest test of the orthogonality cut: eliminating *desert* removes the metaphysical ground of retribution but leaves wholly untouched the withdrawable-without-subtraction / re-inhabitable-without-addition structure of meaning. Blame-elimination is not meaning-subtraction. (The chapter should not lean on the scientism charge against him — Riskin, Fischer, Piovarchy — to dismiss him; it should grant his science entirely and win on the cut, which is the stronger and more honest position.)

The remaining six are the same move applied six more times. **Schopenhauer-via-Einstein** gives the most precise formulation of what conferred sourcehood must survive: the *second-order* denial — "Man can do what he wants, but he cannot will what he wills." The attribution is corrected on the record: the proposition is Schopenhauer's (*On the Freedom of the Will*, 1839); Einstein quotes and endorses it, and is the twentieth-century transmitter, not the author — citing the line as "Einstein's own" would be the fabrication-by-misattribution the casebook forbids. Einstein himself performs the orthogonality move in miniature: he denies will-of-the-will *and in the same breath* says he is "compelled to act as if freedom existed." That forced "as if," lived rather than resolved, *is* the surviving standpoint the chapter formalizes. **Hawking's** "effective theory" of free will is structurally cognate to conferred sourcehood — agency real at its level of description, not abolished by a lower-level complete story — but he grounds the effectiveness in *epistemic intractability* (we cannot compute the outcome), whereas the thesis grounds the standpoint in the *A/B remainder* (a fact about undergoing, not about predictive limits); use him to motivate level-relativity, then mark where the thesis goes deeper than instrumentalism. **Haidt** scales Movement IV to the moral domain — the elephant leans, the rider confabulates as post-hoc press secretary — which is Libet/Schurger confabulation at moral scale; but read precisely his data support an *occasionally-effective* rider ("free won't"), not an epiphenomenal one, so he is granted as confabulation-evidence and explicitly refused as evidence for epiphenomenalism. **Tesla** (the automaton self-report, sourced correctly to the 1900 *Century* article, not *My Inventions*) and **Lincoln** (the 1846 Handbill's "Doctrine of Necessity," with the lifelong reading resting only on Herndon's partisan reminiscence) are *specimens* — one introspective, one biographical — of the very coexistence the orthogonality move predicts: Lincoln is empirical proof that the doctrine of necessity and supreme moral agency inhabit one biography. **Caesar** is epigraph and nothing more; *"the die is cast"* is fatalist idiom, not a doctrine of necessity, and placing it on the argument's load path would be the famous-name parade the casebook warns against.

**The single move, stated once, discharges all seven:**

> Every member makes a claim about how states produce successor states. The thesis makes a claim about the standpoint at which significance-for is re-bestowed. A complete account of the first yields neither the is→ought transit, nor the history→instant transit, nor the location of the inhabited locus whose undergoing is the A/B remainder. Therefore each member is granted in full and the thesis is untouched — because conferred sourcehood was never a gap in the dynamics for determinism to close.

## 4. The Locus / Meaning / Trauma Cluster, Integrated

This cluster is the empirical and clinical casebook for two of Part IV's load-bearing moves — the **A/B undergoing** and the **occasionalism of meaning** — and reaches back to Movement IV's **non-unitary subject**. Its danger is the dissertation's stated risk: four famous names reading as a parade. The defense is that exactly one is genuinely load-bearing for the closure; the others are structural map, honest opponent-witness, and framing-under-quarantine.

**Van der Kolk is the one load-bearing figure, and the load is the phenomenon, not the trade book.** On the first front, *The Body Keeps the Score* makes the A/B undergoing empirical: traumatic experience is registered and retained *below* the narrative-propositional system — somatic state, implicit memory, alexithymia (the inability to put inner states into words) — so the body keeps what the verbalizing self cannot represent. This is the empirical face of Merleau-Ponty's incarnate meaning, the lived pre-reflective body as a meaning-bearing standpoint. The honest caveat, owned in the text: the identification "trauma-as-somatically-stored ≅ the A/B undergoing-remainder" is *the corpus reading van der Kolk*, an interpretive use of his memory-systems data, not a claim he asserts — stated that way it is defensible; stated as "van der Kolk *shows* the A/B remainder is real" it overclaims. On the second, stronger front — genuinely his territory — trauma and depression supply the literal test data for the occasionalism of meaning: significance drains while the facts hold steady. The person reports, accurately, that they have a family, a job, a history — the propositional inventory intact — yet the *mattering* is gone, the world grey, "behind glass"; in recovery the facts have not changed and significance returns. That is *withdrawable-without-subtraction-of-fact* and *re-inhabitable-without-addition* operationalized. Because this depersonalization/anhedonia phenomenon is independently documented (clinical depression, depersonalization-derealization disorder), the closure does not rest on a single popular book — which is what makes him load-bearing rather than illustrative, and what insulates the argument from the methodological criticism the trade book has drawn (cite the phenomena and the supporting literature, not the book's more sweeping neuro-claims).

**Schwartz is load-bearing for the structural map of the non-unitary subject — and strictly fenced.** IFS models the mind as a plurality of *parts* (exiles; protective managers and firefighters) around a **Self that is not itself a part**, characterized by the 8 Cs of Self-energy, with "Self-like parts" that imitate the Self's calm without its connectivity. The precise philosophical payload is the **unity-vs-connectedness distinction**: IFS denies *substantival unity* (no homuncular self) while affirming a *leading, witnessing locus* that integrates by connection rather than by being one more thing — a remarkably close clinical analogue to conferred sourcehood (the Self does not manufacture the parts, yet genuinely leads). But the fence is non-negotiable: Schwartz's later work drifts metaphysical — "unattached burdens" as external entities, the Self as a "sacred essence" connected to the Divine, unburdening as quasi-exorcism. A Self that is a sacred *aseity* is exactly the wrong model for a framework whose thesis is conferral *without* aseity. Cite the clinical-structural IFS; disavow the entity/Divine substance of Self.

**Frankl is illustration-only, and his central verb runs *against* the thesis — which is why he is staged as a tension, not smoothed.** *Man's Search for Meaning* is the clinical witness for significance-surviving-fact: in the camps the facts were fixed (starvation, loss, imminent death), yet the man who "had a why" endured. But Frankl's verbs are *found*, *discovered*, *detected* — he is a *realist* about meaning, with a theistic "super-meaning" horizon, whereas the occasionalism thesis is anti-realist in precisely that sense: no pre-seated meaning waiting to be read off, only a re-speaking *to* a locus. So recruit Frankl as a witness against fact-realism about significance; never recruit "meaning cannot be given" as if it endorsed conferral — it is the opposite verb. This is the single most important place in the cluster not to inflate.

**Jung is handle-with-care, vocabulary under quarantine.** Individuation (the integrative trajectory toward wholeness) and thrownness-into-unauthored-archetypal-structure are genuine resonances with the *thrown inhabited locus* — one inherits the symbolic field as one inherits a language. But the archetypes and collective unconscious carry a rigor-reception liability with exactly the analytic examiners the framework most needs, and Jung's Self is a *substantival archetype of wholeness*, a self-standing telos — an **aseity of the Self** the title forecloses. Cite Jung for structure and vocabulary, framed explicitly as illustrative resonance the corpus reinterprets; never as evidence the locus is real, and never letting the wholeness-telos define the locus.

**The anti-parade discipline, made explicit:** a parade cites four names as four supports; this cluster cites *one* empirical support (van der Kolk, and there the load is the independently-attested phenomenon), *one* structural map (Schwartz, fenced from his own metaphysics), *one* honest opponent-witness (Frankl, whose verb opposes the thesis), and *one* framing device (Jung, quarantined) — and says so. Two standing cautions for the draft: (1) wherever the text says van der Kolk or Schwartz *shows* a corpus thesis, change the verb — they supply phenomena the corpus *reads*. (2) Both Schwartz's and Jung's Selves tilt toward a self-standing Self; in a framework titled *Conferral Without Aseity* an un-fenced Self is self-refutation.

## 5. The Cosmological / Epistemic / Civilizational Frame

This cluster does not argue the no-aseity thesis; it *stages* it at the two scales the dissertation cannot itself inhabit — cosmic and civilizational — and supplies the epistemic floor beneath both. It is a **frame, not a proof**: the opening nihilism and the closing return.

**Sagan is the meaning-closure compressed to one image — illustration, never argument.** *Cosmos*'s "we are a way for the cosmos to know itself" is conferred sourcehood spoken at planetary scale: an indifferent universe with no meaning-of-its-own (the nihilist premise) plus a conscious *locus* that confers significance by the act of looking back. *Pale Blue Dot* performs the withdrawable-without-subtraction structure — the photograph subtracts no physical fact, yet the significance is bestowed, not found ("no hint that help will come from elsewhere to save us from ourselves"). *The Demon-Haunted World*'s "baloney detection kit" is the skeptical discipline that keeps a meaning-conferring mind honest. Sagan is a popularizer who *asserts* the conferral picture rather than grounding it; he earns his place as the load-bearing *image* and must not be cited as if he argues the thesis. The most quotable line is the most often misquoted — the exact *Cosmos* wording is **handle-with-care** and must be pinned to the transcript or cited as paraphrase.

**Harari is load-bearing for conferral-at-scale — but only tethered to Searle.** *Sapiens*'s "imagined orders" thesis — money, nations, rights, gods, corporations, law exist only because large numbers collectively believe and keep re-affirming them — *is* the no-aseity thesis applied to institutions: nothing self-standing; status and authority conferred, relational, re-spoken. This is Searle's institutional facts (status functions, collective acceptance, "X counts as Y in context C") in narrative-historical dress, and Harari supplies the empirical-historical demonstration that Searle gives analytically. His self-as-story / free-will skepticism (the experiencing-self vs. narrating-self pairing, most explicit in *Homo Deus*) attaches secondarily to Movement IV. The discipline: Harari is load-bearing *for the conferral-at-scale claim*, where he converges independently with Searle; he is *not* an authority on free-will metaphysics, where he reports a consensus rather than establishing one. Pair him with Searle in every load-bearing use — alone he reads as pop-history; tethered, he is the civilizational instantiation the thesis needs.

**Hawking–Mlodinow are the epistemic floor — load-bearing, used narrowly.** *The Grand Design*'s **model-dependent realism** (cite both authors; the doctrine is jointly theirs) holds there is no theory-independent test of reality — we access only models, judged by adequacy and coherence, never an aseitic reality-in-itself; the goldfish in the curved bowl could formulate valid laws from inside its frame, and we have no standpoint to call its picture "less real." This is the matching *epistemic* claim to the *ontological* no-aseity thesis: we never reach an aseitic reality to measure the conferral against, which closes the flank "but surely there's a real reality the conferral falsifies against" — there is no model-independent vantage from which to lodge it. It also reinforces orthogonality: a complete causal model is *still a model*, inhabited and selected, not a God's-eye readout. Because the doctrine slides (philosophers of science note) between instrumentalism and structural realism, cite it for the single load-bearing point — no model-independent access — and not its finer commitments.

**How the frame stages the spiral:** *no aseitic meaning anywhere* (Sagan's indifferent cosmos, Hawking's inaccessible reality-in-itself) → *therefore all meaning is conferred at the inhabited locus* (Harari's re-spoken orders, generalized to the significance-for of the Meaning Closure) → *and the conferral is alive while inhabited, void when withdrawn* (the *emet / met* of the Aleph, now legible at cosmic and civilizational scale). The bookends keep the thesis from reading as private phenomenology: the same withdrawable-without-subtraction-of-fact structure that governs one life governs a currency and a cosmos. Two genuine pillars (Hawking–Mlodinow for epistemology, Harari+Searle for civilizational conferral) and one frame-setting illustration (Sagan) — inflating Sagan to a pillar, or citing Harari as a free-will authority, is exactly where breadth curdles into the assembled tell.

## 6. The Rigor Verdict

A fifteen-name roster is a liability before it is an asset. In an analytic viva the examiner reads breadth not as erudition but as a search query that returned too many results; the default inference is *AI-assembled* or *autodidact-magpie*, and once that prior is set every genuine citation is re-read through it. The single inclusion rule:

> **A figure earns the body text if and only if removing them forces you to rewrite a premise. If removing them only weakens a *sentence*, they are illustration — footnote or cut.**

**The minimal load-bearing set (keep three to four, not fifteen):**

1. **Libet (1983)** — the readiness potential and the conscious "veto," the source of the *plural* brain (sub-personal initiator + reflective vetoer) Movement IV requires. Cite it *as contested* — introspective W-timing and the veto are the most-attacked parts of his own apparatus.
2. **Schurger (2012)** — the stochastic-accumulator deflation the plural-subject thesis must *survive*: even if the RP is not a literal pre-conscious decision, the sub-personal/reflective split is still readable off the accumulator dynamics. Pairing Libet *with* his most serious modern critic is the opposite of a parade — it is a live methodological dispute the candidate adjudicates, and it forecloses "you're citing superseded 1983 neuroscience."
3. **A named analytic interlocutor for orthogonality + conferred sourcehood — currently the empty slot, and the most serious finding.** Movement VI and Part III do analytic work neuroscience cannot underwrite: the is→ought and history→instant transits are normative, not dynamical. The orthogonality thesis sits in the lineage of compatibilist and two-perspectives arguments and *needs a named source* — Strawson's reactive attitudes, Frankfurt/Fischer-style sourcehood compatibilism, or a Sellarsian manifest/scientific-image split for the "inhabited standpoint." The roster is **inverted**: the periphery (popularizers, anecdotes) is over-cited while the philosophical spine is under-cited or unnamed. That inversion is itself the AI-tell.
4. **(Cluster-specific load-bearers the closure genuinely needs)** — **van der Kolk** for the occasionalism front (load = the independently-attested intact-facts/drained-significance phenomenon, not the trade book) and **Hawking–Mlodinow** for the no-aseity epistemology and **Harari+Searle** for civilizational conferral. These survive the inclusion rule because removing them forces a premise-level rewrite of Part IV's empirical operationalization and the frame's epistemic floor — provided each is held to its narrow load.

**Illustration-only (cite as color, never as authority):**
- **Sapolsky** — retained *solely* as the determinist foil for Movement VI, one sentence, trade-book status flagged. Sapolsky-the-popularizer arguing no-free-will is the witness the framework is positioned *against*, not support.
- **Haidt** — strong illustration of confabulation at moral scale, but a moral-psychology bridge to Movement IV, not an independent metaphysics; explicitly *not* evidence for epiphenomenalism.
- **Frankl** — testimony for significance-under-fixed-fact, quarantined from his realist metaphysics; his verb opposes the thesis.
- **Sagan** — the load-bearing *image* of the closure; pushes toward cut if treated as argument.
- **Tesla, Lincoln** — specimens (introspective, biographical) of the coexistence the orthogonality move predicts; period color and lived-case, never premises.

**The cuts (reception risk exceeds value):**
- **Jung** — footnote-with-hedge at most; archetypes/collective-unconscious read as non-naturalistic and *cost* credibility on the page where meaning-closure needs maximal rigor, and his substantival Self conflicts with the no-aseity title.
- **Caesar** — epigraph only; fatalist *idiom*, not doctrine; on the load path it is the fastest way to make an examiner conclude the breadth is ornamental.
- **Schwartz's later metaphysics** (not IFS structure) — the Divine/entity/sacred-essence apparatus is cut and fenced; the structural map stays.

**The finding that matters most — honor the critic, do not parade names:** fixing the breadth-liability is not primarily a matter of *cutting* the weak names. It is *adding* the two or three real analytic interlocutors the arguments already depend on but do not yet cite. Cut to a defensible core, name the missing sourcehood-compatibilist and manifest/scientific-image sources, hold every retained figure to its narrow load (van der Kolk to the phenomenon, Harari to Searle, Hawking-Mlodinow to the single epistemic point, Sapolsky to one foil-sentence), and the "AI-assembled breadth" read disappears — because what remains is not a parade but a defensible dependency graph.
