# Hard-Incompatibilist Opponent's Report

## Attack on Component (1): the second-personal remainder reduces without remainder

The drafts claim "I hold YOU responsible" leaves a remainder over forward-looking quarantine/deterrence/repair. It does not. Here is the reduction they think they have blocked.

**The "address" is the address-slot of a behavior-shaping practice.** Both drafts lean on the same load-bearing move: forward-looking shaping is "first-to-third-person," management of an *it*, whereas resentment registers a *you* who can answer. But the second person is not an extra ontological pole — it is the *interface format* of one particularly effective behavior-shaping technology. Address is how social primates run their mutual-modification protocol. When I resent you, I am running a calibrated signal that (a) raises the cost of your defection, (b) recruits third parties to the same, and (c) updates my own future disposition toward you. The phenomenology of "treating you as a source who can answer back" is the *user-facing rendering* of that protocol, not evidence against its being that protocol. Sapolsky's whole point is that the felt difference between "addressing" and "managing" is itself a caused disposition — and one we can recommend revising once we see what it is doing.

**The [reactive] draft concedes the game in its own §1.2.** Its central argument is: Pereboom *recommends replacing* the second-personal stance, and "an abolition is not a reduction... you can only be urged to abandon what is not already identical to the thing you are urged to adopt." This is a non sequitur, and it is the chapter's worst single move. I am not urging you to abandon a thing identical to deterrence. I am urging you to abandon a *wrapper* (the desert-laden reactive attitude) around a *function* (behavior-shaping) — keeping the function, discarding the wrapper. That is exactly a reduction-plus-elimination: the reactive attitude reduces to (function + desert-gloss), and I eliminate only the gloss. The draft's inference "you can only be urged to abandon what is not identical to what you adopt" is false: you can be urged to abandon phlogiston-talk while keeping combustion. Combustion is what phlogiston-talk was *tracking*. Behavior-shaping is what reactive-attitude-talk was tracking. The draft even uses the phlogiston analogy *against itself* in §8 and then waves it away.

**Forgiveness and apology do not survive as a remainder.** Both drafts make forgiveness the crown jewel: "to forgive is to release a claim, not to cease a deterrence program." Reduction: forgiveness is the *relationship-repair* arm of the forward-looking package — the disposition-reset that restores cooperative expected value after a defection, signals to the network that the relationship is back online, and halts the costly resentment-maintenance loop. "Releasing a claim" is the internal rendering of "terminating the sanction-disposition and re-opening the cooperation channel." There is no transaction "between standpoints" that is not a transaction in expected future cooperative value plus signaling. The drafts assert the remainder; they never exhibit a property of forgiveness that is *not* discharged by quarantine + deterrence + relationship-repair. Naming the triad and saying "but the address!" is not exhibiting one.

**Strawson's reactive attitudes are caused dispositions Sapolsky can explain and revise.** The [compat] draft's strongest sub-move (§6) is that the objective stance "feels like exclusion from the community of addressers," and a pure shaping theory supposedly predicts it should feel like *fairer* treatment. This is wrong on its own terms. A shaping theory predicts exactly that exclusion-feeling: being moved from the participant protocol to the management protocol is, for a social primate, a status demotion with real fitness consequences, so of course it *feels* like loss. The feeling is the evolved alarm at being de-prioritized as a cooperation partner. That the demotion *feels* like a denial of standing is fully predicted by — not evidence against — the view that "standing" is the protocol-membership token of a behavior-shaping community. P.F. Strawson himself grants the attitudes are natural facts about us; Sapolsky adds that natural facts about us are caused, explicable, and — once explained — *revisable on the merits*. "Practically inescapable" (the [reactive] draft's fallback) is a claim about our psychological stickiness, not about the attitudes' justification. Inescapability is not a remainder; it is an addiction.

## Attack on Component (2): the occasionalism of agency fails — drain always tracks a dynamical difference

The claim (O): authorship drains and returns *without a corresponding subtraction of dynamical fact*; agency-loss does not *always* track a dynamical difference.

**This is false, and the [reactive] draft admits the falsifier is real and live.** The unwilling addict watching himself relapse *is* in a different dynamical state than the recovered, integrated agent: different prefrontal regulatory engagement, different striatal/dopaminergic dynamics, different stress-axis state. The IFS "part-led vs. Self-led" distinction, if it tracks anything real, tracks a difference in network configuration. Dissociation has neural correlates. *Every* reported drain or return of authorship that is a real psychological event is a real dynamical event. There is no clean case on the table where authorship varies and the dynamics are genuinely, fully matched. The drafts never produce one — they gesture at therapeutic reports "the neural story does not yet cash out one-to-one" ([reactive] §8), which is an appeal to *current ignorance*, not a demonstration of orthogonality. "Not yet measured" is not "not present."

**The retreat to "constituted by, not merely correlated with" is a dualist smuggle.** Both drafts, cornered, narrow (O) from "no dynamical difference" to "the dynamical difference does not *constitute* the ownership." But under physicalism, if the ownership-difference supervenes on the dynamical difference (and they concede supervenience — [reactive] §2.2: "supervenes on but is not exhausted by"), then there is no further fact of ownership that floats free of the dynamics. "Not exhausted by" is doing illicit work: either the normative ownership-fact is fixed by the physical facts (then it is *not* occasionalist — it tracks dynamics with metaphysical necessity, and (O) is false as stated), or it is *not* fixed by the physical facts (then you have property dualism, which the no-aseity framework's own physicalism forbids). The drafts want the word "occasionalism" — withdrawal *without subtraction of fact* — while conceding supervenience, and that is incoherent. Supervenience *is* subtraction-tracking. You cannot have both.

**The manipulation cases prove my point, not theirs.** The drafts' best evidence for (O) is Plum: "matched dynamics, opposite ownership." Two replies. First, the dynamics are *not* matched — Plum-1 has a real-time external controller continuously writing his states; that controller's coupling *is* a dynamical difference, an exogenous driver in the causal graph that the ordinary agent lacks. The ownership difference tracks exactly that causal-structural difference. Second, and decisively: I do not grant that ordinary Plum-4 is a source either. That is the whole four-case argument. The drafts try to break the continuum at the second-personal/bypass distinction, but see below — that break fails, so the manipulation cases give them no orthogonality result; they give a difference in causal *etiology* that tracks, dynamically, all the way down.

## The four-case argument, pressed

The drafts' answer to Pereboom is identical in both: Case 1 is "shaping that bypasses the address"; Case 4 is "shaping that runs through the address"; these differ *in kind*, so the continuum breaks. This fails three ways.

1. **The distinction is not a kind-difference; it is a place on the very gradient I built.** "Constituted through the second-personal community" vs. "installed by bypass" is a difference in the *causal route* by which the agent's dispositions were set. But every case 1→4 is a difference in causal route — that is what the cases *vary*. Manipulators (1), pre-programming (2), engineered upbringing (3), ordinary upbringing (4) are *all* etiologies that set the dispositions. "Did it run through other addressing agents?" — in Case 3 the engineering community *are* addressing agents; in Case 1 the neuroscientists could trivially be stipulated to address Plum while they manipulate him. The drafts' own [compat] §8 admits "engineered upbringing (Case 3) shades into manipulation" and answers only by re-asserting the kind-distinction. Re-assertion is not a principled break.

2. **The break presupposes the thing in dispute.** "Plum-1 is not a source because his ownership was installed rather than taken up in address" — but whether ordinary determined formation is *taking-up* rather than *installing* is *exactly* what Pereboom denies. Ordinary upbringing installs your dispositions by a causal process you did not author, using your caregivers' addressing behavior as one of its installation tools. Calling Case 4 "taking-up" and Case 1 "installing" labels the conclusion; it does not earn it. The reactive-attitude community is *part of the installation apparatus*, not an alternative to it.

3. **The graded-cases concession is a loss disguised as a win.** Both drafts say the marginal cases (cults, coercive control, abusive formation) are "hard in exactly the place the theory predicts." But my continuum *is* that gradient made total. If responsibility fades smoothly as second-personal constitution becomes partial, and ordinary formation is just the far end of a continuum that includes coercive control, indoctrination, and (at the limit) manipulation, then there is no joint at which "real sourcehood" switches on. The drafts have conceded the continuum is continuous and then claimed a kind-break at one end of it. You cannot have a smooth gradient *and* a difference in kind along the same axis.

## My single strongest objection, and whether the drafts answer it

**Single strongest objection (the one to beat):**

> Both float-free components are the *same* move — "there is an irreducible second-personal/ownership fact not captured by the third-person dynamical-plus-forward-looking description" — and that move is, in each case, defended only by (a) asserting the reduction "leaves a remainder" while never exhibiting a remainder-property that quarantine+deterrence+repair fails to discharge, and (b) treating *current inability to fully cash out the fact dynamically* as evidence the fact is non-dynamical. Strip those two, and (R) is the desert-gloss on a behavior-shaping protocol and (O) is supervenient-hence-dynamics-tracking. The "remainder" is not a discovered feature of agency; it is the *name the drafts give to their own refusal to complete the reduction.* The second-personal "you" is the address-slot; the occasionalist "drain" is a dynamical state-change described in the intentional idiom. Nothing floats free. What the drafts call "conferred sourcehood" is "dynamics run through a standpoint that runs a mutual-modification protocol" — which is the necessity-of-locus residue plus a sociological fact about primate signaling, i.e., precisely the FAIL condition both drafts named for themselves.

**Do the drafts answer it?**

*Partially, and they know it — which is the most damning thing.* Both drafts *name this exact objection as their failure condition* and then decline to defeat it, downgrading the verdict to "PASS, conditionally" / "won on balance, not decisively." That is not me catching them; that is them catching themselves and shipping anyway.

Where they have a real answer (and I have to concede it is not *nothing*): the **logical-form argument for (R)** — that the second person is dyadic where the residue is monadic, and is studied in linguistics/indexicals/developmental psychology *independently* of the free-will debate. That independent attestation is the strongest thing in either draft, because it means the address-structure was not invented to dodge me. The [reactive] draft deploys this better than [compat]. My reply stands — independent attestation of a *structure* does not show the structure is *irreducible*; map-coloring is studied independently too and reduces fine — but this is the one place the drafts make me work rather than collapsing.

Where they have *no* answer: **(O)**. Both drafts effectively give it up. [reactive] §8: "(O) passes, conditionally... would fail if reduced to 'enacted at the locus.'" [reactive]'s own pivot-draft author flagged (O) as "the weaker float" that "may not clear the bar... without smuggling in dualism." Correct. Conceding supervenience while claiming withdrawal-without-subtraction-of-fact is incoherent, and no draft resolves it. (O) is dead. On my reading the honest verdict is: **(R) survives as contested-not-refuted on the strength of one argument (dyadic logical form, independently attested); (O) fails.** A chapter that needs *both* floats to clear the FAIL condition therefore does not clearly pass — and the drafts' own "fails iff *both* fall" framing ([compat] §9) is a hedge, because a single surviving, still-contested float is not the robust two-legged PASS the chapter advertises.

**Net:** The drafts answer my objection to (R) well enough to stay alive (logical form + independent attestation — the reduction is not *demonstrated*, so I have not won outright there). They do not answer it for (O) at all; (O) collapses into supervenient dynamics-tracking, which is the residue restated in the intentional idiom. The chapter's advertised two-legged PASS is really a one-and-a-half-legged stand, and both drafts half-admit it. The strongest thing I can say for them is that they lose *honestly*; the strongest thing I can say against them is that "losing honestly" on (O) is still losing, and (R)'s survival rests entirely on one move — the irreducibility of dyadic address — that I have a live, unrefuted reduction of.
