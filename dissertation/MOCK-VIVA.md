## 3. The witness-independence finding — decisive, and answered squarely

The methodologist's challenge is the one that decides whether the thesis's strongest epistemic move survives. It does, *in one form*, and fails *in the form the contribution statement leans on*. There are **two convergences** wearing one word.

**(1) CROSS-DOMAIN convergence — SURVIVES.** Metaphysics (I-II), metaethics (VI), and security architecture (VII-VIII) arriving at the same no-aseity structure is genuine abductive evidence. A metaethicist and a security architect "share no model and no vocabulary" (THESIS.md 1078-79); when distinct disciplinary apparatus lands on one structure, "both met a real edge" carries real, defeasible weight. The one caveat — same author synthesizing all three — lowers but does not void it. The Conclusion's prose (1077-1084) actually describes *this* convergence, and for it the prose is defensible.

**(2) ADVERSARIAL-WORKFLOW convergence — DOES NOT SURVIVE.** The forcing verdict, Remainder-Law test, and type-B analysis "all returning membrane-as-core," called "three independent adjudications" (00-CONTRIBUTION.md 187-88, CONSOLIDATION.md §1), are **etiologically correlated**: same author, same corpus, same load-bearing P1, same membrane spine, same adversarial scaffold. They asked *different questions of the same substrate* — which reveals internal (in)consistency but cannot manufacture mind-independence. And **REMAINDER-LAW-TEST.md line 103 concedes one witness is forward-constructed**: "The Remainder Law is therefore a forward-constructed demarcation criterion built on the corpus's ontology for this experiment, not a verbatim corpus artifact." A criterion built to test the thesis is not an independent witness *to* the thesis. One witness interrogated three times under three labels is Deuteronomy 19:15's single witness, not its two or three.

The thesis convicts itself: Movement VII states "the agreement a single mind reaches with itself confirms nothing" (1084), applies it ruthlessly to the adversary, and **silently exempts its own workflow convergence.** That asymmetry is unprincipled as written.

**The fix is textual and fully in reach:** (a) split the two convergences wherever "three independent adjudications" appears; (b) re-label the workflow verdicts "methodologically independent, etiologically correlated"; (c) move the forward-construction concession from the appendix to the Conclusion and Contribution; (d) state the principled asymmetry or concede there is none and downgrade workflow convergence to internal-consistency evidence. **Crucially, the defensible core does not need the workflow-convergence claim** — it stands on the cross-domain convergence plus its own arguments. The candidate is over-banking a weak chip when the strong chips already win the hand.
