# Opening Defense Statement

My dissertation is *Conferred Existence*. Its single premise is no-aseity: nothing possesses its own existence; to be is to be conferred, received, re-said at each instant. The one structure it finds at every level is the membrane: the ownerless seam that conditions a whole domain and is itself not an item within it; the exception that everything crosses and that does not cross itself.

I want to begin by naming exactly what I am defending, and what I am not defending.

The defensible core of the dissertation has four parts. First, the membrane framing: in security architecture, authentication gives an “is,” while authorization gives an “ought.” I treat that as an instance of Pigden’s autonomy of the deontic. This is a framing, not an identity claim. The separation between authentication and authorization is prior art, above all in ABLP, and the deontic background belongs to von Wright, Searle, and the Hare-Hudson line of debate. My contribution is to name the bridge as deontic and draw out the consequences: no increase in descriptive measurement, however strong, supplies the permission operator.

Second, I defend the thin-index seam. Wherever conferral occurs, there is a tokened occurrence, and that occurrence is indexed. That result is forced non-circularly at the conventional level. I do not claim here to have derived the thick perspectival index. I claim that the thin seam is not optional once an act of conferral is occurring.

Third, I defend the post-revision Remainder Law. The original version over-rejected; the Bell case exposed that. The repaired version rewrites clause iii as a provability condition: whether the relevant proposition is derivable from the separated descriptions under the governing theory’s inferential closure. That is a real correction, not a hidden patch. The filter is now weaker than I first wanted, but clearer and more defensible.

Fourth, I defend the arity-gap argument in the free-will material. Sourcehood is not a one-place property possessed by an agent in isolation. It is conferred in a two-place, second-personal relation. Responsibility, answerability, and address cannot be captured by a purely monadic control vocabulary. Sourcehood is therefore conferred, not causa sui.

The central result, stated honestly, is structural seity, not aseity. The seam is real as structure, but it is not self-grounding. The keystone forcing argument is FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY-ONLY. The larger ambition, that the explanatory gap is relocated to the conferral-seam and forced there, remains a conditional and undischarged aspiration. I can declare it, defend it dialectically, and show why it is attractive. I cannot claim to have proven it.

That boundary matters. The thick index is declared, not derived. Type-B physicalism is declined, not refuted; Chalmers 2006 gives powerful pressure against the phenomenal concept strategy, but it does not prove type-B false. At the ultimate level, I concede the Madhyamaka point: the seam does not survive as an ultimate item. And Advaita Vedanta’s self-established Atman remains an undefeated rival at that ultimate level. My thesis brackets that standoff. It does not pretend to have beaten it.

So the evidence I rely on is not within-corpus agreement. One research program agreeing with itself is not independent confirmation. The genuine evidence is cross-domain convergence: metaethics and security architecture, sharing no model, arrive at the same structure. Pigden’s deontic autonomy and ABLP-style authorization logic meet at the same membrane. That convergence is moderate evidence, not a proof, but it is real evidence.

I also want to state the integrity condition plainly. This work was developed with substantial AI assistance as research substrate. I do not minimize that. The load-bearing claims have been independently re-derived from primary sources and are recorded in the provenance ledger as claims I own and can defend. That disclosure is not external to the thesis. It is an instance of it. The substrate can generate, test, and organize arguments; it cannot perform the authored act of standing behind them for me.

I therefore invite the committee’s hardest questions. I am ready to defend the thick index on the primary Madhyamaka texts, especially the *Prasannapada* and *Vigrahavyavartani*. I am ready to discuss the Advaita standoff. I am ready to specify the membrane’s exact novelty against ABLP and the deontic tradition. And I am ready to answer provenance questions directly.

That is the dissertation I am defending: not a proof of aseity’s impossibility at every possible level, but a bounded argument for conferred existence in the conventional order, where the seam that grants standing never owns the standing it grants.
