I'm the subjectless-meaning opponent. Here's my attack, my strongest objection, and an honest verdict on whether the drafts answer it.

## The strongest objection: the distinction is engineered to be unfalsifiable, and the "predictive" defense is circular

Both substantive drafts (phenom, hermeneutic) stake everything on the **semantic/existential distinction** — concede *Bedeutung* to Millikan/Dennett/Frege, keep *Bedeutsamkeit* for the locus. They anticipate the ad-hoc charge and answer it identically: the distinction is *not* stipulated because it's a "phenomenological invariant identified independently" (phenom §4.3) by three marks — normative-affective signature, first-person ineliminability, Heideggerian founding — and because it *predicts* the orthogonality results in Millikan/Dennett, and "tautologies predict nothing."

This defense fails, and here is the exact mechanism.

**1. The "independent identification" is question-begging at the source.** All three marks are *drawn from the phenomenological tradition whose central commitment is that significance is subject-relative.* Heidegger's *Bedeutsamkeit* is defined as the for-the-sake-of-which of Dasein — it has the locus built into its definition. "First-person ineliminability" presupposes that there is an ineliminable first person, which is the conclusion. The "normative-affective signature" is glossed as "mattering, which is never inert" — but *mattering* was already defined as significance-*for-someone*. So the three "independent" marks are not independent of each other or of the thesis; they are three restatements of the same subject-relativity, sourced from a tradition that postulates it. Calling them "a joint in the world" identified "by a different tradition for different reasons" (phenom §4) is false: it's the *same* tradition (phenomenology) supplying the carving knife to phenomenology's own conclusion. The hermeneutic draft is more honest here — it concedes (§9) that the locus-necessity half "is continuous with the residual aseity" — but it doesn't see that this concession is fatal to the *independence* claim, not just to the floating-free claim.

**2. The "prediction" is not a prediction — it's a redescription of a concession.** The phenom draft's proudest move (§9): "an invariant that predicts independent results is not a tautology." But what does it "predict"? That Millikan's proper functions and Dennett's patterns are *orthogonal to mattering*. Look at how that orthogonality is established (phenom §5, hermeneutic §3): by **conceding** semantic meaning to them and then **defining** existential meaning as the residue they don't touch. The "orthogonality result" is not an independent finding the theory predicted — it is the *concession restated*. You cannot first hand Millikan the entire domain of content "for the sake of the sharpest test," then announce that your theory predicted she'd have nothing to say about mattering. Of course she doesn't — you gave her a domain you pre-defined to exclude it. That's not prediction; it's the construction of a disjoint partition and then pointing at the partition. A teleosemanticist's *modesty* ("I theorize content, not phenomenal significance" — hermeneutic §3) is read as *confirmation*; it is nothing of the kind. Millikan's silence on "mattering" is consistent with mattering being subjectless-but-unaddressed-by-her, exactly as much as with mattering being subject-requiring. The drafts treat her self-limitation as evidence for their reading when it is evidence for neither.

**3. Now the part the drafts never seriously attempt: the positive teleosemantic/functional reduction of mattering itself.** Both drafts admit, with real honesty, that *this* is the failure condition. Phenom §9: "if existential meaning is reducible without remainder to the semantic... the chapter fails." Hermeneutic flags the same gap in its closing note ("a sufficiently clever Dennettian"). But naming the failure condition is not surviving it, and the drafts substitute the former for the latter. So let me actually run the reduction they declined to defeat:

**Mattering naturalized, no subject required.** Take welfare and significance functionally. A state of affairs *matters to* a system S iff it stands in the right functional-dispositional relation to S's goal-directed organization — i.e., it is a condition whose obtaining or failing-to-obtain the system's selected mechanisms are organized to detect, pursue, or avoid, such that S's behavior is counterfactually sensitive to it across the normal range. This is Millikan's proper-function machinery applied not to *content* but to *interests*. The bacterium's reaching anoxic water *matters to the bacterium* in precisely this sense: it is the proper-function success-condition of the magnetosome-guided system, and the bacterium is organized around it. No "for-itself," no phenomenal interiority, no inhabited locus — just an organism with a teleological organization for which some outcomes are functionally better and worse. Dennett completes it: from the design stance / intentional stance, *welfare* is a real pattern — the set of conditions under which the system flourishes-qua-the-system-it-is — as objectively ascribable as a center of gravity, and as little in need of an inhabitant.

The drafts' one reply is the **"as-if/real collapse"** (phenom §5): "an as-if mattering is not a faint mattering, it is *no* mattering; there is no instrumentalism about whether your life matters to you." This is the entire load-bearing wall, and it is **asserted, not argued**. It works by quietly substituting the human case (where there *is* a phenomenal first person who would object to the instrumentalist) for the general case (the bacterium, the AI, the suffering-incapable goal-system) where the functionalist *denies the premise*. The functionalist's claim is not "the bacterium has a faint as-if welfare"; it is "welfare *just is* the functional-dispositional fact, and the phenomenal 'undergoing' you insist on is either (a) itself a further functional fact about self-monitoring/global-broadcast, hence still subjectless at the base, or (b) an explanatorily idle addition." Saying "there is no instrumentalism about whether your life matters to *you*" smuggles in the very *you* whose necessity is at issue. The drafts mistake **a strong human intuition** for **an argument that the intuition tracks a non-functional fact.** That is the *qualia*-intuition relocated to the deontic-affective register (which phenom §4.2 explicitly admits it's doing) — and it inherits all the *qualia* debate's unsettledness, including the live functionalist option that the "remainder" is cognitively illusory.

**4. The killer: the distinction, far from being principled, does the *ad hoc relocation* it accuses the framework of avoiding.** The whole no-aseity framework is a universal solvent (phenom §2 admits "a solvent that dissolves everything dissolves the beaker"). Confronted with the one domain — content/representation — where subjectless theories are strongest and best-developed, the framework *concedes that entire domain* and redraws its thesis around the remainder. That is textbook ad hoc relocation: a theory threatened by counterexamples in region R redefines its claim to exclude R and renames the excluded region "merely semantic." The phenom draft's §4 *celebrates* this as "not a retreat to a defensible perimeter" — but retreating to a perimeter and decorating it is exactly what it is. The "founding relation" (existential founds semantic, §4.3) is asserted to reverse the direction of the objection, but that founding claim is *itself* the phenomenological thesis under dispute; it cannot be the neutral ground that adjudicates it. The hermeneutic draft at least *earns* parts (its refusal to grant Wolf the isomorphism for free, §6, is genuinely better philosophy), but it cannot earn the founding relation either, because nothing in Millikan or Dennett requires reading *Bedeutung* as founded on *Bedeutsamkeit* — that's a phenomenological import, not a result.

## On the SEMANTIC vs EXISTENTIAL distinction specifically (where you asked me to press hardest)

Is it principled or ad hoc relocation? **Ad hoc, on the drafts as written**, for the conjoined reason above: the distinction's criteria are drawn from the tradition that presupposes the conclusion, and the distinction is deployed precisely to wall off the region where the opponents are strongest. A *principled* version would have to satisfy a test neither draft offers: **specify a property of existential meaning, statable in vocabulary neutral between functionalist and phenomenologist, that the functional-dispositional account demonstrably cannot capture — and show the gap is a real remainder, not a redescription of the intuition that there's a gap.** Both drafts gesture at this (the "remainder no functional reduction reaches") but neither *exhibits* the remainder; they *name* it and rest on the intuition's strength.

## Can "mattering" be naturalized without a subject? 

Yes, to the degree that *welfare, interests, and significance-for* are functional-dispositional facts about goal-directed organization — and the burden is on the locus-theorist to show there is a residue *beyond* (a) functional welfare and (b) the further functional fact of phenomenal self-monitoring, that is non-functional. The drafts assert this residue (the "deontic-affective first person"); they do not isolate it from the functionalist's two-tier reply. Until they do, "mattering needs a subject" is the *qualia* hard-problem intuition wearing axiological clothes — possibly true, but not *established*, and certainly not the falsifiable, independently-jointed result the spiral's PASS verdict requires.

## Do the drafts answer the objection? Verdict per draft.

- **[analytic] — does not engage.** It declines to write the chapter, reframing the task as resisting the embedding "corpus authority." Whatever the merits of that refusal, it contributes **zero** to defending the locus claim against teleosemantics. As a response to *my* objection: absent.

- **[phenom] — sees the objection, names it as the failure condition, does not defeat it.** Its §9 is admirably explicit: PASS is declared *conditional on* "the existential/semantic distinction being a real joint and not a verbal one," and it states the exact reduction that would defeat it. But it then rests the survival of that condition on the **as-if/real collapse**, which (per my point 3) is an unargued assertion that begs the human first-person into the general case. So the draft **correctly locates** my objection and **honestly refuses to claim it's beaten** — but its actual reply is the weakest link. It earns a *conditional* PASS only by making the condition carry the unargued premise. By its own standard, that's a FAIL it has dressed as parole.

- **[hermeneutic] — engages best, concedes most, and the concession sinks it.** It refuses cheap moves (won't grant Wolf the isomorphism; won't reinterpret Frankl into agreement), which is real intellectual honesty. Crucially, §9 concedes that the **necessity-of-locus half "is continuous with the residual aseity"** and that the spiral "inhabits" rather than dissolves its last ground. That concession, which it treats as survivable (the *occasionalism* of meaning supposedly floats free), is actually the admission my objection needs: if locus-necessity just *is* the inherited un-grounded ground restated, then the "principled distinction" is the residue wearing phenomenological clothes — which is the ad-hoc-relocation charge, conceded. Its escape hatch (the occasionalism prediction — "withdrawal without subtraction of fact") is the **one genuinely new and falsifiable claim in either draft**, and notably it is *not* about the semantic/existential distinction at all — it's a claim about the *temporal structure* of significance-loss. But it too is functionally reducible: "significance drains while facts hold steady" is well-described as a change in the system's *evaluative/motivational state* (a functional fact about the valuing apparatus) with no change in the *represented facts* — depression as anhedonia is the textbook functional dissociation of welfare-state from world-state, no inhabited locus required. So the draft's strongest surviving plank is *also* naturalizable, and it didn't see that.

**Bottom line:** My strongest objection is that the semantic/existential distinction is an ad hoc relocation whose "principled" defense (independent identification + prediction) is circular, and that mattering is naturalizable as functional-dispositional welfare with the drafts' sole rebuttal — the as-if/real collapse — being an unargued transplant of the *qualia* intuition. **No draft answers it.** The phenom draft *names* it precisely and concedes it as the live failure condition without defeating it; the hermeneutic draft *concedes the load-bearing half outright* and its one free-floating claim (occasionalism) is itself reducible; the analytic draft doesn't enter the ring. The honest verdict the drafts' own test demands is **FAIL** — the distinction is verbal until someone exhibits the remainder in neutral vocabulary, and neither draft does.
