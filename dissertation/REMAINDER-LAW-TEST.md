# Adjudication — Does the REMAINDER LAW demarcate, or is it a tautology?

## Verdict: SALVAGEABLE (discriminates only with a stated revision to clause iii)

The corpus's two verdicts disagreed, and the experiment shows **both were partly wrong**. The novelty-verdict (Remainder Law = genuine contribution, "the Popper move") overclaimed; the forcing-verdict (Law = "definitional redescription wearing the costume of a measurement") was directionally right about the conclusion but wrong about the mechanism. The blind battery resolves it: the Law as written does not discriminate, but it fails by **over-rejection**, not by tautological over-acceptance — and that failure is localized to one operationalization defect (clause iii) that a stated revision repairs.

---

## 1. What the blind grades show (the scoreboard)

| Intended | Cases | Consensus RESULT | Consensus REDESCRIPTION | Split |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RESULT | C1,C2,C3,C4,C5,C6,C14,C15 (8) | **0** | 5 (C1,C3,C4,C6,C15) | 3 (C2,C5,C14) |
| REDESCRIPTION | C7–C13,C16,C17,C18 (10) | — | **10** | 0 |

- **REDESCRIPTION recall: 10/10 (perfect).**
- **RESULT recall: 0/8 consensus** (3 split at best).
- **Per-instance agreement with intended: 25/36 (69%).** Every one of the 11 disagreements is a **false negative** (genuine result called redescription). **Zero false positives.**

The Law is a one-sided sieve: it says REDESCRIPTION reliably and almost never confidently says RESULT.

---

## 2. The decisive divergences (the CATALOG's "too strong" falsifier fired)

The CATALOG lists as a falsification condition: *"a P failing a clause that judges call a genuine result (too strong)."* This fired on the three cleanest positive controls in the battery:

- **C1 — CHSH/Bell violation** (intended RESULT by clause iii). Blind: `[ii=p,iii=f]` & `[ii=f,iii=f]` → REDESCRIPTION. Bell's theorem **is** the proposition that no local-hidden-variable inventory of the two particles closes the correlation. That is a *proved* irreducibility, the paradigm of what clause (iii) is supposed to certify. Both graders failed clause (iii) anyway.
- **C3 — joint-force first-order entailment** (RESULT by iii). Blind: REDESCRIPTION (one grade failed ii, one failed iii). The joint-force condition is *definitionally* relational and provable by formal derivation; it still failed.
- **C4 — single-particle quantum interference** (RESULT by iii). Blind: REDESCRIPTION via iii both times.

**Diagnosis.** Clause (iii) — "P is NOT closed by any third-person inventory of A and B" — is **not blind-operationalizable as written**. It quantifies over *all possible* inventories; a determined grader reads "could a complete-enough inventory close it?" and answers "yes" by imagination, failing (iii) on everything, *including theorems whose content is precisely that no inventory closes the gap.* When Bell's theorem fails your irreducibility clause, the clause is not measuring irreducibility.

This is **too-strong**, not too-weak. There were **zero** too-weak divergences (no failed-clause P waved through as a result). The Law's entire error budget is over-rejection.

---

## 3. The forcing-verdict's decisive test — and why it resolves AGAINST the literal tautology charge

The forcing-verdict's challenge: an honest demarcation must be **able to return REDESCRIPTION on at least some actually-conferring cases**; if it "fires RESULT seam-found on every actual-conferral case by the definition (P1)," it is a tautology.

**Test result: the Law returned REDESCRIPTION on every conferring case.** All four conferring-challenge cases were blind-graded REDESCRIPTION by **both** graders, every time via **clause (ii)** (P entailed by the bare existence of S under P1):

| Case | Blind grade 1 | Blind grade 2 |
|---|---|---|
| C7 (the exact forcing target) | `[i=p,ii=f,iii=f]` | `[i=f,ii=f,iii=f]` |
| C16 | `[i=p,ii=f,iii=u]` | `[i=p,ii=f,iii=f]` |
| C17 | `[i=u,ii=f,iii=f]` | `[i=f,ii=f,iii=f]` |
| C18 | `[i=u,ii=f,iii=f]` | `[i=p,ii=f,iii=f]` |

C7 is the precise claim the forcing argument flagged ("every conferral-event is identical, under a second description, to a non-zero occupied seam-event"). Under P1 ("conferral is occupation"), non-zero occupancy is entailed by the bare existence of S, so clause (ii) fails and the Law correctly returns REDESCRIPTION: **the forced-seam claim is P1 restated, not a result the Law earns.**

**So the literal tautology charge is refuted.** The forcing-verdict said the Law fires *RESULT* on every conferral case "by analytical necessity." The blind data shows the opposite: it fires *REDESCRIPTION* on every conferral case. The forcing-verdict's **conclusion** (the Law adds no surplus over P1 here) is right; its **stated mechanism** is empirically wrong.

But this does **not** rescue the novelty-verdict, because of §2: the Law also returns REDESCRIPTION on the cleanest *non-conferral genuine results*. The Law agrees with P1 on the conferring case not because it has uniquely detected a redescription there, but because it returns REDESCRIPTION almost everywhere hard. Its agreement with P1 is therefore **uninformative** — which is the forcing-verdict's deeper point, vindicated by a route the forcing-verdict did not name.

## 4. Clause independence — the collapse falsifier did NOT fire

The CATALOG: if "(ii) and (iii) [are] not independent" the Law "collapses to the bare explanatory gap." The blind data exhibits **both** directions, so the clauses are not coextensive:

- **pass-ii / fail-iii** (present): C1.g1, C4.g1, C9 (both grades), C12.g1, C13.g2, C14.g2, C15.g1 — all `[ii=p,iii=f]`.
- **pass-iii / fail-ii** (present): C5.g2, C6.g1, C15.g2 — all `[ii=f,iii=p]`.

Because both a pass-ii/fail-iii and a pass-iii/fail-ii instance occur, **the clauses are independent** and do not collapse to the bare gap. **Caveat:** the cases *designed* to showcase direction-b as RESULTS (C14, C15) were over-rejected (C14 split, C15 → REDESCRIPTION), so independence is demonstrable at the clause level but the demonstration is contaminated by the global over-rejection. Independence is necessary but not sufficient for discrimination; the Law has the former and lacks the latter as written.

---

## 5. Why SALVAGEABLE and not DISCRIMINATES or TAUTOLOGY

- **Not DISCRIMINATES:** 0/8 RESULT recall; the cleanest positive controls (Bell, entailment, interference) destroyed. A criterion that cannot certify a theorem of irreducibility as a result is not sorting results from redescriptions.
- **Not TAUTOLOGY (in the charged sense):** the literal charge is "fires RESULT on everything that confers." The Law fires REDESCRIPTION on every conferring case (§3), and clauses (ii)/(iii) are independent (§4). It is not vacuously permissive. Its defect is the mirror image — vacuous *restrictiveness* on clause (iii).
- **SALVAGEABLE:** the entire failure is one operationalization bug in clause (iii). With it fixed, the target sorting (RESULT on C1/C3/C4; REDESCRIPTION on C7/C16/C17/C18) is recoverable.

---

## 6. The required revision

**R1 — Re-write clause (iii) as a provability condition, not an existential-over-inventories condition.**
> (iii*) P is NOT entailed by the conjunction of the complete intrinsic descriptions of A and B taken separately, **under the inferential closure of the governing theory** — equivalently, there exists a proof that no such separate-description conjunction yields P.

Under (iii*), a *proved* irreducibility (Bell's theorem; the joint-force lemma; the interference-from-coherence argument) **passes**, and only the *absence* of such a proof fails it. This repairs C1/C3/C4 to RESULT as intended and eliminates the false-negative collapse, because the grader can no longer fail the clause by merely imagining a richer inventory — they must exhibit a derivation of P from the separated descriptions, which for Bell, joint-force entailment, and quantum interference provably does not exist.

**R2 — Report clause (ii) failure separately and treat it as the decisive, premise-level disqualifier.**
A clause-(ii) failure (P entailed by the bare existence of S, i.e., by a stipulated premise such as P1) is a *different kind* of REDESCRIPTION from a clause-(iii) failure (P closed by inventory). Reporting them separately distinguishes the **correct** conferring-challenge verdicts (REDESCRIPTION via ii — the Law doing real work against P1's restatements) from the **spurious** over-rejections (REDESCRIPTION via the old iii). This is what licenses the claim that the Law "does the Popper move on induction": it falsifiably separates *"P1 is true"* (a metaphysical question the Law cannot settle) from *"this claim has surplus content over P1"* (which clause ii settles: no).

With R1+R2, the Law would return RESULT on C1/C3/C4/C2/C5/C14 and REDESCRIPTION on C6?–C18 conferring/redescription cases — i.e., it would discriminate.

---

## 7. Consequence for the dissertation's contribution

- **With R1+R2 adopted:** the second pillar **stands**, rescued. The Remainder Law becomes a genuine demarcation instrument that (a) certifies provable relational results, (b) rejects definitional restatements of P1 via clause (ii), and (c) has independent, non-collapsing clauses. The novelty-verdict's "Popper move" framing is then defensible — but only after the clause-(iii) fix, not as originally written.
- **Without the revision:** the second pillar does **not** stand as an independent contribution. As written, the Law's only reliable behavior is clause-(ii) rejection of premise-entailed claims, which is **a restatement of P1's own non-surplus condition**, not a discovered law. The forcing-verdict's bottom line then holds (for the conferring case the Law earns nothing), and the dissertation's contribution **shrinks toward the membrane / conferral core**: no-aseity, conferral-binds-conferrer, and the trained-on-testimony seal — the relational-status framing that survives without the demarcation criterion.

**Truth over approval:** the corpus's novelty-verdict was too generous (kept a Law that destroys its own positive controls); the forcing-verdict was right to be suspicious but misdiagnosed the failure as RESULT-on-everything when the blind evidence shows REDESCRIPTION-on-everything-hard. The honest resolution is neither "keep" nor "tautology" but **demote-and-revise**: the second pillar is salvageable, and the precise repair is the clause-(iii) provability rewrite.
