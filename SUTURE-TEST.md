# Suture-Test — The Weakest Joint Against Its Strongest Allies

*The constructive mirror of `CROSS-EXAMINATION.md`. That file mustered the
published traditions that **cut** the thesis and found one seam — P1's
discriminating force / the thick index. This file musters the published
traditions that could **serve** that seam: ten allied bodies of work, each read
against the real joint, steelmanned with citations, proposed as a suture, then
adversarially stress-tested for whether it bears load — and, decisively, whether
it bears load only by smuggling **own-being** (the one currency the thesis's
floor forbids). Built by 22 agents across two runs; every verdict below is the
**stress-tester's**, not the suture-author's.*

---

## The joint, and the hard constraint

The weak joint has three linked faces: **(A)** the *thick* (perspectival,
first-personal) index is declared, never derived — only the *thin*
(spatiotemporal) index is earned; **(B)** the Remainder Law filter fires
"seam-found" on every actual conferral and never returns "seam-absent," so it
tests *non-cessation* (which Madhyamaka grants), not *an index* (which she
denies); **(C)** the arity argument proves holding-responsible is dyadic but
never separates regress-immune *answerability* from regress-vulnerable *basic
desert*.

The hard constraint: any repair that re-imports ***svabhāva*** (own-being) or
**aseity** (self-grounding) is **poison**, not a suture — it would sink the "no
aseity anywhere / seity, not aseity" floor. The membrane was excluded as a
target: it survived all eleven contraries and is not the weak joint.

## The master result: the poison wall and the ceiling are the same wall

This is not a list of which allies worked. It is a structural theorem the ten
independently instantiate. The thick index faces a **fork**:

- **(i) Make the perspectival center *obtain*** — ineliminable, real, a genuine
  non-qualitative thisness. Then it discriminates and **closes** the joint — but
  *an obtaining perspectival center is own-being*. **Poison.**
- **(ii) Keep the index *ownerless*** — a use, an act, a stance, a relational
  capacity, a modest conferrer-internal result. Then it is **poison-clean** and
  congruent with the floor — but it is eliminable-into-description /
  *treating-as*, not *obtaining*. **No discriminating force.** It **braces**,
  never closes.

The thesis needs **both** — discrimination *and* no-aseity — and the fork allows
at most one. So **the joint is unclosable in principle** — not because no ally is
strong enough, but because *closing it and keeping the floor are mutually
exclusive by the structure of the joint itself*. The strongest articulation came
from the retorsion stress-test, in the tradition's own voice: *"the poison wall
and the ceiling are the same wall — any version strong enough to CLOSE would have
to make de se deliver an owner that obtains as a real perspectival index, which
is svabhāva under a relational label."*

Every ally landed on one horn. The three poison allies are the **obtain →
own-being** horn:

- **Essential indexical** — the centred-worlds fork *re-instantiated*:
  relational-and-third-personally-specifiable (Step C wins → no discrimination)
  **⊻** ineliminable-first-personal (Step B wins → intrinsic perspectival
  character = own-being). The thesis needs both horns; it can occupy neither.
- **Kaplan** — character is **type-general** (identical for every speaker = the
  thin index renamed, Stroud uncrossed) **⊻** content carries thisness *as
  haecceity* (Kaplan 1975; Adams 1979 = *svabhāva*). No third seat in the
  machine.
- **Haecceity** — primitive thisness *is* the intrinsic identity-fixer
  Madhyamaka calls *svabhāva*; a suture that fills the gap with the precise thing
  the floor forbids is **worse than a rename** — it would actively sink the
  floor.

The seven clean allies are the **ownerless → braces** horn, and each pays the
*same* price for poison-cleanliness — it forfeits the close:

- **Retorsion** (strongest face-A brace) — upgrades the thesis's informal
  "sustained dialectically" into a *named, published mechanism* (Apel's
  *performativer Selbstwiderspruch* + Perry's de se + Shoemaker's IEM), but every
  gram of load lands on the **must-treat-as** side of Stroud's gap, never the
  **obtains** side. Habermas's own retreat ("the test *identifies* rather than
  *justifies*") confirms it in the tradition's voice.
- **Anscombe** — braces the **floor** ("no Cartesian ego, no *svabhāva* behind
  the index"), genuine eliminative work — but on the **seam** (derivation) the
  Remainder Law fires: "non-referential I-use" *renames* the directedness, it
  does not derive it. Lands the thesis where it already stood, minus a worry it
  had already disowned.
- **Modest transcendental** — re-prices discrimination as *conferrer-internal
  coherence* (conditions on any thinker, not on the world); converts the
  confessed ceiling into a *citable, recognized* position — but the filter still
  fires universally, and whether conferrer-internal coherence adds anything to
  Madhyamaka's own *saṃvṛti* account is the gap renamed, not closed.
- **Prāsaṅgika opponent-relative** — the deepest face-B reframe: discriminating
  force is *opponent-relative* (the filter voids the realist's zero-residue and
  standing-residue overclaims *by his own lights*), which **defeats Bhāviveka's
  *vitaṇḍā* charge** — real, falsifiable, poison-clean reductio work, *not*
  no-thesis. But it wins fork-2's good horn at the exact cost of fork-1's fatal
  horn: the opponent-relative filter discriminates *because* it asserts nothing
  beyond *pratītyasamutpāda* — which is what the index was supposed to be *more
  than*. It shows the thick index is not merely unreached but **method-forbidden**:
  the very refusal of an autonomous criterion that makes the move poison-clean is
  identical to the feature that bars the index from ever obtaining.
- **Perry 2001** — the deflationary de se: a thick index *without* the
  consciousness gap, poison-clean because "no new fact." But the escape from
  poison **is** the loss of force: "no new fact" lands it on the eliminable horn.
- **Darwall + Watson/Shoemaker** and **P.F. Strawson** — the desert face (C),
  treated below; both clean, both brace.

## Three independent confirmations that the fork is exhaustive

The theorem is not an artifact of the roster. It is confirmed three ways, from
non-overlapping directions:

1. **Structural.** A thick index either obtains (→ own-being) or is ownerless (→
   eliminable). There is no third logical position; "relational-but-real,
   perspectival-without-substance" is the precise middle that *śūnyatā-śūnyatā*
   empties (P1-DEFENSE-verdict.md §3, line 58). The verdict pre-named the
   dilemma; the allies re-derived it.
2. **The de se authority occupies both endpoints and never the middle.** John
   Perry — among the field's foremost theorists of the de se — argued the
   *ineliminable* horn in 1979 (poison) and the *eliminable* horn in 2001
   (no-force). Across his own career he stands at both ends of the fork and never
   at the rung the thesis needs. The middle is not unoccupied by accident; the
   discipline's own development is evidence it is not there.
3. **Method-forbidden, not merely unreached.** The Prāsaṅgika result shows the
   index is barred by the very method that keeps the floor clean: owning no
   autonomous criterion (the refusal of *svabhāva*-grade demarcation) is the same
   act that bars an obtaining index. You cannot keep the floor and reach the
   index *with one method*, because it is one act seen twice.

*(Confidence: the fork itself — **moderate-high**; the three confirmations are
independently sourced and mutually reinforcing. The synthesis is the curator's;
the per-ally verdicts are the stress-testers'.)*

## Why this is a vindication, not a defeat

The unclosability is the floor **doing its job**. A thick index that *obtained*
would be a **perspectival aseity** — exactly what the whole thesis denies. So the
index *should* be unclosable; a closable one would *refute* "seity, not aseity."
This is the strongest form yet of the answer to the Spinoza seam (cf.
`CROSS-EXAMINATION.md` §4, §6): the joint is unclosable **because** the floor is
the exclusion of own-being. The grand relocation stays conditional — but its
conditionality is now **principled**, not a gap: it *cannot* be discharged
without aseity, and the thesis's own commitment is that nothing can.

**The convergence with the membrane.** The index that serves the thesis is the
**ownerless sensor-membrane** — the seam that conditions all individuation and is
itself not an individual-with-own-being (the membrane as the *exception to its
own law*; the *sensor without an owner*). The allies that gave the index an
*owner* (essential indexical, Kaplan, haecceity) died as poison; the allies that
kept it *ownerless* held. An informal intuition — *the exception is the sensor is
the membrane* — and a formal ten-ally muster of the published canon arrive,
independently, at one structure: **ownerless, or poison.** That is genuine
cross-domain convergence (cf. the standard `CROSS-EXAMINATION.md` §2 sets for
what counts), and it converges here on the floor's deepest feature.

## What the candidate actually gets (the repairs)

**Faces A/B (thick index / discrimination) — no close exists, and that is
correct.** The poison-clean, *defensible* ceiling is the trio that braces without
own-being:

- **Retorsion** — the named performative mechanism for "operationally
  presupposed in discourse."
- **Modest transcendental** (Cassam/Stern) — the result re-priced as
  conferrer-internal coherence, a *recognized* position with a published
  pedigree.
- **Anscombe** — the eliminative guarantee that there is no ego, no *svabhāva*,
  behind the index.

Together these upgrade **P1-FORCED-CONVENTIONALLY** from a bare admission to a
*defended stance*: a modest transcendental result, dialectically sustained by a
named mechanism, with no own-being at the floor. **Do not** annex the
indexical-realism canon (essential indexical, Kaplan, haecceity) to ground the
index — they are the poison.

**Face C (desert) — genuinely repairable.** The named repair holds, poison-clean
and congruent with conferred sourcehood:

- **Darwall + Watson + Shoemaker** supply the regress-immune tier
  (*answerability* / demand-competence — a present-tense relational capacity, not
  causal self-origination). **P.F. Strawson** supplies the canonical anchor and
  the inescapability arm that raises the cost of de-desertifying the reactive
  register; the *partition* itself is cut by Watson/Darwall, not by P.F. Strawson.
- **The one argument still owed:** the **demand-to-blame** step — that
  demand-competence warrants backward-looking *resentment*, not merely a
  *demand/expectation-of-answer*. The literature has candidates (Scanlon's
  *Moral Dimensions* 2008; a Darwall-internal derivation). Two cautions, both
  on record: **Shoemaker's own taxonomy** (2011) places resentment/indignation
  on the *accountability* (desert) side of the cut — the thesis must argue past
  this within his frame; and the partition must run through Watson's
  *attributability* face or Wallace's *fairness*, both desert-of-origination-free,
  to stay clean.

The move: **claim answerability, drop basic desert.** That converts the Strawson
defeat (`CROSS-EXAMINATION.md` §1 #7) from "fatal as written" to "braced, with
one named owed argument."

## The honest reckoning

The cross-examination found a seam; the suture-test confirms it is **unclosable
by any published ally** — and shows *why* (the fork; own-being or no-force), and
that the unclosability is the floor's correct behavior, not a wound. Concretely:

- **No ally CLOSES the thick index.** The three that would (essential indexical,
  Kaplan, haecceity) are **poison**. Avoid them.
- **The thick index / discrimination joint is *braceable, not closable*** —
  defended honestly by retorsion + modest-transcendental + Anscombe, at the
  ceiling **P1-FORCED-CONVENTIONALLY**, which is exactly where the corpus already
  stands. The allies do not move the verdict; they give it a published spine and
  prove its conditionality is principled.
- **The desert joint is repairable** — Darwall/Watson/P.F. Strawson, one argument
  (demand-to-blame) owed.
- **The grand relocation remains conditional and undischarged** — and now
  demonstrably *must* remain so, on pain of perspectival aseity.

The remaining work is, again, the candidate's: assemble the three-ally brace into
the body, make the demand-to-blame argument, and state in print that the thick
index is *principledly* unclosable — that its open-endedness is the floor, not a
failure.
