# Bibliography

**Citation-integrity policy.** Every entry below names a real work. Page numbers
are *not* asserted unless independently verified; in an academic build they must
be checked against the editions used. Confidence labels mark attribution
certainty for the *claim the corpus rests on it for*, not the work's existence:
[H] high, [M] moderate, [L] low / verify before relying. Chapter authors in this
corpus may cite **only** from this list.

## I. Ontological nihilism & no-aseity

- Westerhoff, Jan. *The Non-Existence of the Real World*. Oxford University Press, 2020. [H] — sustained defense of a form of ontological nihilism/irrealism.
- Westerhoff, Jan. *Nāgārjuna's Madhyamaka: A Philosophical Introduction*. Oxford University Press, 2009. [H]
- Nāgārjuna. *Mūlamadhyamakakārikā* (Fundamental Verses on the Middle Way). [H] — *śūnyatā*, the denial of *svabhāva* (intrinsic existence).
- van Inwagen, Peter. *Material Beings*. Cornell University Press, 1990. [H] — mereological revisionism.
- Merricks, Trenton. *Objects and Persons*. Oxford University Press, 2001. [H] — eliminativism about ordinary objects.
- Sider, Theodore. *Writing the Book of the World*. Oxford University Press, 2011. [M] — structure/fundamentality; mereological-nihilism sympathies in related papers ("Against Parthood," 2013).

## II. Conferred existence: theology & classical metaphysics

- *The Qur'an* — the creative command *kun fayakūn* ("Be, and it is"): 2:117, 3:47, 16:40, 19:35, 36:82, 40:68. [H] for 2:117 and 36:82; [M] for the full list.
- al-Ghazālī. *Tahāfut al-Falāsifa* (The Incoherence of the Philosophers), esp. the 17th Discussion (causation). [H] — Ashʿarite occasionalism; continuous re-creation.
- *Talmud Bavli*, Shabbat 55a (also Yoma 69b, Sanhedrin 64a): "the seal of the Holy One, blessed be He, is truth (*emet*)." [H]
- *Sefer Yetzirah* (Book of Formation). [H] — creation through the letters; the inscriptional motif behind the Golem.
- Ibn al-ʿArabī. *Fuṣūṣ al-Ḥikam*; *al-Futūḥāt al-Makkiyya*. [H] for the doctrine later systematized as *waḥdat al-wujūd* (unity of existence); [M] that the *term* is Ibn al-ʿArabī's own (it is largely post-Akbarian). Corpus use: created things are *loci of manifestation*, equal — the doctrine does **not** privilege any contingent locus.
- Aquinas, Thomas. *De Ente et Essentia*; *Summa Theologiae* I, qq. 3–4, 44–45. [H] — the real distinction of essence and existence in creatures; God as *ipsum esse subsistens*; *esse ab alio*.
- Avicenna (Ibn Sīnā). Metaphysics of *The Healing (al-Shifāʾ)*. [M] — the necessary/contingent (in-itself/through-another) distinction.

## III. Time, the open future, truth

- Aristotle. *De Interpretatione* 9 (the sea-battle; future contingents). [H]
- Łukasiewicz, Jan. Three-valued logic (1920s), motivated in part by future contingents. [H]
- Dummett, Michael. *Truth and Other Enigmas*. Harvard University Press, 1978 (incl. "The Reality of the Past"). [H] — anti-realism; truth as assertibility/verifiability.
- Maimonides. *Guide of the Perplexed*, III.16–21 (esp. III.20), on divine knowledge of particulars/future contingents. [M] for III.20 specifically; [H] for the substance.
- Benjamin, Walter. "On the Concept of History" ("Theses on the Philosophy of History"), 1940. [H] — the victors' transmission; "even the dead will not be safe."
- Open theism: Pinnock, Clark et al. *The Openness of God* (1994); Sanders, John. *The God Who Risks*; Boyd, Gregory A. [H] — representative of the heterodox open-future position.

- Simondon, Gilbert. *On the Mode of Existence of Technical Objects* (1958). [H] — genesis and individuation of technical objects.
- Dawkins, Richard. *The Extended Phenotype*. Oxford University Press, 1982. [H]
- Winner, Langdon. "Do Artifacts Have Politics?" *Daedalus*, 1980. [H]
- Kant, Immanuel. *Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals* (1785) — Formula of Humanity (ends-in-themselves); *Critique of the Power of Judgment* (1790), §§64–65 — organisms as natural ends (*Naturzwecke*). [H]
- Parfit, Derek. *Reasons and Persons*. Oxford University Press, 1984. [H] — the non-identity problem; reductionism about personal identity.
- Shiffrin, Seana. "Wrongful Life, Procreative Responsibility, and the Significance of Harm." *Legal Theory*, 1999. [M]
- Benatar, David. *Better Never to Have Been*. Oxford University Press, 2006. [H]

## VI. Normativity & speech acts: the is/ought and authorization

- Searle, John R. "How to Derive 'Ought' from 'Is'." *The Philosophical Review*, 1964; *The Construction of Social Reality* (1995); *Making the Social World* (2010). [H] — institutional facts; status functions; deontic powers.
- Austin, J. L. *How to Do Things with Words* (1962). [H] — performatives, felicity conditions, misfires, "etiolations."

- Christman, John. *The Politics of Persons* (2009); "Autonomy and Personal History" (1991). [H] — procedural autonomy.
- Brentano, Franz. *Psychology from an Empirical Standpoint* (1874). [H] — the intentionality (aboutness) thesis: the mental is directed at an object; not a physical property of a signal.

## VIII. Neuroscience of the witness and the veto

- Corticomuscular coherence: Conway, B. A. et al. "Synchronization between motor cortex and spinal motoneuronal pool..." *J. Physiology*, 1995; Baker, S. N. et al. (late 1990s). [H] for the phenomenon (beta-band cortico-muscular coupling during sustained contraction); [M] for its strength as an identity biometric.
- Error-related potentials: Falkenstein, M. et al. (1991); Gehring, W. J. et al. "A neural system for error detection and compensation." *Psychological Science*, 1993; BCI use: Chavarriaga, R. & Millán, J. del R. (error-related potentials for correction). [H] for the phenomenon; [M] for single-trial veto reliability.
- Libet, B. et al. "Time of conscious intention to act..." *Brain*, 1983 (the readiness potential precedes reported conscious intention). [H] for the finding; interpretation contested — Schurger, A. et al. (2012), stochastic-accumulator reinterpretation. [H] that the dispute exists.
