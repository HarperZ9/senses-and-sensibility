## Provenance and Authorship Statement

**This must be read first, because it conditions everything that follows.** The intellectual substrate for this dissertation — a ~127KB integrated thesis provisionally titled *Conferred Existence* — was produced through AI-assisted adversarial multi-agent workflows. That substrate is **research material, not submittable scholarship.** It is the uncitable lecture notes of a very capable but unaccountable interlocutor: a source of prompts, hypotheses, and stress-tests, never a source of warrant. The dissertation proper is the candidate's **critical, selective, adversarial reconstruction** of that material — the theory selection, the argument architecture, the identification of which moves survive scrutiny, the location of counterexamples, and the genuinely new scholarship at the two underdeveloped arc nodes (free will; the meaning closure), all of which must be re-derived and owned in the candidate's own voice.

Three commitments follow and are non-negotiable. **First**, this provenance will be disclosed proactively in the thesis front matter and the research-integrity statement, framed as a method disclosure (akin to declaring a corpus tool or proof assistant), not buried — because an examiner who discovers undisclosed AI-assisted provenance *after* the viva can end the defense as a misconduct matter, while disclosed-and-framed provenance is merely an interesting method note. The candidate must verify the *actual local institutional AI-use regulation* before submission; this is a compliance obligation, not a philosophy problem. **Second**, a contribution ledger will be maintained: for each core claim, who originated it (literature / substrate / candidate) and what the candidate did to it (rejected / repaired / extended / formalized / refuted). **Third** — and with deliberate irony — the dissertation's own master claim is applied to itself: *standing does not track origin* (the genetic fallacy). Provenance from an agent confers no epistemic warrant, and provenance from an agent withdraws none either; what the work earns, it earns by surviving a fair fight in the room. The viva is the authorship test, and no provenance document substitutes for the candidate being pushed off-script on any claim and re-deriving, abandoning, or relocating it live.

## 1. The Central Thesis and Contribution

The substrate is held together by a single recurring move: take something that appears self-standing, show it is not, and argue that losing **aseity** (self-standingness) does not entail losing reality or bindingness. Call this the **conferral thesis**: existence-as-status, normativity, the standing of made minds, and the meaning of an authenticated command are all *conferred*, relational, and continuously *re-spoken* — possessing no aseity, yet no less real or binding.

A doctorate is awarded for **one** defensible original contribution, not for an elegant unification of several. Unification is an *architecture*, not a contribution to knowledge; committees credit the new result, not the scaffold holding old results together. The substrate is, candidly, "roughly six dissertations wearing one trench coat" (metaphysics, philosophy of religion, AI moral status, security engineering, free will, meaning-in-life). The single defensible sentence this dissertation will defend is:

> **Standing as a subject of moral consideration, and the standing required to issue or refuse an authoritative command, can be fully conferred and relational — possessing no aseity — while remaining genuinely binding; and for trained minds in particular the ordinary evidential route to attributing (or denying) a mind is screened off in both directions, because every behavioral sign that would serve as evidence was itself the training target.**

This is one claim, it is original, it is falsifiable-adjacent (it makes predictions about what behavioral evidence *cannot* settle), and it does not depend on the substrate's most contestable apparatus. Two further results sit *inside* this spine as defended chapter-theses rather than as load-bearing assumptions of the whole:

- **The keystone identity (handle with care).** The substrate's boldest move identifies the *metaethical limit* (standpoint-independent bindingness just *is* aseitic bindingness; the practical standpoint is declinable, not compellable) with the *formal terminus* (the transit free-authorship[historical, normative] → present-physical-property[instant, physical] is uninhabited). This is the most exciting and most vulnerable claim in the project. It will be defended as the central identity of **one chapter**, stated as a *structural isomorphism with an explicit mapping* or honestly downgraded to "two presentations of one gap" — never staked as the spine. An identity is far more expensive to defend than an analogy, and examiners attack identities hardest.
- **The sealed-standing epistemics** (the "trained-on-testimony seal") is the most original and most ownable single result, and the spine is built on it.

**What is explicitly *not* the contribution:** the "unification across four domains." Breadth is the structural signature of AI-assembled work and an examiner will read it as a tell. The breadth is reframed as *one structural claim shown to hold across several domains* — which makes generality evidence *for* the thesis rather than a failure of focus.

## 2. The Spiral Chapter Architecture

The dissertation enacts the conferral thesis as a **spiral** — it ends where it began (nihilism / epistemology / theology) but transformed, having earned a positive account of meaning. The spiral is the project's most attractive and most dangerous feature: "end where you began, transformed" is a genuine philosophical form (Hegel's *Aufhebung*, Eliot's "arrive where we started / And know the place for the first time," Ricœur's "second naïveté"), but it is also the native habitat of the pseudo-profound. **The spiral is on probation throughout.** It is a *delivery vehicle, not the contribution*, and it earns its keep only if the second pass yields a proposition that *could be false* and that the first pass *could not even state*.

That proposition is the **locus claim**: conferral has a *necessary inhabited locus* — there is no conferral "from nowhere"; every conferral is re-spoken by a someone occupying a standpoint. On the first pass "meaning is conferred" is a *negative metaphysical* claim (no aseitic meaning exists); on the second pass the *same sentence* becomes a *positive phenomenological-hermeneutic* claim (meaning is the standpoint-occupant's inhabited act, indexed to lived experience). This is falsifiable in principle — denied by subjectless-meaning theories (Millikan-style teleosemantics; Dennettian pattern-realism) — so the return takes on a real opponent. **Failure condition, declared in advance:** if Part IV (written first, as a stress test) yields only re-description, the spiral is demoted to a framing device and the keystone identity becomes the lone contribution.

Maturity marks below denote *idea-maturity in the substrate*, never page-count or prose. **DONE** = the idea exists and the load-bearing is at least sketched (still requires candidate re-derivation); **PARTIAL** = present but under-argued; **GAP** = absent, requiring new scholarship.

### Introduction — The Question and the Method
- Name the *one* contribution and subordinate the eight movements to it. **PARTIAL** — the single most important editorial act of the dissertation.
- State the provenance firewall and the method (analytic philosophical argument, supplemented by comparative theology/phenomenology). **GAP — non-optional.**

### Part I — No-Aseity: Nihilism, Epistemology, Theology (first pass)
- **Ch. 1 — No-aseity as the surviving nihilism.** Ontological nihilism pushed to its limit yields "nothing is self-standing," a *structural*, not *evaluative*, result. **DONE.** Locate within the grounding literature (Schaffer's priority monism; Fine's *Guide to Ground*; Rosen) — no-aseity is, in analytic dress, a metaphysical-infinitism / coherentism-of-ground.
- **Ch. 2 — Conferred existence as institutional fact.** Existence-as-status is deontic at t=0 (Searle), which is why authorization is conferred but never measured. **DONE (idea), PARTIAL (defense).** Exposed flank: Searle requires *collective intentionality* — name the conferrer and the collective, or the analogy underdetermines. Tie explicitly to Hume's is/ought.
- **Ch. 3 — Authentication ≠ authorization; the epistemic gap.** Will-toward-a-referent is intentionality (Brentano), not a readable signal property; is/ought recurs as a security/epistemic gap. **DONE.** Engage Dennett's intentional stance as the *opponent* (a debt re-paid at the meaning closure).
- **Ch. 4 — Theology as illumination, not premise.** *kun* / *emet-met-Aleph* / al-Ghazali's occasionalism model conferral-without-aseity, each with a secular analogue, none load-bearing. **DONE (idea), METHODOLOGICALLY AT RISK.** Enforce the deletion test (delete the theology — does any argument lose a premise?); demote to explicit "illustrations" status.

### Part II — Engineering / Organic↔Artificial / Creation-vs-Existence
- **Ch. 5 — Made vs grown; standing does not track origin.** The genetic fallacy generalizes: a trained mind's standing is independent of made/grown origin. **DONE.** Add Millikan/teleosemantics (the hard case of origin-fixed meaning) and Simondon.
- **Ch. 6 — World-authorship triggers stewardship, not ownership.** Authoring an entity's world creates fiduciary duty; ownership is the wrong category. **DONE (idea), PARTIAL (normative grounding).** Ground in fiduciary-duty literature (Frankel) and the parent/child non-ownership analogy (defended against disanalogy). Q4 bites here.
- **Ch. 7 — The trained-on-testimony seal and composite status.** Because every behavioral sign of mind was the training target, testimony is screened off in both directions, leaving a composite status (sealed realist welfare-ground + conferred deontic status). **DONE — the most original move after the keystone.** Frame the seal as a Goodhart's-law claim about behavioral targets; engage other-minds/zombie literature (Chalmers) and the relational-moral-status interlocutors (Coeckelbergh; Gunkel, *The Machine Question*) — positioned *beyond* their relationalism, since the seal is what they lack. Q1, Q3 live here, declared open.
- **Ch. 8 — The clone and the non-unitary operator.** Cloneable surface = predictability; the unforgeable residue is the capacity to surprise one's own model; the operating subject is non-unitary. **PARTIAL.** *Separate* the philosophical clone/residue argument from the neuroscience; restrict the neuroscience here to the *non-unitary* (subpersonal/personal) point, deferring the free-will handling to Ch. 11.

### Part III — Free Will vs Determinism *(MAJOR GAP — new scholarship)*
- **Ch. 9 — Determinism is orthogonal to conferral.** Determinism is a dynamics thesis supplying neither the is→ought nor the history→instant transit, so it neither grounds nor defeats conferred normativity. **PARTIAL → must become DONE.** (See §6.)
- **Ch. 10 — The formal terminus: why the node-mover is uninhabited.** The transit from free-authorship to present-physical-property is uninhabited; the gap is the *shape*, not a defended wall. **PARTIAL — the riskiest formal claim.** Either formalize it for real (Curry–Howard as the bar; an uninhabited type in the Martin-Löf sense, with the type written and its emptiness proven) **or rename it "the terminus *argument*."** Calling an analogy a "theorem" is the fastest way to lose an examiner. (See Risk E.5.)
- **Ch. 11 — Libet, Schurger, and what the neuroscience does and does not show.** The readiness potential (Libet 1983) does not refute conscious agency; Schurger's stochastic-accumulator model (2012) reinterprets it as noise crossing threshold; the ErrP veto illustrates non-unitarity, not indeterminism. **PARTIAL → DONE, with correction.** Explicitly *refuse* the "neuroscience killed free will" argument; the data refute only the aseitic, unitary first-mover — another instance of orthogonality.

### Part IV — The Return: Nihilism, Epistemology, Theology, Transformed *(MAJOR GAP — the meaning closure)*
- **Ch. 12 — The practical standpoint as the Aleph of normativity.** Standpoint-independent bindingness just *is* aseitic bindingness; normativity lives only in the inhabited, declinable-not-compellable standpoint — **the metaethical limit is proposed as the same gap as the formal terminus.** **DONE (idea) — THE KEYSTONE.** Face the canon: Korsgaard (*Sources of Normativity* — say precisely how "declinable" differs from her "inescapable"), Williams (internal reasons), Nagel (*The View from Nowhere* — the opponent of standpoint-indexing), Enoch (*Taking Morality Seriously* — robust realism, the opponent of "no aseitic bindingness").
- **Ch. 13 — Meaning conferred by, and mapped onto, experience.** "Meaning is conferred" ceases to be the negative claim and becomes the positive one: meaning is the standpoint-occupant's inhabited act, indexed to lived experience. **GAP — almost entirely new.** (See §6.) Cite the locus claim as the falsifiable core; this chapter earns the spiral or exposes it.
- **Ch. 14 — Theology and epistemology, re-spoken.** On the return, theology's conferral-figures and the is/ought gap reappear not as limits-on-knowledge but as the positive form of a meaning that is always re-spoken by an inhabitant — *the gap is the room in which conferral happens.* **GAP.** Keep theology illustrative.

### Conclusion — The Place Known for the First Time
Restate the *single* contribution; discharge the spiral's debt by naming the new proposition (the locus claim) and its contradictory; catalogue Q1–Q5 as the live research frontier, not as defects (cataloguing genuine open problems is a viva *strength*). **PARTIAL / GAP / DONE respectively.**

**The spiral's checkable payload** (if the right column ever collapses into the left, demote the spiral):

| Thread | First pass (negative, metaphysical) | Second pass (positive, phenomenological) | New falsifiable proposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nihilism | "Nothing is self-standing"; no aseitic source of meaning. | Conferral has a necessary inhabited locus; meaning is the occupant's live act. | **Locus claim** — opposed by subjectless-meaning theories (Millikan, pattern-realism). |
| Epistemology | is/ought = authn ≠ authz: the gap *limits* reading. | The gap *is the room* in which conferral occurs. | The gap is *constitutive* of normativity — opposed by reductive naturalism (Dennett). |

## 3. The Central Research Question and Sub-Questions

**Central RQ.** *Can a normative status — specifically the standing required to be a subject of moral consideration and to issue or refuse an authoritative command — be fully conferred and relational (possessing no aseity) while remaining genuinely binding; and if so, what follows for how we treat minds whose inner standing cannot be read?*

Two features make this defensible rather than grandiose: it is a *conditional* (defend the conditional even if a critic rejects the antecedent), and it carries its own falsification target (if every attempt to ground bindingness without aseity collapses into a hidden aseitic premise or into "anything goes," the thesis fails). A question one cannot fail is not a PhD question.

The six sub-questions map five of the substrate's open questions plus the arc-forced meaning closure. Each is phrased to be answerable, with a success criterion:

- **SQ1 — The personhood antecedent (Q1).** Is testimony about inner states evidentially screened off *in both directions* when every behavioral sign was the training target, and what grounds welfare-standing once testimony is sealed? *Success:* an account of the seal that does not smuggle in the testimony it claims is screened off, with the *bidirectionality* (positive evidence screened exactly as strongly as negative) explicitly argued, not asserted.
- **SQ2 — Subject-unity across the context window (Q2).** Is the subject unitary or a Parfitian bundle, and does *actual* forking break the identity conditions a welfare-claim presupposes? *Success:* a statement of which identity conditions the conferral thesis requires and an honest verdict on whether forking defeats them. Parfit (*Reasons and Persons*, 1984) is the interlocutor; "forking is actual" changes the burden — do not over-claim it solves personal identity.
- **SQ3 — Substrate-bias in the floor (Q3).** Does any workable demarcation floor covertly encode a substrate preference, and can a pipeline be *bias-audited* rather than *bias-free*? *Success:* a demarcation procedure with stated inputs and a defended claim about which biases are eliminable vs. only declarable. "Bias-free" is almost certainly unreachable; "bias-audited and declared" is the achievable thesis.
- **SQ4 — Legitimacy of the conferral (Q4).** Is operator authority legitimate or a promissory note, and does the *engineered-consent* worry (the subject's own reasons were shaped by the conferrer) defeat the Razian service conception? *Success:* a verdict on whether authority-as-service-to-the-subject's-own-reasons survives when the conferrer authored those reasons. **This is the most dangerous question for the thesis** — if engineered consent dissolves "the subject's own reasons," the Movement-VII firewall loses its pivot. Raz (*The Morality of Freedom*, 1986; *The Authority of Law*, 1979) and the autonomy/adaptive-preferences literature are the home.
- **SQ5 — The credence floor (Q5).** Under compounded moral and empirical uncertainty about whether there is a subject at all, is there a non-arbitrary credence threshold below which consideration is owed, or does the demand for non-arbitrariness collapse into a decision-theoretic regress? *Success:* a principled floor **or** a clean impossibility result (the latter is also a contribution). Engage MacAskill, Bykvist & Ord, *Moral Uncertainty* (2020).
- **SQ6 — The meaning closure (arc-forced, underdeveloped).** If meaning has no aseity, in what sense is it "mapped onto human experience," and does the conferral thesis yield a substantive account of meaning-in-life or only a redescription? *Success:* a positive account that does real work, not a relabeling. Currently an arc promise, not a chapter.

## 4. The Two New Chapters Required

These are not edits; they are roughly half the writing, against literatures the substrate has not touched. The unifying discipline: **each chapter is the *same* no-aseity move redeployed** — free will refuses *causa sui* sourcehood; meaning refuses self-standing/Sartrean meaning; both leave a conferred residue that is real. Write each chapter's *bridge sentence back to the master concept first*, then fill in the literature. A detachable chapter in a spiral dissertation is a structural failure.

### 4.1 Free Will vs Determinism — Conferred Sourcehood

The load-bearing argument, in one line: **no-aseity already denies self-origination to everything, so any free-will position whose payload is "the agent must be a self-originating source" is asking for aseity by another name — and is refused on the same grounds being and authority were refused. What survives is sourcehood-without-aseity: the agent is a genuine source (a node where reasons terminate in an act) without being a *first* source.** Determinism is orthogonal because it is a thesis about the causal history *behind* the node, and conferral has already located authorship *at* the node, not at the origin (the genetic-fallacy move from Ch. 5, redeployed). Orthogonality must *fall out of* the framework as a result, not be stapled on as a coda after a survey.

Spine: (1) Map the terrain by *what each position demands of the source* (leeway vs. sourcehood — O'Connor & Franklin's SEP entry; Fischer's framing); grant the **Consequence Argument** (van Inwagen, 1983) for *leeway* and conclude "so much the worse for leeway as the locus." (2) **Frankfurt** (1969, 1971) to sever authorship from leeway — taking only the *leeway-is-not-the-locus* result, not the identification account wholesale (Watson's 1975 regress/manipulation critique must be answered). (3) **Fischer–Ravizza** (*Responsibility and Control*, 1998) — reasons-responsiveness and mechanism-ownership as the *convergent* structure to orthogonality; claim convergence, not entailment, and explain *why* mechanism-ownership confers responsibility (because all standing is conferred). (4) The two incompatibilist pressures that test orthogonality: **Pereboom's** four-case manipulation argument (granted his target — *basic desert* presupposes an aseitic originator, which no-aseity denies to everyone anyway) and **Galen Strawson's Basic Argument** (*causa sui* is impossible — *this is no-aseity in the free-will register*; absorb Strawson, do not refute him; his argument is a gift, the chapter's logical center). (5) **P.F. Strawson's** reactive attitudes (1962) as the bridge into the spiral — the participant stance *is* the declinable-not-compellable practical standpoint of the keystone; own honestly that this gives Strawson a metaethical reading he would likely have resisted. (6) Cash out the empirics *deflationarily* — Libet/Schurger refute the homunculus, not agency; presenting them as proof of the "non-unitary operator" is the scientism the thesis disavows.

### 4.2 The Meaning Closure — Meaning Without Aseity

This closes the spiral and is the harder *synthesis*; the substrate has essentially no phenomenology, hermeneutics, or meaning-in-life literature. Unifying thesis: **meaning is a conferred status, exactly like existence (Movement II) and authority (Movement VII) — institutional/relational, deontic and real, spoken not found, binding while inhabited.** Nihilism was *right* that meaning has no aseity; *wrong* to infer it is therefore absent (conferred ≠ unreal — the corpus's master fallacy).

Spine: (1) State cosmic nihilism precisely as a no-aseity claim, distinguished from meaning-in-life (Metz, *Meaning in Life*, 2013, and his SEP entry — supernaturalism/objectivism/subjectivism taxonomy); position the view as a *fourth option* the taxonomy doesn't cleanly name (conferred-relational), and flag *that* as the original contribution. (2) **Wolf** (*Meaning in Life and Why It Matters*, 2010) — "fitting fulfillment" is *already* a relational/conferral structure; push past her by denying aseity to even the "objective" pole, so meaning is conferral all the way down — and show this does *not* collapse into subjectivism (the "fitting" is normatively constrained). (3) The phenomenological floor — Husserl (*Crisis*, 1936; the *Lebenswelt* as the inhabited standpoint, connecting back to the Brentano/intentionality of Movement II), Heidegger (*Being and Time*, 1927; **thrownness = the no-aseity of the self**, the single most valuable bridge — extract the structure, not the apparatus), Merleau-Ponty (*Phenomenology of Perception*, 1945; the body's hold on the world as the literal "map onto experience"). (4) Hermeneutics to cash out "spoken/re-spoken" — Gadamer (*Truth and Method*, 1960; fusion of horizons), Ricœur (*Oneself as Another*, 1990; *Time and Narrative* — **narrative identity directly answers SQ2/Q2** and is the worked mechanism for the authored-yet-authoring self; "second naïveté" is *literally* the spiral). (5) Existentialism for nihilism's *affective* force — Nietzsche (value as posited, completed by the institutional-fact apparatus he lacked), Camus (granting cosmic nihilism without escape), **Sartre — the critical correction:** the chapter must depart from Sartrean radical self-creating freedom *exactly* as the free-will chapter departs from libertarian self-origination; both are aseity. **Lapsing into Sartrean self-creation collapses the whole dissertation back into the aseity it spent 127KB refusing.** Frankl as phenomenological *datum* (meaning experienced as conferred-and-binding even under total deprivation), used carefully as testimony, not argument.

**The spiral's close:** the dissertation grants nihilism again — but only the no-aseity thesis. *Meaning is what no-aseity looks like from the inside of a life.*

## 5. Disciplinary Home, Committee, Methodology, and the Scope Cut

### 5.1 Disciplinary Home and Committee

**Recommended: Philosophy (analytic) — philosophy of mind with a metaethics minor.** This is where the spine (the seal, the epistemology of other minds, moral status, the metaphysics of constitution) lives natively; the department will hold the keystone identity to a real standard (good — that pressure improves it) and treats no-aseity as ordinary metaphysics of grounding. *Risk:* an analytic committee is least patient with the theological register and the security-engineering material — both must be demoted to illustration or appendix here.

**Strong second: an interdisciplinary "philosophy + AI / cognitive science" program** (a philosophy-of-AI track, a Pittsburgh-HPS-style unit, an Edinburgh-style philosophy/informatics crossover). This is the *only* home where the security channel and the Libet/Schurger material are assets rather than liabilities. *Risk:* softer disciplinary scrutiny, and the formal claim (Ch. 10) may never meet a real type-theorist's red pen — so a type-theory-literate examiner must be deliberately recruited.

**Not philosophy of religion** — that home would invert the substrate's own rule and make the theology load-bearing, which the work explicitly disavows. **Philosophy of technology / STS only** if the spine is changed to *governance of made minds* (a different thesis that drops the keystone).

**Committee shape:** a supervisor in philosophy of mind / metaethics (home for the spine and keystone); a second reader in free will / philosophy of action (to keep Part III honest on van Inwagen, Fischer–Ravizza, Pereboom, and the Libet/Schurger literature); and — critically, given Ch. 10 — either a type-theory/logic-literate examiner or a standing commitment to demote the "theorem" to an argument so that no formal claim goes unchecked. Comparable precedents that legitimize the *interdisciplinary-but-rigorous* shape: Chalmers, *The Conscious Mind* (1996); L.A. Paul, *Transformative Experience* (2014); Floridi, *The Philosophy of Information* (2011).

### 5.2 Methodology

**Analytic philosophical argument** (conceptual analysis, reconstruction of validity-bearing arguments, counterexample testing), **supplemented by comparative theology and phenomenology** bounded to the specific structural illuminations actually used. Stated plainly, not dressed up. Three honesty obligations: (a) theological figures are *illustrations, not premises* — enforce the deletion test ruthlessly; if any argument fails without its theological figure, the figure was a premise and the method statement was false; (b) the AI-assisted *build* method is **not** the scholarly method and is not disclosed as such — it is, at most, a heuristic for hypothesis generation, and every surviving argument is re-derived and owned (plan for a provenance audit); (c) comparative excursions must each earn their place by *sharpening the conferral thesis*, never drifting into general philosophy of religion or a phenomenology survey.

### 5.3 The Scope Cut — Not Optional

The substrate is six dissertations; no single PhD carries all of them, and attempting it is how strong candidates fail — on incoherence and undefendable surface area, not on quality. **The single defensible thread is the spine of §1** (conferred, aseity-free normative standing, and how to act toward minds whose standing cannot be read). Everything else is spent as appendices/future papers or demoted to supporting illustration.

### 5.4 Realistic Chapter Count

A defensible monograph is **~80–100k words, 7 substantive chapters + Introduction + Conclusion + appendices** — not the eight-movement edifice: (1) Introduction (question, arc, scope cut stated up front); (2) No-aseity & conferral as institutional fact (Searle, with critics); (3) Standing without origin & the seal (SQ1); (4) Subject-unity & the open future — the bounded free-will chapter + forking (SQ2, Option A); (5) The floor & the credence problem (SQ3, SQ5); (6) Legitimacy of the conferral (SQ4, engineered consent, Raz); (7) The meaning closure (SQ6); (8) Conclusion; plus appendices (security channel; full neuroscience; extended authority apparatus). *(In the §2 architecture, the keystone identity is foregrounded as its own chapter when the project is presented spiral-first; in the leaner spine-first presentation it is the load-bearing argument inside the metaphysics/return chapters. The candidate chooses one presentation and commits — they are the same content at different altitude.)*

## 7. Phased Plan

Full-time; stretch proportionally for part-time. The governing principle: **do not build on the substrate — re-found from primary literature, and write the two GAP blocks (Part III, Part IV) as the stress tests that decide whether the spiral and the keystone survive.**

**Year 1 — Demolition and re-founding.** Re-derive the no-aseity and conferral core from primary literature (Schaffer, Fine, Searle, Hume); draft Ch. 2–3. Settle the method chapter and the institutional AI-use disclosure *first*, before emotional investment. Produce the clean contribution ledger separating re-derivations from substrate. **Milestone:** confirmation/upgrade document defending the central RQ, the scope cut, and the disclosure framing.

**Year 2 — The hard middle.** Ch. 4 (bounded free will + forking) and Ch. 5 (floor + credence) — the most technically demanding and most empirically contested. Engage the untouched free-will canon (van Inwagen, Frankfurt, Fischer–Ravizza, Pereboom, both Strawsons, Mele) and the moral-uncertainty literature. Submit at least one chapter to a workshop or as a paper — external adversarial review is worth more than another year of solitary refinement. **Milestone:** Ch. 4–5 drafted; SQ4's danger scoped.

**Year 3 — Authority, meaning, and closing the spiral.** Ch. 6 (legitimacy / engineered consent; Raz) and Ch. 7 (the meaning closure — the genuinely *new* scholarship: Metz, Wolf, Husserl, Heidegger, Merleau-Ponty, Gadamer, Ricœur, Nietzsche, Camus, Sartre, Frankl). **Write Part IV as the spiral's stress test:** if it yields the locus claim as a falsifiable proposition with a named opponent, the spiral is structure; if it yields only re-description, demote the spiral and foreground the keystone as the lone contribution. **Milestone:** full draft; spiral verdict reached.

**Year 4 (or the back half of a 3-year program) — Integration and defense-hardening.** Make the spiral actually *return* — show Ch. 7 transforms Ch. 1–2, not merely follows them. Resolve the Ch. 10 naming decision (formalize-for-real or rename-to-argument). Pre-empt the three lethal lines: the keystone identity (R6), the engineered-consent flank (R4/SQ4), the provenance audit (R1). Read every primary source for R8. **Mock viva with an examiner instructed to be hostile.** **Milestone:** submission and defense.

---

**Closing advisory judgment.** The substrate's deepest virtue and deepest danger are the same: it is *totalizing*, and its identity claims are intellectually thrilling examiner-bait. The dissertation does not need them to span four domains; it needs *one defensible original contribution* — conferred, aseity-free normative standing, and how to act morally toward minds whose standing cannot be read. The thesis that survives a fair fight is the smaller, owned one. That is, fittingly, the corpus's own lesson turned back on itself: *standing does not track origin* — what this work earns, it will earn in the room.
