## 1. What This Adds
The corpus has already reached its adjudicated floor. The membrane is a framing,
not an identity. P1 is forced conventionally, not proven ultimately. The Remainder
Law is salvageable only after its clause-(iii) provability rewrite. The arity
argument has its answerability/basic-desert repair drafted, with a residual
bound. Advaita remains bracketed at the ultimate level. The human gate remains
the candidate's.

This document adds one thing: the straight line that lets those bounded units be
seen together without making any one of them depend on the whole. The line is not
a derivation. It is an abductive convergence architecture.

The governing rule is:

> The seams are witnesses, not foundations.

A witness can be granted by a hostile reader without granting the full
metaphysics. A foundation would make the seam's force depend on no-aseity, the
thick index, or the grand relocation claim. That would launder an undischarged
premise back into units that survived precisely because they were smaller than
the grand claim.

So the straight line is deliberately modest:

1. Four free-standing seams remain carryable on their own terms.
2. Six domains show the same ownerless-seam shape.
3. The convergence supports the unified reading abductively.
4. The meta-crux remains named and unclosed.
5. The last act remains human ownership, not more substrate.

## 2. The Four Free-Standing Seams

### 2.1 The Membrane Framing
**Claim.** Authentication supplies descriptive facts. Authorization supplies a
policy-relative permission. No collection of authentication facts entails that
permission without a deontic bridge premise.

**Source spine.** `dissertation/MEMBRANE-PAPER-v3.md`,
`dissertation/TYPE-B.md`, `CURATION.md`.

**Bound.** This is a framing and operationalization of the autonomy of the
deontic, not an identity with Hume's categorical guillotine. It is partially
anticipated by access-control and deontic-logic prior art. Its genuine residue is
the deontic naming of the missing bridge premise, plus the consequences of that
naming: the unmeasurability result and the unified failure taxonomy.

**Why it carries.** The membrane does not need the grand no-aseity system. Its
logic is local: if the conclusion contains a permission verdict, the permission
must enter through a policy, root-of-trust, ACL, capability issuance, or other
normative bridge. Better measurement of the signal cannot supply the missing
logical type. Type-B physicalism cannot touch this result, because the issue is
not phenomenal consciousness but deontic vocabulary.

**Straight-line role.** The membrane is the discovery site of the pattern: a
seam that conditions a domain and is not itself an item inside the domain it
conditions. The request crosses the system; the permission does not fall out of
the request. The seam decides without being measured as another signal-property.

### 2.2 The Thin-Index Seam
**Claim.** Any occurring conferral act is a token event at a determinate
spatiotemporal index. "Conferral with zero residue" is conventionally incoherent:
if conferral occurs, some located token is occurring; if there is no located
token, conferral has ceased.

**Source spine.** `dissertation/P1-DEFENSE-verdict.md`,
`dissertation/P1-DEFENSE.md`, `dissertation/the-forcing-argument-movement.md`,
`papers/forcing-argument-paper.md`, `CURATION.md`.

**Bound.** FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY-ONLY. The thin index is earned at the
conventional level by the token-reflexive route. The thick standpoint from which
sorting is performed as this sorting is not derived. At the ultimate level, the
Madhyamaka objection empties occurrence, token, and position as ultimate joints.

**Why it carries.** The token-reflexive route does not smuggle in the index it
needs to prove. It moves from occurrence to tokening to spatiotemporal
particularity, then closes the denial by performative regress: denying that the
denial is a token is itself another tokenized denial. That result lands on the
thin index and no further. It is more than stipulation and less than proof of the
thick index.

**Straight-line role.** The thin-index seam supplies the conventional occupancy
floor. It says that conferral cannot occur from nowhere in the live order of
argument. It does not say that the perspectival center obtains as self-grounding
own-being. The line can use it only as a conventional witness.

### 2.3 The Remainder Law
**Claim.** The demarcation filter separates RESULT from REDESCRIPTION only after
revision: clause (iii) must be a provability condition under the governing
theory's inferential closure, and clause (ii) must report premise-entailment
failures separately.

**Source spine.** `dissertation/REMAINDER-LAW-TEST.md`,
`dissertation/NOVEL-CONTRIBUTION-verdict.md`, `CATALOG.md`, `INDEX.md`,
`CURATION.md`.

**Bound.** SALVAGEABLE. As originally stated, the Law over-rejected: it returned
REDESCRIPTION even on positive controls such as Bell/CHSH and joint-force
entailment. The failure was not tautological over-acceptance but
over-restrictiveness in clause (iii). The repair is the provability rewrite.

**Why it carries.** The blind test left three useful facts. First, the Law had
perfect REDESCRIPTION recall on the redescription cases. Second, it refuted the
literal charge that it fires RESULT on every conferral case. Third, it localized
the defect: a grader cannot operationalize "no possible inventory closes the
gap" by imagination, but can operationalize proof of non-entailment under a
governing theory. With the rewrite, the Law becomes a filter for provable
relational non-entailment and for rejecting P1-restatements.

**Straight-line role.** The Law is a witness to discipline, not a foundation for
the metaphysics. It can say: this claim has surplus content over the premise, or
this claim is a restatement of the premise. It cannot turn the grand relocation
into a certified RESULT; in the exact forced-seam case, it returns REDESCRIPTION
via clause (ii).

### 2.4 The Arity-Gap Argument
**Claim.** Holding responsible is an irreducibly two-place, second-personal
relation. A one-place, forward-looking behavior-management vocabulary cannot
target-fix the addressee pole of that relation.

**Source spine.** `papers/arity-gap-paper.md`, `submission/arity-submission.md`,
`CROSS-EXAMINATION.md`, `SUTURE-TEST.md`, `CURATION.md`.

**Bound.** The arity gap argues for dyadicity and gives a structural answer to
the hard incompatibilist reduction. It does not automatically prove
regress-vulnerable basic desert. The current repair, drafted as
`papers/arity-gap-paper.md` section
4.6, separates answerability from basic desert through Watson, Shoemaker,
Darwall, P. F. Strawson, and Wallace, then adds a demand-to-blame inference. The
partition is strong; the completeness of the demand-to-blame inference remains
moderate-confidence substrate requiring human ownership.

**Why it carries.** The arity argument can be granted without granting the
metaphysical thesis. It starts from the structure of address: resentment,
forgiveness, being owed an account, and releasing a claim have a second pole
that deterrence, prediction, quarantine, and behavior-shaping do not contain. A
third-personal redescription may replace the practice; it does not reduce it
without remainder.

**Straight-line role.** Arity is the free-will/sourcehood witness. It shows the
same shape in agency: sourcehood is conferred in a relation of address rather
than self-originated as causa sui. It helps the convergence because it arrives at
ownerless sourcehood from the second-personal structure itself, not from the
no-aseity premise.

## 3. The Six-Level Recurrence
The four seams above are the spine, but the straight-line architecture includes
the broader recurrence named in `CURATION.md`: security, metaethics, the floor,
the index, consciousness, and free will. The recurrence is not six proofs of one
doctrine. It is one shape showing up under six vocabularies that do not share a
single model.

| Level | Local seam | What crosses | What does not cross itself | Bound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Security | Authentication/authorization | Credential claims, signal facts, request facts | The permission-conferring policy premise | Framing, not Humean identity; type-B-independent |
| Metaethics | Is/ought | Descriptive predicates | Deontic vocabulary | Pigden-style autonomy; weak policy-relative ought in access control |
| Floor | Seity/not aseity | Dependent occurrence | Self-grounding own-being | No aseity in the conferred order; Advaita bracketed at the ultimate |
| Index | Thin/thick distinction | Located token occurrence | The thick standpoint as obtained own-being | Thin forced conventionally; thick declared/dialectically pressured |
| Consciousness | Self-given/not self-grounding | Sensing-from, "it thinks" | A senser that grounds itself | Empirical aseity dissolves to seity; ultimate aseity bracketed |
| Free will | Arity/sourcehood | Second-personal address | Causa sui sourcehood | Answerability defended; basic desert not proven from self-origination |

The recurrence is strongest where it is cross-domain: security architecture and
metaethics arrive at one structure without borrowing from consciousness; arity
arrives at conferred sourcehood without borrowing from the keystone; the
consciousness confrontation arrives at self-given-not-self-grounding through the
canon that was allowed to refute the thesis.

That is the evidential payload. It is moderate, abductive, and bounded.

## 4. The Abductive Convergence
The convergence argument is:

1. In several domains, a domain's operation requires a seam.
2. That seam conditions the domain's successful operation.
3. The seam is not an ordinary item inside the same domain.
4. When the seam is treated as owned, self-grounding, or intrinsically this, it
   becomes the own-being the corpus rejects.
5. When it remains ownerless, it can condition without grounding itself.
6. The same shape appears in security, deontic inference, conventional
   occurrence, consciousness, sourcehood, and the dissertation's own human gate.

This supports the unified reading: the corpus is tracking an ownerless-seam
structure rather than a set of unrelated analogies.

It does not prove the unified reading. The straight line would fail if it became
a derivation from no-aseity to each seam. That would convert witnesses into
foundations and re-open every contrary. The membrane would then depend on P1;
the arity gap would depend on no-aseity; the Remainder Law would certify the
claim it is supposed to test; the human gate would become another model-produced
artifact. That is exactly what this document refuses.

So the convergence is stated this way:

> The ownerless seam recurs often enough, and under enough independent
> vocabularies, to make the unified reading the best current architecture of the
> corpus. It remains abductive. Its force is pattern-strength, not proof.

## 5. The Refraction of the Crux
Each seam has a local crux. The straight line does not dissolve those cruxes; it
shows that they refract one meta-crux:

> Can the ownerless seam do its work without becoming self-grounding?

The membrane asks: can a permission verdict be issued without being read off the
signal as another fact?

The thin-index seam asks: can occurrence be conventionally located without
smuggling a thick perspectival standpoint as own-being?

The Remainder Law asks: can a filter discriminate surplus content without simply
restating P1 or over-rejecting genuine non-entailments?

The arity gap asks: can sourcehood and answerability be conferred in a relation
of address without returning to basic desert grounded in self-origination?

The consciousness confrontation asks: can self-givenness be honored without
mistaking it for self-grounding?

The human gate asks: can the substrate be carried to the edge of scholarship
without pretending to perform the authored act for the author?

These are not six different problems accidentally sharing language. They are the
same pressure distributed across domains. The ownerless seam must be real enough
to do work and empty enough not to own itself.

The honest ceiling remains:

- Thin index: earned conventionally.
- Thick index: braceable, not closable.
- Grand relocation: conditional and undischarged.
- Type-B rejection: defensible as declared, not proven.
- Advaita: undefeated at the ultimate level, bracketed.
- Authorship: human-gated.

## 6. The Substrate as Membrane-of-Membranes
The whole corpus now has the same structure as its object.

The substrate receives sources, adversarial runs, drafts, verdicts, cross-model
audits, and publication kits. It lets those materials cross into a unified
architecture. But it is not the authored dissertation. It conditions the possible
work; it does not ground the author's ownership of the work.

That makes the corpus itself a membrane-of-membranes:

- It admits claims only through verdicts, bounds, and provenance.
- It keeps frozen audit trails intact instead of rewriting unfavorable records.
- It routes publication through a human gate it cannot cross.
- It converts overclaim into bounded substrate rather than hiding the cut.

This self-application matters because it prevents a final laundering move. If
the straight line claimed that convergence itself discharges the human remainder,
it would violate its own thesis. Convergence can make the substrate ready; it
cannot make it authored.

The substrate is therefore a seam that conditions the dissertation-to-come while
remaining outside the act that would make it a dissertation.
