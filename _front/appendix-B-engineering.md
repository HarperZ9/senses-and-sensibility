*Every entry names a real work; page numbers are asserted only where verified;
`[high]` / `[moderate]` / `[low]` mark attribution confidence.*

- Brentano, Franz. *Psychology from an Empirical Standpoint* (1874). [high] — the
  intentionality (aboutness) thesis: the mental is directed at an object; aboutness
  is not a physical property of a signal.
- Hardy, Norm. "The Confused Deputy." *ACM SIGOPS Operating Systems Review* (1988). [high]
- Miller, Mark S. *Robust Composition* (PhD thesis, 2006). [high] — object-capability model; POLA.
- Goldwasser, S., Micali, S., Rackoff, C. "The Knowledge Complexity of Interactive
  Proof Systems." *SIAM J. Computing* (1989; STOC 1985). [high] — zero-knowledge.
- Merkle, Ralph C. Hash trees / "A Certified Digital Signature" (1979/1987). [high]
  — content-addressed integrity.
- Dodis, Y., Reyzin, L., Smith, A. "Fuzzy Extractors…" *EUROCRYPT* 2004 / *SIAM J.
  Computing* 2008. [high]
- Ratha, N., Connell, J., Bolle, R. "Enhancing security and privacy in
  biometrics-based authentication systems." *IBM Systems Journal* (2001). [high] —
  cancelable biometrics.
- Kocher, P. "Timing Attacks…" (1996); Kocher, Jaffe, Jun, "Differential Power
  Analysis" (1999). [high]
- SGX/enclave side channels: Foreshadow (2018); Plundervolt (2019). [moderate] —
  representative; verify specifics.
- Corticomuscular coherence: Conway, B. A. et al., *J. Physiology* (1995); Baker,
  S. N. et al. (late 1990s). [high] for the phenomenon; [moderate] as a biometric.
- Error-related potentials: Falkenstein et al. (1991); Gehring, W. J. et al.,
  *Psychological Science* (1993). [high] for the phenomenon; [moderate] for
  single-trial veto reliability.
- Libet, B. et al., *Brain* (1983) — readiness potential precedes reported
  conscious intention. [high] for the finding; interpretation contested. Schurger,
  A. et al. (2012) — stochastic-accumulator reinterpretation; *argued*, not settled.
- Two-person rule / dual control; m-of-n threshold authorization. [high] as
  established practice. Computational irreducibility (informally, Wolfram):
  illustrative, not load-bearing. [moderate]
