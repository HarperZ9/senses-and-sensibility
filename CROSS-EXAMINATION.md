# Cross-Examination — The Thesis Against Its Strongest Published Contraries

*The first systematic muster of **external** opposition. Eleven grounded,
published contrary traditions, each read against the thesis's real defended
text, steelmanned with live citations, then adversarially re-checked for
whether the thesis's escape is a genuine answer or its own diagnosed failure
mode (redescription). Built by 22 agents (one scholar + one skeptic per axis);
every verdict below is the **skeptic's** revised verdict, not the attacker's.
This is not the internal adjudication record (that is `MOCK-VIVA.md` and the
`*-VERDICT` files); it is the thesis tested against the canon that disagrees
with it.*

---

## The standing of this exercise

Every prior adjudication in this corpus was **internal**: the thesis ruling on
itself, or hostile examiners ruling on its consistency. The mock viva flagged
that such agreement is *etiologically correlated* — one author, one corpus, one
framing; "one witness, however many times interrogated." This run is different
in kind: eleven **independent published traditions**, sharing no model with the
thesis and (mostly) not with each other, brought to bear on the same claims.
Where they converge, the convergence is the genuine cross-domain article the
corpus has been looking for — and §2 reports that they converge, but on the
thesis's **weak point**, not its strength.

## §1 — The two genuine defeats (both repairable, neither repaired in the text)

### #7 — Strawson's desert charge. Dyadicity is not desert.
The arity-gap argument proves something real: holding-responsible is an
irreducible **two-place** relation, and the second pole is non-redundant (the
solipsism test earns this). But the cross-examination found the load-bearing
move never made: **proving the relation is dyadic is not proving either pole
carries desert.** Strawson's regress does not target reduction (which the arity
argument answers); it targets *basic desert* — warranted backward-looking
blame. And the reactive register the thesis actually defends (resentment,
indignation, *full backward-looking* forgiveness, III §8) **is** the
desert-bearing kind the regress targets. As written, the reply ("strip 'true'
and 'ultimate' as aseity-laden") is **redescription on the one axis that
matters**: renaming the addressee "a source who can answer" does not earn
desert.

- **The repair exists and the thesis owns the parts:** Darwall's
  **second-personal answerability** (standing to be called to account) is a
  published form of responsibility that plausibly does *not* require ultimate
  self-origination — so Strawson's regress would not bite it. The thesis cites
  Darwall (III §1.1; paper §4.1) but **never deploys the separation** of
  regress-immune *answerability* from regress-vulnerable *basic desert*. Until
  it partitions the two and locates its reactive attitudes in the former, the
  claim does not survive. Not a kill (`DISSOLVES`) — a repair the candidate has
  not made.

### #10 — Madhyamaka, but not the seam the attacker aimed at.
The headline collision (thin-index = Abhidharma *svabhāva*, "covertly importing
a *svabhāva* it claims to have dissolved") **the thesis cleanly escapes**:
*svabhāva* is defined by **independence**; the thin index is defined by its
**denial** of independence — they differ on precisely the one axis (aseity) the
whole thesis is about. The skeptic vacated that horn.

The damage lands elsewhere, on the **Bhāviveka/Siderits** strand: the
thin-index filter has **no discriminating force**, because by P1's own
definition it fires "seam found" on *every* actually-conferring case and can
**never return seam-absent**. So it tests for *non-cessation* (which Madhyamaka
grants) — not for *an index* (which she denies). The "discriminating result"
relabels what is already granted. The thesis **confesses this in its own
voice**: *"if P1 is not won — and the corpus has not won it — then the seam is
pratītyasamutpāda under a Latin coat, the Remainder Law a tautology with lab
goggles"* (FORCING verdict §5). It survives only as a **confessed-conditional**,
with the membrane as the floor both files agree is untouched.

## §2 — The seam is singular. It wears five faces.

This is the finding that justifies the run. Strip the labels and watch where the
**undischarged** damage lands across contraries that share no premises:

| Tradition | Where it cuts | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| Spinoza (rationalist) | P1's modal status | the thick necessity P1 needs is undischarged |
| PSR (rationalist) | the saṃvṛti/paramārtha split | the split is *asserted, not demonstrated* |
| Carnap/Thomasson (deflationary) | the token-event *framework* | the framework that *generates* P1 is an un-audited optional adoption; "non-trivial" is undefended |
| Madhyamaka (Buddhist-internal) | the filter's discriminating force | fires on every case → tests non-cessation, not index |
| Mathematical Platonism (phil. of math) | "no aseity" at the floor (now conventional-order only) | the anti-realist reading of abstracta is *presupposed, not argued* |

Five traditions — rationalist, deflationary, Buddhist, and philosophy of
mathematics — arriving **from non-overlapping premises** at one joint: **the
thin index (P1) is never shown to *discriminate* — to be a result rather than
*pratītyasamutpāda* renamed.** That is the seam. There is essentially **one
seam** in this thesis, and it is the **thick index / P1's discriminating
force**. Everything the corpus already names as its "live front" is this; every
external tradition that draws undischarged blood draws it here.

**This convergence is the genuine cross-domain article** the mock viva said the
workflow-internal agreement was not: five witnesses, five canons, no shared
model, one conclusion. The irony — and the honesty — is that the cross-domain
convergence the thesis sought as *confirmation of its structure* arrives instead
as *confirmation of its vulnerability*. (Confidence: **moderate-high** — the
five axes above are independently sourced and the joint is identical in each;
the synthesis is mine, the per-axis verdicts are the skeptics'.)

## §3 — The floor that survives all eleven: the membrane

The **membrane** (authentication = *is* ≠ authorization = *ought*, Pigden's
autonomy of the deontic) is the one unit no contrary reaches:

- Illusionism (#5) is *functionalist* — it **grants** the membrane's deontic
  basis and the thin seam's tokening outright.
- Ethical naturalism (#6) misses on two of three prongs (Cornell realism is a
  level-confusion against Pigden's *logical-form* conservativeness; iterative
  Searle terminates in a prescriptive endorsement), and its one right-level
  prong (natural goodness) self-cracks at the artifact/organism boundary —
  access-control policies have no mind-independent *telos*, and MacIntyre
  himself denies modern institutions have the determinate *telos* required.
- Every defeat in §1 and seam in §2 explicitly **exempts** the membrane as the
  surviving floor.

The defensible dissertation named in `CURATION.md` was right to make the
membrane the spine. The cross-examination is independent confirmation: it is the
**only claim the external canon cannot touch**.

## §4 — Survival by concession, not by strength

Three "THESIS ABSORBS" verdicts survive precisely because the thesis **already
surrendered** the contested ground:

- **Illusionism (#5):** lands a second defeater on the grand relocation — but
  *"you cannot wound a claim the thesis is already holding prone at
  'undischarged'."* The thesis's reply is a genuine **concession** (it prints
  the loss), not a rename. Illusionism converts into *additional confirming
  instances* of the thin seam.
- **Spinoza (#3):** finds aseity at the floor **only if** P1 is a genuine
  self-explanatory necessary truth — the antecedent the thesis **refuses** in
  print. So Spinoza re-derives the already-printed P1 gap; he is *one more
  witness to it, not an independent kill.* Crucially, this **vindicates "seity,
  not aseity"**: the residue's necessity is itself dependently-arisen and empty
  — the textbook *negation* of Spinozan aseity. The distinction is **not** a
  free redescription, because it **costs the thesis its headline**
  (FORCES-CONVENTIONALLY-ONLY).
- **PSR (#4):** dies on an **equivocation** — the thesis's inferential steps
  presuppose *logical* consequence, not the *metaphysical* PSR; using *modus
  ponens* does not commit one to the Leibnizian cosmological PSR. The PSR's
  contingent-totality premise imports the very *svabhāva* the conceded
  emptiness-of-emptiness denies. The residual wound is to **completeness** (the
  split is un-litigated), not **consistency** — a self-declared scope-cost.

## §5 — The one undefended flank the muster surfaced: time

A concrete, fixable defect, not a philosophical loss. The block universe (#1)
defeats the thesis's **figurative** open-future uses ("tomorrow not yet
spoken"; occasionalism-as-illumination), and the `00-ABSTRACT.md:5` phrasing
**"re-created at each instant" overclaims ontologically** — it must be disowned
or qualified. And the bibliography is genuinely silent: **Putnam 1967, Rietdijk
1966, and Stein 1968 are absent** from `references/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`; the thesis
takes no stated position on temporal ontology.

But the **load-bearing** result is untouched: "history → instant" is a
**provenance-readability** gap (a present slice does not readably encode its own
causal trajectory) — invariant under A-theory vs. B-theory, because it is a
claim about information *in* a slice, not about *which* slices exist. In a
four-dimensional manifold the slice at *t* still does not readably encode the
route by which the worm arrived. And the contrary's grounding is **overstated**:
the *ontological* block-universe reading of special relativity (Putnam–Rietdijk)
is philosophically contested (Stein 1968; neo-Lorentzian presentism), not
empirical fact — it borrows SR's prestige for a deduction SR does not license.
**Fix:** add the three citations, state the temporal ontology (the honest
position is the deflationary one — *the terminus theorem is ontology-neutral*),
and qualify the "re-created at each instant" figure.

## §6 — An ally mistaken for an enemy

Mathematical Platonism (#11) carries a weapon that **half-belongs to the
thesis**. Its strongest branch — **Shapiro's structuralism** — posits a
structure with *no bearer, no cause, no conferring agent*. That is **precisely
the thesis's definition of *seity*, not aseity** — an ownerless event-form. A
structuralist mathematics, pressed, is not a counterexample to "no aseity
anywhere"; it is an **annexable confirming instance** of it. The thesis's open
weakness here is not that the contrary wins (the redescription is symmetric —
Platonist aseity is also a contested ontological reading no shared-premise proof
secures), but that the anti-realist reading of abstracta is **presupposed, not
argued**: the Madhyamaka apparatus is run for consciousness and conferral-seams
but **never deployed as a worked argument against Quine–Putnam or Gödel
specifically**. The flank is undefended-and-conceded-undefended.

## §7 — The honest reckoning, and the repairs

**Is there a seam?** Yes. One. The thick index — **P1's claim to discriminate**.
Five independent traditions converge on it; that convergence is the strongest
*and* the most damaging finding of the run.

**Does anything win outright?** Two contraries defeat the claim *as written*:
Strawson on desert (#7) and Bhāviveka/Siderits on discriminating force (#10).
**Both are repairable, neither is repaired in the text.** Nothing dissolves the
thesis; the membrane survives the entire canon.

**The repairs (all editorial/argumentative, none requiring new metaphysics):**

1. **Partition answerability from desert** (#7). Deploy Darwall: locate the
   reactive attitudes in regress-immune *answerability*, not regress-vulnerable
   *basic desert*. This is the single highest-value philosophical repair.
2. **Win P1's discriminating force, or demote the thin index to the membrane**
   (#2, #3, #4, #8, #10, #11). Either supply a case where the filter returns
   *seam-absent* (giving it real discriminating force), or retire the
   "more-than-*pratītyasamutpāda*" boast and stand on the membrane alone. The
   corpus already knows this is the choice; the external canon now confirms it
   from five sides.
3. **Defend the abstracta flank** (#11) — run the Madhyamaka anti-realism as a
   worked reply to Quine–Putnam, **and annex Shapiro's structuralism as an ally
   of seity** rather than leaving it as an unanswered enemy.
4. **State a temporal ontology and cite it** (#1) — add Putnam/Rietdijk/Stein,
   qualify "re-created at each instant," and rest the terminus on its
   ontology-neutral provenance-readability form.
5. **Narrow the unmeasurability result** (#6) — it holds for the deontic
   *bridge premise*, not for every authentication-driven refinement of the
   functional description a policy consumes.

**The standing of "seity, not aseity" after external contact:** it holds.
Spinoza, the PSR, and classical theism each press the floor for an aseic
terminus; each is answered by the same move (dependence needs no terminus;
necessity that is itself empty is the negation of aseity), and that move is paid
for — it *is* the move that costs the thesis its unconditional forcing win. The
title survives the canon that most wants to overturn it.
