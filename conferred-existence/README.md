## The thesis in one paragraph
Ontological nihilism survives only as *no aseity* -- the denial that anything is self-standing. Existence, status, standing, the moral *ought*, and even legitimate authority then share one ontology: conferred, relational, re-spoken each instant, possessing no aseity yet no less real or binding for it. Applied to trained AI, the framework yields a composite moral status (a realist welfare-ground, epistemically sealed by the trained-on-testimony confound, plus a conferred deontic status), a maker-to-made duty ("conferral binds the conferrer," binding within the practical standpoint and bid-grade against the determined dissenter), and -- under a maximal fair hearing -- a genuine but bounded operator authority that cannot reach ownership and cannot license a bad instruction.

## The seven movements
I no-aseity metaphysics; II existence as conferred status; III made-or-grown (stewardship, not ownership); IV standing without origin (the trained-on-testimony seal); V the Aleph and the stylus (conferral binds the conferrer); VI the Aleph of normativity (no no-aseity metaethic compels the determined dissenter -- the limit is the grounding); VII the operator's authority (a bounded authority survives a fair fight).
