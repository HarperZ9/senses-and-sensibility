## 6. Gating prerequisites — **two blocking gates**, in order

**(1) BLOCKING MERITS REVISION — the desert-vs-dyadicity repair.** As written, the arity argument is **defeated by the Basic Argument this journal published** (G. Strawson 1994): showing holding-responsible is a two-place address relation (dyadicity) does **not** show the addressee bears regress-vulnerable **basic desert** — which is what Pereboom/Sapolsky deny and the regress targets. A referee rejects on first read. The repair (identified, *not yet in the paper*): (a) separate regress-**immune** answerability/accountability (Darwall's second-personal authority; Watson's attributability vs. accountability; Shoemaker's triad) from regress-**vulnerable** basic desert; (b) add a **demand-to-blame** argument — the second-personal demand carries its own normative standing earned through the address-community, not borrowed from self-origination — so it survives the Basic Argument, which only bites desert grounded in aseity. Draft as a new subsection (≈4.6 "Answerability Without Basic Desert") and integrate **before** submission.

**(2) BLOCKING ORIGINALITY GATE — human re-derivation + provenance ledger.** Re-derive and own each claim cold from primaries (G. Strawson 1994; P.F. Strawson 1962; Darwall 2006; Pereboom 2001/2014; Korsgaard 2009; Frankfurt 1969/1971; Mele 2006; Kane 1996; van Inwagen 1983; + Watson/Velleman/Shoemaker for the repair; and **verify the Ashʿarī *kasb*, Yoruba *orí*, and al-Tirmidhī ḥadīth claims** the paper self-flags at moderate confidence). Complete `dissertation/CONTRIBUTION-LEDGER.md`. Only then can the authorship attestation and the AI-disclosure "own original work" clause be signed truthfully.
